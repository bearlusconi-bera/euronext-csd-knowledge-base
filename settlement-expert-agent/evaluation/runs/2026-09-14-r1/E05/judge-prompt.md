## 5. Exceptions and controls

**5.1 Rejection points.** X-TRM validation failure → rejection message to the entering party; T2S validation failure → `sese.024.001.12` "Rejected" and an X-TRM rejection message to the entering party (`[[milan-xtrm-lifecycle]]` §§3.4.2, 3.4.5, PDF 41, 44; `[[t2s-messages]]` §2.3.8.2, PDF 778). **Reason codes are not admitted in this bundle**, so no rejection can be mapped to a code here.

**5.2 Realignment failure — the cross-CSD-specific risk.** "If T2S cannot create the necessary realignment due to erroneous links or accounts configuration or to unsuccessful validation on potential T2S generated realignment Settlement Instructions… **the inbound Settlement Instruction is cancelled**" (`[[t2s-messages]]` §2.3, PDF 753). This is the single most important failure mode of this route and it is a static-data failure, not an instruction-content failure. *Pre-validating the MT23/link position before instructing is a **Proposed design choice**; the evidence states the consequence, not the control.*

**5.3 Unmatched instructions, recycling and automatic cancellation.** "T2S automatically cancels Settlement Instructions that remain unmatched after a certain period of time" (`[[t2s-matching]]` §1.6.1.2.3, PDF 271); system cancellation is triggered "when the pending Unmatched Settlement Instruction exceeds the recycling period" and on unsuccessful revalidation after a reference-data update or at start-of-day revalidation (`[[t2s-allegement]]` §1.6.1.3.3, PDF 275–276). **The recycling-period values are not in this bundle.** Milan's own rule: automatic cancellation is imposed where instructions "have not passed the daily validation phase" or "are not matched or are not settled within the time limits provided in the Instructions", and "Participants are informed of the progress and outcome of the cancellation process and of any automatic cancellation" (`[[milan-finality]]` Article 70(7)–(8), PDF 50).

**5.4 Cancellation boundaries (Milan).** Unilateral cancellation by the entering participant is possible **up to the time of matching**, provided the instruction was not entered as non-changeable; **matched** instructions may be cancelled **bilaterally** with both participants' consent or by an entity acting on their behalf under a mandate previously filed with Monte Titoli; cancellations go through acquisition and, for matched instructions, matching, and "when the cancellations are matched, the original settlement Instructions are cancelled"; market operators and CCPs may ask Monte Titoli to block these functions for their instructions; Monte Titoli may enter cancellations at participants' request; **CoSD Settlement Instructions may only be cancelled by Monte Titoli** (`[[milan-finality]]` Article 70(1)–(6), PDF 50). On the T2S side, a cancellation needing both legs while the counterparty has not instructed produces a Status Advice with **no delay period** to each side (`[[t2s-allegement]]` §1.6.1.3.3, PDF 276).

**5.5 Amendment.** Unmatched instructions "may be changed by the Participants, but only as regards status indicators" (`[[milan-finality]]` Article 69(4), PDF 50). X-TRM narrows this further: participants "can edit only… partialisation indicator; priorities of Regulation; blocks for connections between Settlement Instructions (linkages blocks)"; one modification instruction per settlement instruction; a partially settled instruction can be changed only for the priority indicator; anything else requires delete-and-re-enter; modification instructions are themselves validated and valorised and return an acceptance or error message (`[[milan-xtrm-lifecycle]]` §3.4.7 A, PDF 45–46). *Source anomaly:* the acceptance condition is printed as a double negative ("accepted and processed unless: settlement instruction that must be modified **is not** already settled or canceled… **is not** identified as 'CoSD'"), which read literally inverts the evident rule. **Reasoned inference:** the intended rule is that modification is refused once the instruction is settled or cancelled and for CoSD instructions — consistent with §3.4.7 C ("hold & release functionality is not avaible for settlement instructions entered in CoSD modality") and with T2S denying a Hold Instruction whose reference is cancelled, settled, CoSD or already held by that actor (`[[t2s-hold-release]]` §1.6.1.6.3, PDF 289). The Italian text prevails; verify before coding.

**5.6 Hold and release.** Milan: the participant may hold settlement, or hold the re-proposal of instructions not settled (also partially), until a specific release, provided the instructions were not entered as non-changeable; market operators and CCPs may have the function blocked; Monte Titoli may also hold at participants' request (`[[milan-finality]]` Article 71, PDF 51). T2S: two actor-set indicators (Party Hold, CSD Hold) and three set automatically by T2S (CSD Validation Hold, Party Hold via a restriction or the account's Hold Release Default, CoSD Hold); holds are independent; **T2S does not allow actors to hold an instruction already identified as CoSD**, and an instruction held before CoSD identification goes on CoSD Hold with the blocking deferred until released; instructions on hold can still be matched, amended or cancelled; successful execution returns an execution message plus a Status Advice (`[[t2s-hold-release]]` §1.6.1.6.1–1.6.1.6.3, PDF 284–289). X-TRM adds that the H&R enablement is configured by role (Party) or at securities-account level (the default configuration), that it is unavailable for CoSD instructions, and that for repos it applies to spot and forward legs separately, even after the spot has settled (`[[milan-xtrm-lifecycle]]` §3.4.7 C, PDF 47).

**5.7 Partial release.** A Release Instruction may release part of the quantity if: the referenced instruction exists; the quantity is lower than the original and complies with the Settlement Unit Multiple and its decimals; the same Settlement Type is used; **the ISD has been reached**; the Securities Movement Type is 'DELI'; and the referenced instruction is not T2S-generated. Then further checks: matched; not awaiting approval or revoked; not settled; not cancelled; not CoSD-flagged; on Party Hold; compliant with partial-settlement rules (MSU, SUM, cash threshold); **no other hold applies to it or to its counterparty instruction; none of the related realignment instructions is on hold**; both instructions allow partial settlement; and neither is constrained by a settlement link (`[[t2s-hold-release]]` §1.6.1.6.6, PDF 292–294). A partial release is valid for the current business day only: at the relevant cut-off it is cancelled and the instruction returns to Party Hold for the full unsettled quantity (`[[t2s-partial-settlement]]` §1.6.1.9.3, PDF 344).

**5.8 Partial settlement.** T2S settles a fraction of the quantity "when full settlement is not possible due to lack of securities or cash" — but partial settlement is **triggered only for lack of securities** (securities only, or securities and cash), **not for lack of cash only** (footnote 224). Conditions: the partial-settlement window is running; the instructions are eligible; the threshold criteria are met. Eligibility requires that the matched pair is FOP, DVP or DWP, that the partial settlement indicator is not "No" on either instruction, and that they are **not linked by the T2S parties** through Before, After, With or a pool reference. Thresholds are in quantity or in cash value depending on instruction type, instruction threshold type, ISIN and currency (Table 68); cash-value thresholds are set by the T2S Operator per settlement currency and separately for unit-quoted and nominal-quoted ISINs — **the actual values are not in this excerpt**. A full settlement attempt always precedes a partial one (`[[t2s-partial-settlement]]` §1.6.1.9.3 with Table 68 and footnotes 224–229, PDF 343–345; reviewed 14 September 2026, source reviewed 13 September 2026).
**Reasoned inference, flagged:** the eligibility exclusion is written for links set **by the T2S parties**, and the partial-release checks expressly contemplate "related Realignment instructions", so T2S-generated realignment links do not read as an automatic bar to partial settlement. This is an inference from two passages, **not a documented rule**; Table 71 (rules applying to linked instructions) is not admitted, so confirm before relying on it.

**5.9 Resource contention.** Where many instructions hit the same resource (same DCA, or the same securities account not allowed to go negative), "the Settlement Instructions could be grouped without any business links between one another", and the optimising process may be invoked (`[[t2s-posting]]` §1.6.1.8.1, PDF 303).

## 6. Finality and cancellation boundaries

Three moments, all from the Milan Service Regulations as of 26 January 2026 (`[[milan-finality]]` Articles 69–72, PDF 50–51 (printed 49–50); reviewed 13 September 2026; **English translation, the Italian text prevails**; source identity checked, no independent whole-edition supervisory approval certification; LIMITATION: no insolvency runbook, legal opinion or unreviewed Article 72 remainder admitted):

1. **SF1 — entry.** Instructions are "entered" into the Settlement System for the purposes of Article 2(2) of Legislative Decree 210/2001 **from the moment the validation time in T2S ends**.
2. **SF2 — irrevocability.** Instructions cannot be revoked by a participant or a third party **from the time of their matching in T2S**, *without prejudice to bilateral cancellation under Article 70(2)*. Matching is therefore irrevocability-subject-to-bilateral-cancellation, not settlement.
3. **SF3 — finality.** The transfer of securities and cash becomes final **from the time of the debiting of the cash**, or of the securities where settlement by cash is not provided for. For this EUR DvP, the cash debit is the operative event.

On the platform side, posting "updates the cash balance, securities position and limit headroom, **resulting in the irrevocability of the settlement**" (`[[t2s-posting]]` §1.6.1.8.1, PDF 303; reviewed 13 September 2026).

**Boundaries to respect in design.** (a) The `sese.032.001.11` realignment notifications announce **created instructions**, not completed ledger movements. (b) The all-or-none relationship binds the business instruction to **its own realignment chain**; it does not establish atomicity of an arbitrary two-security exchange (LIMITATION on `[[t2s-realignment]]`). (c) Cancellation after matching is bilateral only, and CoSD instructions only by Monte Titoli. (d) Whether the buyer's CSD applies the same finality moments under its own rules is **not in this bundle** — only Milan's Regulations were retrieved.

INTRODUCTION




Clients of Euronext Securities Milan (ES-MIL) are allowed to move securities between
their ES-MIL’s account and an account on a different CSD through the links that ES-MIL
has established with several foreign CSDs, in T2S and outside T2S.

While T2S helped to standardize cross csd instructions, there are still several cases,
especially on external transactions, which may require the usage of specific settlement
formats.

In addition, ES-MIL’s Clients, also need to provide their counterparties with their correct
SSI (Securities Settlement Instructions) in order to settle a transaction.

This document is designed with the aim to provide Client’s with a useful tool that
summarizes all the information needed to execute a transfer from/to ES-MIL, versus
another CSD, in a single page.

This Document contains:

    •  Information on how to identify the CSD where ES-MIL holds a foreign security
    •  Templates for allowed external settlement instructions in Swift 15022 and XTRM
    •  Templates for T2S allowed cross csd settlement instructions in Swift 15022 and
     XTRM
    •  Settlement restrictions
    •  SSI to be shared with Counterparties to help them matching ES-MIL’s Clients
       instructions where applicable

We aim to keep this Document updated reflecting the latest changes in the market.

ES-MIL is not responsible for the correctness of your settlement instruction, the
counterparty will always have to provide you with their most updated SSI, we kindly ask
you to get in touch with ES-MIL in case of doubts.

3.4.2 Validation in X-TRM Service

Validation is the process specific for each type of operation, which  executes
formal, logical and congruency checks on the elementary data of each individual
settlement instruction.

If the operation is not validated in X-TRM, the service sends a rejection message
to the subject that entered it.

If the operation exceeds correctly the validation process in X-TRM, the Service
forwards the settlement instruction to the subsequent phases of the process.

  3.4.3 Valorisation (c.d.“Enrichment”)

The  valorization  is  the X-TRM  process  that  allows  to add  the  necessary
information to complete the settlement instructions.

The additional information consist of the data in the database of the X-TRM (e.g.
relations between the parties, securities account and other default information)
and other data calculated according to the algorithm defined.

The functionality of enhancement allows, according to predefined algorithms, of:

    •  calculate the cash amount of a CVT operation, using the details available
       in the messages and other information stored in the database of the X-
     TRM;

    •  create the settlement instructions ("spot" and "forward") of an operation
     PCT / PCR valued using data available in the transaction originally entered
     by the participant.

Valorisation  is performed only  if no  errors have been detected during the
validation phase.

  3.4.4 Doubling operations

All trades sent from markets, central banks and central counterparties, which by
definition are already matched, are submitted to the doubling process.

The process consists of duplicating individual communications received by the X-
TRM Service into two matched operations, so that each counterparty can fully
visualise their operations.

The X-TRM Service attributes a reference code to each individual operation.

  3.4.5 Routing of settlement instructions to the Settlement Service

Once  completed  the  Valorization  process  the X-TRM  forwards  settlement
instructions to the Settlement Service.


The forwarding of operations to the Settlement Service occurs in two different
methods:

•  batch method: before the starting of the night-time settlement phase;

•  real-time: at the moment when the instructions are entered, during the
   opening hours of the X-TRM Service.

Settlement instructions  relating to operations acquired from Markets and/or
Central Counterparties are forwarded to the Settlement Service according to the
timing agreed respectively with the Central Counterparties and the Markets and
indicated in the Service Notice.

Settlement Instructions relating to OTC transactions are sent in real time to the
Settlement Service as they must be subjected to matching.

T2S platform conducts  its own validation applying specific rules to check the
required fields and optional fields and / or additional possibly used.

If the settlement instruction  is not validated in T2S, X-TRM Service sends a
rejection message to the subject that entered it.

Only if the settlement instruction passes also the Validation process in T2S the X-
TRM Service confers a univocal reference code (reference ID) and sends a
message of acceptance to the person who posted it.

       instruction not found;

   C. HOLD AND RELEASE FUNCTIONALITY

The X-TRM Service allows the suspension of settlement instructions sent to the
Settlement  Service,  in  the manner and  within  the  limits  specified  in  the
document T2S User Requirements.

To the settlement instructions is attributed to the state of ‘’release’’ unless one of
the four indicators "hold" provided by the T2S platform (Party Hold, Hold CSD,
CSD Validation Hold, Hold cOsd) is activated.

The enabling to the use of H&R functionality can be configured based on the role
(Party)  of  the  Participant  or  at  the  level  of  securities  account  (default
configuration).


The hold & release functionality is not avaible for settlement instructions entered
in CoSD modality.

The change of the indicator may occur during the opening of the X-TRM Service.

requested the suspension "hold" of a settlement instruction, on the ISD and only
if the corresponding settlement instruction is in the "release" state.

The valorisation of the hold/release indicator for the repurchase agreements
applies both to the spot and to the forward transactions and  it is possible to
change it distinctly for each of the two operations, also after the settlement of
the spot transaction.

The hold and release functionality of operations entered by the Markets and by
the Central Counterparties,  it  is possible for X- TRM Participants only  if the
management  company  of  the  markets  and  central  counterparties  have
communicated to Monte Titoli to make this functionality available.
