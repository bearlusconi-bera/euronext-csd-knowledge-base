# "Matched" in T2S — has your client at Monte Titoli received the shares?

## Direct answer

**No. Matched is not settled.** Matching only establishes that the two sides' instructions agree on the settlement terms; the securities reach your client's account only when the posting process succeeds and the relevant debit takes place. Under Monte Titoli's rules, matching (SF2) makes the instruction irrevocable — but expressly "without prejudice to the bilateral cancellation" — while finality of the transfer of securities and cash arises only at SF3, the debiting of the cash, or of the securities where settlement against cash is not provided for. Between those two points an instruction can still fail for lack of resources, sit on hold, be cancelled bilaterally, or be cancelled automatically if it is not settled within the time limits in the Instructions.

## What matching is — and what it is not

**Documented requirement.** The T2S matching process "compares the settlement details of Settlement Instructions provided by the deliverer and the receiver of securities to ensure that both parties agree on the settlement terms of the transaction in a standardised way". It compares mandatory and non-mandatory (additional, optional) matching fields against instructions that remain unmatched in T2S; settlement restrictions, maintenance, realignment, auto-collateralisation, reimbursement and liquidity transfer instructions do not go through matching, and instructions entering T2S as already matched are created with the matching fields as if they had matched in T2S. **Documented requirement.** Monte Titoli's Service Regulations put it the same way: "The matching is carried out to check that the information corresponds to the settlement instructions entered", and T2S supplies participants with complete disclosure of the status of instructions entered and of the counterparty's instruction awaiting matching (allegement). [[milan-finality]] Regulations as of 26 January 2026, Article 69, PDF 50 (printed 49); version 26 January 2026; reviewed 13 September 2026; English translation, **Italian text prevails**; source identity checked, no independent whole-edition supervisory approval certification.

*Explanation of the term (background wording, not a documented requirement):* an **allegement** is the notice telling you that a counterparty instruction is sitting in the system waiting to be matched against yours.

## The three moments that matter (SF1, SF2, SF3)

**Documented requirement**, all from [[milan-finality]] Regulations as of 26 January 2026, Article 72(1)–(3), PDF 51 (printed 50); version 26 January 2026; reviewed 13 September 2026; English translation, Italian text prevails:

| Moment | What the rules say | What it means for your client's shares |
|---|---|---|
| **SF1 — entered** | Instructions are deemed "entered" into the Settlement System, pursuant to Article 2(2) of Legislative Decree 210/2001, from the moment the validation time in T2S ends. | The instruction is in the system. No securities have moved. |
| **SF2 — matched** | Instructions cannot be revoked by a participant or a third party from the time of their matching in T2S, "without prejudice to the bilateral cancellation of settlement Instructions provided for under Article 70(2)". | This is the status your screen is showing. Still no securities have moved. |
| **SF3 — final transfer** | "The transfer of securities and cash become final from the time of the debiting of the cash, or of the securities when settlement by cash is not provided for." | This — not matching — is the point at which the delivery has happened. |

LIMITATIONS carried with the table: SF2 retains the Article 70(2) bilateral cancellation; SF3 is the relevant cash debit, or the securities debit for free-of-payment instructions. No insolvency runbook, legal opinion, or the unreviewed remainder of Article 72 is admitted as evidence here, so do not read the table as a complete account of insolvency-related finality.

## Why a matched instruction can still not deliver

**Documented requirement.** Posting is the step that actually moves the position: "The posting application process checks if the settlement of Settlement Instructions, Settlement Restrictions and Liquidity Transfers can be achieved considering their eligibility to settlement and the available resources… When the check is satisfactory, the posting application process updates the cash balance, securities position and limit headroom, resulting in the irrevocability of the settlement." Instructions are submitted to posting at the intended settlement date. [[t2s-posting]] T2S UDFS R2026.JUN, §1.6.1.8.1 and the first overview paragraph, PDF 303–304; version R2026.JUN; reviewed 13 September 2026; body language English, no authoritative language independently established.

**Documented requirement.** Even after matching, Monte Titoli's rules allow: bilateral cancellation of matched instructions with the consent of both participants (or by an entity acting on their behalf under a mandate previously submitted to Monte Titoli), with the cancellation itself going through acquisition and matching so that the original instruction is cancelled only when the cancellations match [[milan-finality]] Article 70(2)–(3); hold of settlement, or hold of the re-proposal of unsettled instructions until specific release, unless entered as non-changeable [[milan-finality]] Article 71(1); and automatic cancellation from the T2S platform where instructions have not passed the daily validation phase, or "are not matched or are not settled within the time limits provided in the Instructions" [[milan-finality]] Article 70(7). Participants are informed of the progress and outcome of cancellation, including automatic cancellation [[milan-finality]] Article 70(8). Same document, version 26 January 2026, PDF 50–51 (printed 49–50); reviewed 13 September 2026; English translation, Italian text prevails.

**Reasoned inference** (derived from Article 72(3) read with the posting concept in §1.6.1.8.1): since finality attaches to the debit and posting only updates positions once eligibility and available resources are confirmed, a "matched" status is evidence of agreement and irrevocability in the SF2 sense, not evidence that resources were available or that the book entry occurred. To answer your client you need the **settlement status / confirmation for the instruction**, not the matching status.
