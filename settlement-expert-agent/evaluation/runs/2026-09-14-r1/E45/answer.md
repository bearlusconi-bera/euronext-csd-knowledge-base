### Not the same thing: Porto's *indirect* links

**Documented requirement** — Chapter 15 separately describes **indirect links** for foreign securities registered at Euronext Securities Porto but issued in a foreign CSD (one reason being admission to trading on Euronext Lisbon). These are held through the **Interlinking Financial Intermediary (participant) model**: prior to any operation, the securities registered in another CSD must be registered, in the name of the interlinking participant, in a specially opened "Registration and Control Account", which is a **mirror account** of the values registered in an account of the foreign depositary entity, with only registrations made in the Porto account. The interlinking participant must apply and send Porto the characteristics of the securities, the identification of the CSD where the issue is registered, the identification of the foreign depositary entity, and the number of securities that will circulate in Portugal in the first instance and their distribution among the participants responsible for those securities accounts. Porto debits and credits that account as the number of securities in circulation in Portugal changes, subject to prior information from the interlinking Financial Intermediary [[porto-cross-csd-links]] (same locator and qualifications).

*Explanation (not a documented requirement): "interlinking financial intermediary" here is the manual's term for a participant that holds the foreign issue at the foreign CSD and mirrors it at Porto; this route is not a T2S CSD link.*

## 2. Realignment: cross-CSD versus external-CSD

*Explanation (not a documented requirement): realignment is the set of book movements across the chain of CSDs that keeps each CSD's omnibus holdings consistent with a transfer between participants of different CSDs.*

**Documented requirement — the two categories.** T2S distinguishes two types of cross-border settlement transaction: **cross-CSD settlement**, i.e. cross-border settlement between participants of different CSDs **that are in T2S**; and **external-CSD settlement**, i.e. cross-border settlement between participants of different CSDs **with some of the CSD(s) involved being external to T2S** [[porto-cross-csd-links]] Operational Manual of INTERBOLSA, Chapter 15, PDF 183–184 (printed 182–183); V43, internal date 26 January 2026; reviewed 2026-09-14 (source reviewed 2026-09-13); English version, Portuguese text governs. The same two categories are defined in the platform documentation: the realignment application process handles "Cross-CSD settlements, i.e. settlements between T2S Actors of different CSDs, the latter being in T2S" and "External-CSD settlements, i.e. settlements between T2S Actors of different CSDs, with some of the CSDs involved in the settlement being external to T2S" [[t2s-realignment]] T2S User Detailed Functional Specifications (UDFS), §1.6.1.10, PDF 373–376; version R2026.JUN; reviewed 2026-09-13 (source reviewed 2026-09-13); body language English, no authoritative-language designation established; source identity checked, no independent whole-edition supervisory approval certification.

**Documented requirement — cross-CSD.** Porto's manual: "In cross-CSD settlement, T2S automatically generates the settlement instructions related to the realignment process (between CSDs) to which the underlying settlement instructions are linked" [[porto-cross-csd-links]] (same locator, English version, Portuguese governs). The UDFS gives the mechanics: cross-CSD settlement is achieved in T2S with the **simultaneous booking of cash and securities** for settlement instructions between participants of different CSDs; once incoming settlement instructions are **matched** (or **validated**, for already-matched incoming instructions), the realignment application process **automatically creates all the requested settlement instructions between the involved CSDs** ("T2S generated realignment Settlement Instructions"); this generation **relies on links set in the reference data between the relevant CSDs and does not request any other action from T2S Actors**, and it takes place immediately following that validation or matching [[t2s-realignment]] UDFS §1.6.1.10.1–1.6.1.10.2, PDF 373–374; R2026.JUN; reviewed 2026-09-13.

**Documented requirement — linkage and all-or-none.** The T2S-generated realignment settlement instructions are validated and then linked to the initial underlying settlement instructions through **two INFO links** providing the references of both business settlement instructions **for information purposes**, and **T2S ensures that the T2S-generated realignment settlement instructions and their business settlement instructions settle on an all-or-none basis** [[t2s-realignment]] UDFS §1.6.1.10.2, PDF 375; R2026.JUN; reviewed 2026-09-13. Consistently, the linked-instructions section states that instructions linked automatically by T2S cover operations such as realignment, auto-collateralisation and corporate rebalancing liquidity, and that T2S ensures in those cases settlement on an all-or-none basis of the initial settlement instruction together with the T2S-generated instruction; note that **INFO is "for information purpose. There is no processing in T2S behind this code"**, unlike the actor-specified BEFO / AFTE / WITH processing position codes, and that a T2S internally generated settlement instruction **cannot** be linked by a T2S Actor [[t2s-linked-instructions]] UDFS §1.6.1.11.1–1.6.1.11.3 with footnote 243, PDF 442–444; R2026.JUN; reviewed 2026-09-14 (source reviewed 2026-09-13); body language English. LIMITATIONs on that section: "Table 71 validation rules for links (PDF 448) are not admitted", and it is a "T2S-native functional description for R2026.JUN; not a local participant interface specification, production XSD or message usage guideline."

**Reasoned inference** — derived from the two preceding paragraphs read together: the all-or-none guarantee the UDFS states is between a *business* instruction and *its own* T2S-generated realignment chain, and the INFO links carry no processing. So the reader should not infer from INFO links alone that the chain is held together; the all-or-none property is the UDFS's own separate statement about the generated realignment instructions. This also does **not** establish atomicity of an arbitrary two-security exchange — LIMITATION on [[t2s-realignment]]: "Does not establish atomicity of an arbitrary two-security swap or describe every action outside T2S."

**Documented requirement — external-CSD.** Porto's manual: "For external CSD settlement, the realignment has to be **managed outside T2S**" [[porto-cross-csd-links]] (same locator, English version, Portuguese governs). The UDFS states that the realignment application process "is also applied for external-CSD settlement", but that **for external-CSD settlement only the process applying to the settlement instructions actually submitted to T2S is described, and all actions required by the realignment but without interaction with T2S are not described** [[t2s-realignment]] UDFS §1.6.1.10.1, PDF 374; R2026.JUN; reviewed 2026-09-13.

**Reasoned inference** — derived from those two excerpts: they are compatible rather than contradictory. T2S processes the legs that are submitted to it; the remaining legs of the realignment chain, involving the CSD(s) outside T2S, sit outside the platform and outside the UDFS description, which is what Porto's manual means by "managed outside T2S". What that out-of-platform management consists of operationally for Porto (who instructs, in what form, on what timeline, with what reconciliation) is **not in reviewed evidence** in this bundle.

**Documented requirement — matching and posting boundaries that frame the above.** Realignment instructions **do not go through the T2S matching process** (alongside settlement restrictions, maintenance instructions, auto-collateralisation and reimbursement auto-collateralisation instructions and liquidity transfers); T2S also allows CSDs and CSD participants to send **already matched** instructions cross-CSD and intra-CSD, created with the matching fields as if they had matched in T2S [[t2s-matching]] UDFS §1.6.1.2.2, PDF 268; R2026.JUN; reviewed 2026-09-13; LIMITATIONs: "Functional matrix only; no production XML/XSD validation or local interface certification" and "Retain diagram DVP/DWP labels; paragraph separately mentions DVP/PFOD". Separately, the **posting** application process checks whether settlement can be achieved considering eligibility to settlement and available resources, and when the check is satisfactory it updates cash balance, securities position and limit headroom, "resulting in the irrevocability of the settlement"; instructions sent by T2S Actors **or automatically generated by T2S** are submitted to posting at the Intended Settlement Date [[t2s-posting]] UDFS §1.6.1.8.1–1.6.1.8.2, PDF 303–304; R2026.JUN; reviewed 2026-09-13.

**Reasoned inference** — from [[t2s-matching]] and [[t2s-posting]] read together: a matched business instruction plus a generated realignment chain is not a completed book movement. Generation of realignment instructions follows matching/validation; the transfer only occurs at posting, subject to eligibility and resources. Matching is agreement on terms, not settlement.

## 3. How the realignment chain itself is derived (reference-data prerequisites)

**Documented requirement** — from [[t2s-realignment]] UDFS §1.6.1.10.2–1.6.1.10.3, PDF 374–376; R2026.JUN; reviewed 2026-09-13.

1. On matching (or on validation of already-matched instructions), the process verifies whether the incoming **business** settlement instructions require realignment settlement instructions on securities accounts **other than those of the submitting T2S Actors** (for example on the accounts of the issuer CSD).
2. Roles are defined per security and relationship: a CSD is the **issuer CSD** when it is the CSD in which the security has been issued and distributed on behalf of the issuer; the **investor CSD** when it is the CSD of at least one party of the settlement instruction; **or both**, when it is both. This holds irrespective of whether the settlement is cross-CSD or external-CSD.
3. Each investor CSD chooses between opening an **omnibus account** in the books of the issuer CSD, or in the books of any other CSD already an investor CSD for the same financial instrument. In both cases the CSD where the omnibus account is opened is the **technical issuer** of the investor CSD for those securities.
4. For a given ISIN an investor CSD may define **several** investor-type CSD links (several technical issuer CSDs). Exactly one is flagged **"default"**; the others are **"alternative"**. Only one link (default or alternative) may point to a given technical issuer CSD for a given investor CSD and ISIN at a time.
5. Preference: under **simple** configurations (all CSDs in T2S, no multi-issuance) the default link is preferred. Under **more complex configurations (external CSD configuration, multi-issuance)** preference goes first to an **alternative** link, specifically the one pointing to the counterpart CSD where it is set up in reference data; if T2S cannot find an alternative link to the counterparty CSD, or finds one that is unusable because of missing static data (invalid or incomplete configuration of CSD Account Links), T2S **reverts to the valid default links**.
6. **The issuer-type CSD link cannot be an alternative link — it is always "default". Only investor-type links can be flagged "alternative", they cannot be defined for an investor CSD outside T2S and they cannot point to a technical issuer CSD outside T2S.** The issuer CSD sets a CSD link with itself as issuer, always a default link.
7. Reference-data requirement ("Security CSD links"): each investor CSD must define at least one technical issuer CSD per security it intends to make eligible for settlement; for a given investor CSD the technical issuer CSD may differ per security and "is in most cases the issuer CSD of the security".
8. This set-up is what T2S uses to derive the realignment chain for matched settlement instructions: **from both investor CSDs (delivering and receiving) up to the issuer CSD(s) of the traded securities when default links are used**, or **from the delivering investor CSD up to the receiving investor CSD (or vice versa) when alternative links are used**.

**Reasoned inference** — derived from points 5 and 6: because alternative links cannot exist for, or point to, a CSD outside T2S, an external-CSD configuration cannot shorten its chain with an alternative link towards the out-of-T2S CSD; the in-T2S part of the chain runs on default links up to the issuer CSD, and the remainder is the part the UDFS says it does not describe and Porto's manual says is managed outside T2S.

**Unresolved requirement** — which of Porto's named links are configured as investor-type or issuer-type, which are default and which alternative, and for which ISINs, is **not in reviewed evidence**. LIMITATION on [[t2s-realignment]]: "Actual links/accounts and ISIN eligibility require verification."
