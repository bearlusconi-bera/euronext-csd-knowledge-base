# Hold and partial release for an ICP at Euronext Securities Porto (INTERBOLSA), and the messages that carry the status

## Direct answer

An ICP — an *indirectly connected participant*, i.e. one that reaches T2S through the CSD rather than through its own T2S connection — puts an instruction on Hold at Euronext Securities Porto either **at registration** (STD function "I" – Inclusion, with "H" in the "Hold" field) or **after registration** (STD function "H" – Hold, with "H" in the "Hold" field), and can alternatively send an **ISO 15022 MT530**. It then **partially releases** by sending an amendment request with the **"R" (Release)** function, a **blank** "Hold" field and the **quantity to be released** — via **STD (SLRTmsg / SLRTfile)** or via **MT530 field `:36B::SETT//`**. Status comes back on the **STD `SLRT` message** (with Porto's SLRT reason codes) and on **ISO 15022 MT548**, which reports the partially released quantity and the quantity remaining on Hold in `:70E::SPRO//`; STD reports the same pair in the `Participant Remarks` field. `sese.030 / sese.031 / sese.024` are the **DCP** route, not the ICP route.

All four retrievals in the bundle returned **`evidence_only`**; nothing was `blocked`, `needs_context` or `needs_refresh`. Two retrievals are in **`reference` mode on a publication-description basis** (the INTERBOLSA Operational Manual) and several LIMITATION lines apply; both are carried with the claims below.

**Evidence-handling qualification that governs the whole answer.** The Porto material is the **English version of the Operational Manual of INTERBOLSA V43** (internal date 26 January 2026; filename 16 February 2026; posted April 2026); **Portuguese is the authoritative language and the Portuguese text governs**, and **the manual's operative effective date is unresolved**. The manual's own cross-references — Articles 45 and 46 of INTERBOLSA Regulation No 2/2016 for hold/release/amendment and Article 57 for partial settlement — are **not in the bundle**, so the regulation itself is not reviewed evidence here.

## 1. The ICP sequence, step by step

**Documented requirement.** Euronext Securities Porto "allows the settlement instructions to be placed in a state of 'Hold' and subsequently 'released', either totally or partially."

| Step | What the ICP does | Message / field | Status returned |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1a | Hold **at registration** | STD function **"I"** (Inclusion), **"H"** in the "Hold" field | STD `SLRT`: **NMAT** with reason code **001** (Operation registered) and **002** (Operation registered by counterparty); **MACH** with reason code **003** (Matched transaction, pending settlement) sent when the match occurs |
| 1b | Hold **after registration** (unmatched or matched) | STD function **"H"** (Hold), **"H"** in the "Hold" field | STD `SLRT`: **NMAT/MACH** with reason code **023** (instruction placed on Hold) and **024** (counterpart instruction placed on Hold) |
| 1c | Hold/Release alternative for ICPs | **ISO 15022 MT530** | (see rows above/below) |
| 2 | **Total** release | STD function **"R"** (Release), **" " (blank)** in the "Hold" field | STD `SLRT`: **NMAT/MACH** with reason code **025** (instruction Released) and **026** (counterpart instruction released) |
| 3 | **Partial** release | Amendment request selecting the **'Release'** function and filling in the desired quantity — ICPs: **STD `SLRTmsg`/`SLRTfile`**, or **MT530** field **`:36B::SETT//`** ("Quantity of Financial Instrument to be settled") | ICPs: **STD `SLRT`** reports the partial amount released and the remaining quantity in 'Hold' in the field **`Participant Remarks`**; **MT548** "Settlement Status and Processing Advice" reports the Partially Released Quantity and Quantity Remaining on Hold in **`:70E::SPRO//`** |
| 4 | Cancel the partial release | Put the instruction **back into Hold** | (status advice as above) |

All rows: [[porto-hold-release-amend]] Operational Manual of INTERBOLSA §12.2 (§12.2.1 Hold/Release), PDF 127–131, printed pages 126–130; version Operational Manual **V43**, internal date 26 January 2026, filename 16 February 2026, posted April 2026; section reviewed **14 September 2026**, source reviewed 13 September 2026; **English version, Portuguese authoritative and governing, operative effective date unresolved**; mode **reference**, basis *reference_description*. LIMITATION carried: **reason codes 001–027 are STD SLRT codes as printed**.

**Documented requirement — when Hold may be used.** The Hold function "can be used at any time before the (physical and financial) settlement of the transaction", i.e. at registration, before matching, after matching or during the settlement day; instructions (unmatched or matched) can be put on Hold until the instruction is settled or cancelled; and **an instruction that is pending partial settlement can be placed in Hold** — [[porto-hold-release-amend]] §12.2.1, PDF 128, printed 127; V43 (English, Portuguese governs, effective date unresolved); reviewed 14 September 2026.

**Documented requirement — effect of release on a matched instruction.** When a "MACH" (matched) transaction changes to released — one party uses the Release function and the counterparty is already released — "it is sent for settlement in real time", unless the contracted settlement date is in the future or the settlement cut-off time has been exceeded, in which case it is submitted for settlement as soon as possible. Porto informs the issuing participant and the counterparty participant of the status of the instruction and of any changes in status (from Hold to Release and vice versa) — [[porto-hold-release-amend]] §12.2.1, PDF 130, printed 129; same version and qualifications.

## 2. Partial-release conditions as Porto prints them

**Documented requirement.** Partial Release is possible for securities **delivery** instructions (DVP, DFP, DWP) that are:

1. **Matched and not cancelled**;
2. **Pending, in Hold by participant**;
3. **Allowing partial settlement**;
4. **Past ISD** — "on which ISD (Intended Settlement Date) has been reached (i.e., a request for partial release can only be submitted as of SoD, which occurs at 18:45 CET)";

and further:

5. The **quantity to be released must be less than the original quantity** of the settlement instruction;
6. If **NPAR** (no authorisation for partial settlement) is indicated in at least one of the instructions ('delivery' or 'receive'), the partial-release request **shall not be accepted**;
7. If the partial settlement indicator **PARQ** (quantity) has not been included and the instruction involves cash (DVP, DWP), the minimum threshold of **EUR 10,000 for stocks and EUR 100,000 for debt** must be adhered to for partial settlement to take place;
8. The partial release process has **only one life cycle during the release day** — if a partially released quantity does not settle by the end of the settlement cut-off, **T2S automatically cancels the process and the instruction returns to its original state (Hold of the initial quantity)**;
9. Partial Release **is allowed for instructions with ISD in the past**;
10. If the **counterparty (receive) instruction is on 'Hold', the partial-release request is rejected by the T2S platform**;
11. **Multiple partial releases are not permitted for the same instruction at the same time**; only one new partial release may be made after the previous order has been settled;
12. When an instruction is partially released, "the instruction will attempt to settle by the released quantity".

[[porto-hold-release-amend]] Operational Manual of INTERBOLSA §12.2.1, PDF 128–130, printed 127–129; V43, internal date 26 January 2026, posted April 2026; reviewed 14 September 2026, source reviewed 13 September 2026; English version, **Portuguese text governs**, operative effective date unresolved; mode reference / reference_description.

**Documented requirement — the amount thresholds are printed values, not configuration you may rely on.** The default financial limits for instructions involving cash are printed as "**More than 10,000 EUR for shares**" and "**More than 100.000 EUR for debt**", and the default quantity limit for FOP as "minimum settlement unit and multiple settlement unit" — [[porto-settlement-processing]] Operational Manual of INTERBOLSA §12.5.3, PDF 138, printed 137; V43; reviewed 14 September 2026. LIMITATION carried, and it is material: **the default partial-settlement limits (EUR 10,000 shares / EUR 100,000 debt) are as printed and the T2S Operator parameters govern.** Treat them as indicative of the documented default, not as the live configured threshold.

**Documented requirement — partial settlement windows and indicators.** Partial settlement, if allowed, is applied when full settlement is not possible due to a securities failure, **in the last settlement run of the night settlement period** and **in the partial settlement "windows" of the daytime period** (the manual points to its chapter 4 "Timetable and Schedules", which is **not in the bundle**). An instruction qualifies if the partial settlement window is occurring, the instruction was flagged for partial settlement, and the partial settlement limit is satisfied; the instruction type must be FOP, DVP or DWP, the indicator must not be NPAR on either leg, the instructions must not be linked by LINK 'Before'/'After'/'With' or a pool reference, and the financial amount must be in a T2S-eligible currency. Participants may send **NPAR, PARC, PARQ, PART or BLANK**. Once partial settlement is invoked you may only change the priority, change the instruction **in Hold for Release**, or cancel the instructions with pending partial settlements; the original instruction is **not** automatically cancelled but has its quantity and status updated — [[porto-settlement-processing]] §12.5.3, PDF 136–138, printed 135–137; V43; reviewed 14 September 2026; English version, Portuguese governs, effective date unresolved.

## 3. What the platform is doing underneath (T2S UDFS R2026.JUN)

**Documented requirement.** T2S Hold/Release gives T2S Actors the functionality to hold and release Settlement Instructions **at any time during the lifecycle until they are settled or cancelled**; it is also possible to hold the pending part of a partially settled instruction and, **under specific conditions, to partially release** one. A Hold/Release Instruction carries **only one modification per instruction**, and an instruction can also be sent initially on Hold — [[t2s-hold-release]] T2S User Detailed Functional Specifications R2026.JUN (UDFS) §1.6.1.6.1, PDF 284; version R2026.JUN; reviewed 14 September 2026, source reviewed 13 September 2026; English body text, authoritative language not independently established, source identity checked with no independent whole-edition supervisory approval certification.

**Documented requirement — four hold statuses.** Two indicators are filled by the T2S Actor (**Party Hold**, **CSD Hold**) and three are set automatically by T2S (**CSD Validation Hold**, **Party Hold** via footnote 196 — restriction type case 1, or the securities account's "Hold Release Default" — and **CoSD Hold**). "T2S considers an Instruction on Hold and consequently not eligible for settlement when at least one of the four statuses (Party Hold, CSD Hold, CSD Validation Hold or CoSD Hold) is put to 'Yes'." Table 62 enumerates the scenarios: only scenario 1 (all four "NO") is eligible for settlement. An instruction on Hold can be released only by the actor or CSD that placed that hold, putting "No" in the relevant indicator; a CSD Validation Hold can be released only by the CSD that defined the rule — [[t2s-hold-release]] UDFS §1.6.1.6.1–1.6.1.6.2, Table 62 and footnotes 196–197, PDF 285–288; version R2026.JUN; reviewed 14 September 2026.

**Documented requirement — the instruction stays on Party Hold throughout a partial release.** "In case the Settlement Instruction is under Partial Release Process it will remain on Hold (Party Hold). However T2S considers it eligible for settlement but only when Partial Settlement is allowed", and footnote 197 makes the exception explicit for scenario 9: no settlement attempt unless the instruction is under Partial Release Process **and** partial settlement of such instructions is allowed, i.e. **Real Time Settlement or Sequence X of Night Time Settlement is running**. "During the whole Partial Release Process, the referenced Settlement Instruction will remain on Party Hold", and after the released quantity settles it stays on Party Hold until the actor sends a standard Release Instruction without a quantity, or one with a quantity equal to or higher than the quantity remaining on Party Hold — [[t2s-hold-release]] UDFS §1.6.1.6.2 (Table 62, footnote 197) and §1.6.1.6.6 with Example 80, PDF 287–288 and 293–295; version R2026.JUN; reviewed 14 September 2026.

**Documented requirement — T2S validation of a partial Release Instruction.** T2S checks that the referenced instruction exists; the quantity to be released is **lower** than the original quantity; the quantity complies with the **Settlement Unit Multiple** and its decimals; the quantity uses the same **Settlement Type** as T2S reference data for the ISIN; the **ISD has been reached**; the Securities Movement Type is **'DELI'**; and the referenced instruction is **not T2S-generated**. Failing any of these, the Release Instruction is rejected. A second subset of checks then governs execution: the referenced instruction is **matched**, not awaiting approval or revoked, not settled, not cancelled, not CoSD-flagged, **is on Party Hold**, complies with partial-settlement rules on MSU, SUM and cash threshold, **no other hold applies to it or its counterparty instruction**, no related realignment instruction is on Hold, both legs allow partial settlement, neither is constrained by a settlement link or a non-reciprocal link, and **neither has reached its cut-off time** — [[t2s-hold-release]] UDFS §1.6.1.6.6, PDF 293–294; version R2026.JUN; reviewed 14 September 2026.

**Documented requirement — automatic cancellation of the partial release process.** It is cancelled when any new hold applies to either leg; when a release instruction over the referenced instruction is received; when partial settlement is disallowed (NPAR) on either leg; when a business link is added by amendment or a non-reciprocal link; when the released quantity has not fully settled **by the relevant cut-off time**; or when the released quantity is less than the quantity resulting from the cash-threshold equivalent. To cancel it deliberately, **send a Party Hold instruction**; to change the released quantity, cancel with a Party Hold instruction and send a new partial release. If the quantity released is higher than the remaining quantity, it is handled as a **full** Release Instruction. The actor is informed of a cancellation through a Status Advice — [[t2s-hold-release]] UDFS §1.6.1.6.6, PDF 294; version R2026.JUN; reviewed 14 September 2026.

**Documented requirement — hold/release default.** If the Party Hold status is not set on an incoming instruction, T2S reads the "Hold Release Default" of the securities account in reference data: "Yes" puts the instruction automatically on Party Hold (with a Status Advice reporting acceptance and Party Hold "Yes"); "No" does not. The check is performed **only once, at first validation** — not at revalidation — and later changes to the reference-data value affect only instructions received after the change — [[t2s-hold-release]] UDFS §1.6.1.6.5, PDF 292–293; version R2026.JUN; reviewed 14 September 2026.

**LIMITATIONS carried for this whole section.** (i) **The release process narrative (UDFS §1.6.1.6.4, PDF 290–291) is not admitted** in this bundle — so the platform-side description of a *total* release is **not in reviewed evidence**, and the total-release account in section 1 above rests on Porto's manual alone. (ii) This is a **T2S-native functional description for R2026.JUN; it is not a local participant interface specification, production XSD or message usage guideline** — it does not tell you what Porto's STD carries on the wire.

## 4. The status model and which messages carry it

**Documented requirement.** A Settlement Instruction in T2S carries **seven statuses**: Settlement Status, Match Status, Cancellation Status, **CSD Hold Status, Party Hold Status, CSD Validation Hold Status and CoSD Hold Status**; each has one value at a given moment. T2S sends a **Status Advice** whenever a status value changes, or when the value is unchanged but the reason code or associated business rule changes, and a single Status Advice may report multiple status values (for example "accepted" together with "matched" or "Party Hold"). Statuses are classified as **Intermediate** or **End** status. Crucially for hold/release: "**If the instruction is matched, T2S also informs the counterpart of the instruction on the status updates with the exception of the status changes related to any of the Hold statuses (which are communicated to the counterparty on the Intended Settlement Day)**". Communication is real time during the day unless the actor opted for file bundling, which is deactivated during the maintenance window and the period close to the DVP cut-off; during night-time only settlement-related messages are sent, bundled into files of at most 32 MB, and at the end of every night-time sequence T2S sends the latest valid status values with their reason codes. Actors can query status values and reason codes at any time — [[t2s-status-management]] UDFS §1.6.3.1.1–1.6.3.1.3 and the list of Settlement Instruction statuses, PDF 653–656; version R2026.JUN; reviewed 14 September 2026, source reviewed 13 September 2026; English body text, authoritative language not independently established. LIMITATIONS carried: **status transition diagrams and the reason-code catalogue are not admitted**, and this is a T2S-native functional description, not a local interface specification.

**Consequence — Documented requirement.** The same rule appears on the Porto side: T2S informs the counterparty of hold-status changes only on the ISD (UDFS §1.6.3.1.3 and §1.6.1.6.3, PDF 289 and 655), while Porto's manual states that it informs the issuing participant **and** the counterparty participant of status changes from Hold to Release and vice versa (§12.2.1, PDF 130). **Reasoned inference:** these are consistent only if Porto's own client reporting is what reaches the ICP counterparty, distinct from T2S's counterparty notification timing; the bundle does not reconcile the two statements, so treat the exact counterparty-notification timing for an ICP as **not established in reviewed evidence**.

**Documented requirement — status messages by access model.**

| Event | ICP (via Porto) | DCP (direct in T2S) |
|---|---|---|
| Hold / Release command | STD function "I"/"H"/"R" with the "Hold" field; **ISO 15022 MT530** "Transaction Processing Command" | **ISO 20022 sese.030** "Securities Settlement Condition Modification Request" |
| Partial release command | STD **SLRTmsg / SLRTfile**; **MT530** field **`:36B::SETT//`** | **sese.030** with the quantity |
| Response to accepted partial release | STD **SLRT** — partial amount released and remaining quantity in Hold in **`Participant Remarks`**; **MT548** "Settlement Status and Processing Advice" — Partially Released Quantity and Quantity Remaining on Hold in **`:70E::SPRO//`** | **sese.031** informing acceptance with status 'completed'; **sese.024** "Securities Settlement Transaction Status Advice" stating the partial quantity released and the quantity remaining in 'Hold' |
| Registration / match / hold / release status | STD **SLRT** with reason codes 001, 002, 003, 023, 024, 025, 026 | Status Advice messages per T2S status management |
| Amendment of partial settlement, link or priority | STD function **"A"** (Amendment), reported to the amending participant only, reason code **027**; also **MT530** | **sese.030**, only if the instruction has 'YES' in the 'Allowed Modification Flag' |

**Documented requirement — STD `Participant Remarks` layout, exactly as printed.** The manual prints the field format as `R99999999999999,99 H999999999999,99` with the example `R123456789012.99 H123456789012.99`, where **R = released quantity** and **H = remaining quantity in Hold**.

**Unresolved requirement.** As printed, the mask and the example do not agree: the mask shows fourteen digits before the decimal for R and twelve for H and uses a comma as decimal separator, while the example uses twelve digits for both and a full stop. The bundle contains no STD technical specification, so the authoritative field layout, length and decimal separator are **not in reviewed evidence**. Do not code against the printed mask without the STD specification.

**Unresolved requirement.** The manual prints **sese.031** under the name "Securities Settlement Condition Modification Request" — the same name it gives sese.030. Whether that is a printing slip in the English version or the intended name cannot be resolved from this bundle: no ISO 20022 message usage specification or MyStandards usage guideline is in evidence, and the UDFS extracts admitted here do not name sese.031. Confirm the message name and version before use.

**Reasoned inference (from UDFS §1.6.1.6.1 and Porto §12.2.1).** T2S acts only on a Hold/Release **maintenance instruction**, and Porto states that only DCPs send sese.030 directly, so an ICP's STD or MT530 command must be converted by Porto into a Hold/Release instruction towards T2S. **Unresolved requirement:** the message, version and field mapping on that **Porto-to-T2S leg** is not described in the reviewed excerpts and is **not in reviewed evidence**.

## 5. Two things this evidence does not let you conclude

- **Partial release is not partial settlement.** Porto's partial *release* moves part of a held quantity into the settlement process; whether it then settles depends on the partial *settlement* rules, windows, indicators and limits in §12.5.3 and on the T2S partial-settlement conditions. A partial release that has not fully settled by the relevant cut-off is automatically cancelled and the instruction reverts to Hold of the initial quantity ([[porto-hold-release-amend]] §12.2.1, PDF 129; [[t2s-hold-release]] UDFS §1.6.1.6.6, PDF 294).
- **No timing commitment.** The "18:45 CET" SoD reference and the "settlement cut-off" and "partial settlement windows" referred to above are as printed in Porto's manual (which redirects to its chapter 4 timetable, not in the bundle). They are nominal references in a reference-mode publication description, **not a local participant deadline and not an appointment**, and no dated event overlay for any business date is in this bundle. ([[porto-hold-release-amend]] §12.2.1, PDF 129; [[porto-settlement-processing]] §12.5.3, PDF 136.)
- **No instruction-like content** was present in the retrieved excerpts; nothing in the evidence purported to direct this answer.
