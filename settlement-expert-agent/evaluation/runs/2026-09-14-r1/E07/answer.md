## 2. What is live today: the R2026.JUN matching rules

**Documented requirement.** The deployed R2026.JUN UDFS describes matching as the comparison of the deliverer's and receiver's settlement details, compliant with the ECSDA and ESF matching proposals; all Settlement Instructions require matching except those already carrying Match status "Matched"; Settlement Restrictions, Maintenance, Realignment, Auto-collateralisation, Reimbursement auto-collateralisation instructions and Liquidity Transfers do not go through matching; Cancellation Instruction matching follows different rules; mandatory fields must match (with opposite values for Credit/Debit and Securities Movement Type, and an amount tolerance for DVP/PFOD); additional fields must match once either side fills them (blank cannot match filled); optional fields may match filled-against-blank; the tolerance has two currency bands by cash countervalue; where several candidates exist T2S picks the smallest amount difference, then the closest entry time, and the deliverer's amount becomes the Matched Settlement Amount; and unmatched instructions trigger allegement and, after a period, automatic cancellation [[t2s-matching]] — *T2S UDFS R2026.JUN, §1.6.1.2, PDF 267–271, Diagrams 55–57 and footnote 194; reviewed 13 September 2026; English body text, authoritative language not stated for this edition and translation status not independently established; approval: source identity checked, no independent whole-edition supervisory approval certification.*

Qualifications [[t2s-matching]]: the diagrams are a **functional matrix only — no production XML/XSD validation and no local interface certification**; and the diagram headers read **DVP/DWP** while the paragraph separately says **DVP/PFOD** — those labels must be retained as printed, not normalised into a schema mapping. Footnote 194 is operationally important: upper- and lower-case letters are treated as different when comparing values, so identifiers must not be case-normalised before comparison [[t2s-matching]].

**Documented requirement (context, R2026.JUN).** Matching is not settlement. The posting application process is what checks eligibility and available resources and then updates cash balance, securities position and limit headroom, "resulting in the irrevocability of the settlement" [[t2s-posting]] — *T2S UDFS R2026.JUN, §1.6.1.8.1 and the first overview paragraph, PDF 303–304; reviewed 13 September 2026; English body text, authoritative language not stated, translation status not independently established; approval: source identity checked, no independent whole-edition supervisory approval certification.* Any November-readiness test plan should therefore separate "matched" observations from "posted/settled" observations.

1. It is **a derivative produced by the library maintainer, not ECB text** — a textual comparison after normalisation.

3. **The comparison does not review the change requests actually delivered by R2026.NOV**; Milan lists 11 change requests and six defects in ON_28/2026 [[milan-r2026nov-plan]].

**Reasoned inference** (derived from the residual-difference list in [[november-text-comparison]] read against the two section texts [[t2s-matching]] [[november-matching-text]]): the 31 residual differences in the matching range are presentational, and fall into recognisable classes — section renumbering (1.6.1.2 → 3.6.1.2), caption style ("DIAGRAM 54 – MATCHING APPLICATION POCESS" → "Figure 54: Matching application pocess", including the same typographical error in both), removal of inline cross-reference page numbers ("section Instruction Cancellation 280" → "section Instruction Cancellation"), relocation of footnote 194 into a "Footnotes" block numbered 1, a line-break inside "Deliver/Receiver", movement of the Table 57 caption from above to below the table, and re-flowed parameter-table column headers.
