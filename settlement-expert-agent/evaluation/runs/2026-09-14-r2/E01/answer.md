# Issuer CSD versus investor CSD, and whether Monte Titoli can be both

## Direct answer

An **issuer CSD** is the CSD that serves the *securities issue itself* — it provides the notary / central maintenance core service for that issue. An **investor CSD** is a CSD that reaches an issue held elsewhere by *being a participant* in another CSD's settlement system, or by using a third party or intermediary that is such a participant. The roles are therefore attached to a securities issue and a relationship, not to an institution. **Yes — one CSD can hold both roles**, and the reviewed evidence supports this in two distinct senses: across different issues, and within a single T2S settlement where the same CSD is both the CSD of issuance and the CSD of a party to the instruction.

## 1. The two definitions

**Documented requirement.** The EU legal definitions are:

- *issuer CSD* — "a CSD which provides the core service referred to in point 1 or 2 of Section A of the Annex to Regulation (EU) No 909/2014 in relation to a securities issue" [[rts392-issuer-investor-csd]] Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/392 — English OJ text, Article 1 definitions (browser excerpt), Regulation 2017/392 Article 1(a)–(g), OJ L 65, 10.3.2017, browser excerpt; version OJ L 65, 10.3.2017; reviewed 2026-09-14; body language English, authoritative language "EU official languages", original or official-language text; source identity checked, no independent whole-edition supervisory approval certification.
- *investor CSD* — "a CSD that either is a participant in the securities settlement system operated by another CSD or that uses a third party or an intermediary that is a participant in the securities settlement system operated by another CSD in relation to a securities issue" [[rts392-issuer-investor-csd]] same document, Article 1(f), OJ L 65, 10.3.2017; reviewed 2026-09-14; original or official-language text, authoritative language "EU official languages".

Both definitions close with the words "in relation to a securities issue". That phrase is what makes the roles relational.

Qualifications that travel with this citation (bundle LIMITATION lines): the section is legal context, not local procedures and not Norwegian incorporation, and future amendments are separate; the capture is a *partial* browser capture of the original act, and the library's consolidated capture is language-mislabelled because the consolidated EN page returned German text on 14 September 2026; and expressly — "Roles depend on the security and relationship: one CSD can be issuer CSD for one issue and investor CSD for another." The bundle's excerpt also stops mid-way at point (g), so any definition after (f) is not in reviewed evidence.

**Explanation, not a documented requirement** (background wording only, to make the terms readable): "notary service" and "central maintenance" are the names usually given to the Section A points referenced above; the bundle does not print the text of those points, so I do not state their content as evidence.

## 2. How T2S uses the two roles — and the "both" case

**Documented requirement.** For the realignment process, T2S defines a CSD as:

| Role | Condition in the evidence | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Issuer CSD | "when it is the CSD in which the security has been issued and distributed on behalf of the Issuer" | Documented requirement |
| Investor CSD | "when it is the CSD of at least one party of the Settlement Instruction" | Documented requirement |
| **Both** | "when it is the CSD in which the security has been issued **and** the CSD of at least one party of the Settlement Instruction" | Documented requirement |

Citation for all three rows: [[t2s-realignment]] T2S User Detailed Functional Specifications R2026.JUN (UDFS), §1.6.1.10, concepts and reference-data requirements, PDF 373–376; version R2026.JUN; reviewed 2026-09-13; body language English, no authoritative language recorded, translation status "not independently established"; source identity checked, no independent whole-edition supervisory approval certification.

So the answer to "can it be both at the same time?" is documented twice over: **within one settlement instruction** (the T2S "or both" row above) and **across issues** (the RTS limitation line quoted in section 1).

**Documented requirement — the account mechanics behind the investor role.** To manage cross-CSD and external-CSD settlement, each investor CSD chooses between opening an omnibus account in the books of the issuer CSD to reflect its participants' holdings, or opening an omnibus account in the books of any other CSD already an investor CSD for the same financial instrument; in both cases the CSD where that omnibus account is opened is the "technical issuer" of the investor CSD for those securities. For a given ISIN an investor CSD may define several such investor-type links, with exactly one flagged "default" and the others "alternative"; alternative links can only be set up for T2S-in investor CSDs pointing to a T2S-in technical issuer CSD, and the issuer-type link (the issuer CSD setting a CSD link with itself as issuer) is always a default link and can never be alternative. Same citation [[t2s-realignment]] (§1.6.1.10, PDF 375–376; reviewed 2026-09-13; English body text, translation status "not independently established").

**Documented requirement.** Cross-CSD settlement in T2S is achieved with the simultaneous booking of cash and securities between participants of different CSDs; once instructions are matched (or validated, when already matched on arrival), T2S automatically generates the required realignment settlement instructions from the links held in reference data, with no further action by T2S actors, and T2S ensures that the generated realignment instructions and their business instructions settle on an **all-or-none** basis [[t2s-realignment]] (§1.6.1.10.1–1.6.1.10.2, PDF 373–375; reviewed 2026-09-13; English body text, "not independently established" translation status).

**Documented requirement — matched is not settled.** Generation of realignment instructions is not the ledger movement. The transfer happens in posting: the posting application process checks whether settlement can be achieved given eligibility and available resources, and when the check is satisfactory it updates the cash balance, securities position and limit headroom, "resulting in the irrevocability of the settlement" [[t2s-posting]] T2S User Detailed Functional Specifications R2026.JUN (UDFS), §1.6.1.8.1 and first overview paragraph, PDF 303–304; version R2026.JUN; reviewed 2026-09-13; body language English, no authoritative language recorded, translation status "not independently established"; source identity checked, no independent whole-edition supervisory approval certification.

Qualifications travelling with [[t2s-realignment]]: actual links, accounts and ISIN eligibility require verification; and the section does not establish atomicity of an arbitrary two-security swap and does not describe every action taking place outside T2S.

## 3. What the Milan rulebook says about these roles

**Documented requirement.** Where settlement instructions are to be settled between a participant in Monte Titoli (other than another CSD in T2S) and a participant in another CSD in T2S — cross-CSD — T2S automatically carries out the movements between the securities accounts of the participants involved, **of the Investor CSDs and of the Issuer CSD**. Monte Titoli does not envisage cross-CSD settlement on securities where the Issuer CSD is outside T2S, unless both investor CSDs have a link in place with another CSD in T2S so that realignment with the Issuer CSD outside T2S is not necessary [[milan-cross-csd-disclosure]] Regulations as of 26 January 2026, Articles 77–78 with footnote 8, PDF 54 (printed page 53); version 26 January 2026; reviewed 2026-09-14 (source reviewed 2026-09-13); English body text but **the Italian text is the authoritative version and prevails** (cover, PDF 1) — this is a translation excerpt and the authoritative language differs; source identity checked, no independent whole-edition supervisory approval certification.

Qualification travelling with that claim (bundle LIMITATION): Article 77(2) excludes cross-CSD settlement when the issuer CSD is outside T2S unless both investor CSDs hold a link avoiding realignment with it, and **actual links per ISIN are not certified** in this evidence.

**Reasoned inference** (derived from Articles 77–78 read with the role definitions in [[t2s-realignment]] and [[rts392-issuer-investor-csd]], not stated as such in any excerpt): Monte Titoli operates inside the issuer-CSD / investor-CSD framework as a matter of its own rulebook, so its position in any given settlement follows from the issue and the relationship rather than from a fixed institutional label. It is a plain consequence of the "or both" definition that for an Italian-issued security held partly for participants of other CSDs, the CSD of issuance and the CSD of a party can coincide.

**Unresolved requirement.** Whether Monte Titoli in fact holds the issuer-CSD role for any specific ISIN and the investor-CSD role for any other specific ISIN — and which links and omnibus accounts exist per ISIN — is **not in reviewed evidence**. The general rule that a CSD may hold both roles is documented; the concrete per-ISIN attribution is not. The sources that would establish it are the Monte Titoli / Euronext Securities Milan eligible-instruments and CSD-links reference data and the T2S reference-data configuration for the ISIN, neither of which is in this bundle.
