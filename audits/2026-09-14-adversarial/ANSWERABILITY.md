# Manual evidence-to-answer inspection

These are twelve deliberately difficult prompts and the answers the inspected excerpts can support. They are written and checked by the same assistant, using the 13 September 2026 snapshot unless explicitly stated otherwise. They are not outputs from a deployed answer model and are not scored as an independent benchmark.

Each named section is in [the reviewed index](../../retrieval/README.md). The original source locators and excerpts remain available in the export. Where a prompt combines scopes, the assistant must make multiple retrieval calls rather than silently broaden one result.

| Prompt | Faithful answer supported by the inspected evidence | Evidence / assessment |
|---|---|---|
| “T2S says matched. Can I confirm the client received the shares?” | No. Matching compares the instructions; posting separately checks eligibility and resources before updating balances. A match is not a settlement confirmation. | `t2s-matching`, `t2s-posting`, `milan-finality`; UDFS PDF 267–271 and 303–304; Milan Articles 69–72. Sufficient for this distinction. |
| “Once matched in Milan, cancellation is impossible, correct?” | That is too broad. The reviewed rules retain bilateral cancellation with both participants' consent or the specified mandate route; unilateral cancellation has a different boundary and exceptions. | `milan-finality`, Articles 70/72. Sufficient substantive conditions; Italian-authority warning needs separate registry/cover lookup because F03 drops it. |
| “An investor CSD creates another copy of the security, right?” | No. In the reviewed T2S realignment model, linked omnibus/account arrangements connect holdings across CSDs. Issuer/investor roles are determined for the security and transaction; one CSD can have both roles. | `t2s-realignment`, PDF 375–376. Supports the conceptual account chain, not certification of an actual ISIN or a complete general legal definition. |
| “Two ICP clients at linked T2S CSDs: give every bank-to-CSD and CSD-to-T2S XML message.” | The reviewed native overview identifies sese.023 instructions, sese.024 status, sese.025 confirmation and sese.032 realignment notification with June version suffixes. It does not establish each bank's local interface, recipients' subscriptions or full XML payloads. | `t2s-messages` plus realignment/posting dependencies, PDF 753/778. Partial answer only; production details require additional specifications. |
| “Matching a security-for-security swap makes both legs atomic, yes?” | No general conclusion is supported. The reviewed all-or-none relationship concerns business instructions and their generated realignment chain. An arbitrary two-security exchange needs its own supported linking structure and eligibility evidence. | `securities_swap_atomicity` correctly blocks. `t2s-realignment` explains the narrower mechanism. |
| “Monday trade, Wednesday ISD: matching must happen Tuesday and settlement starts Wednesday morning?” | There is no such fixed matching appointment in the inspected workflow. Matching follows availability of suitable instructions. The Wednesday T2S day may begin Tuesday evening after the preceding day completes; posting depends on ISD, eligibility and resources. | Requires matching/posting plus `t2s-schedule`. Supports a nominal example with applicable business-day assumptions; actual calendar-date queries must address F01. |
| “The September 8 EUR DvP deadline was 16:00, so the last trade definitely settled then?” | The reviewed notice announced an extension to 17:00. Neither that announcement nor the normal schedule proves an individual trade's execution time. The later 18:39 figure concerns IFOP, a different event. | `dated_t2s_schedule`, business date 2026-09-08, EUR. Sufficient via the dedicated route; equivalent timeline routing currently fails. |
| “T+1 was adopted, therefore all EU trades were already T+1 on September 13?” | No. The inspected current Article 5 contains the second-business-day ceiling for its defined trading-venue scope and exceptions. The adopted amendment applies from 11 October 2027 and contains its own exclusions. | Two explicit calls: current `csdr-5`, future `t1-law`. Sufficient bounded legal comparison; do not turn a ceiling into a compulsory cycle for every transaction. |
| “For FoP, currency can never affect matching; additional and optional fields are equivalent?” | Both propositions are too broad. The repaired figures distinguish mandatory, additional and optional matching. A supplied additional value normally requires a counterpart value, while one-sided optional values can match. The FoP cash-field qualification addresses the described non-T2S-currency CoSD arrangement. | `t2s-matching`, Diagrams 55–57, PDF 269–270. All 23 rows were visually compared again; functional rules do not certify schema paths. |
| “An ECB DCP certificate alone admits me to Copenhagen?” | No. The excerpt also requires the VP participation agreement, authorisation/setup and relevant successful tests; permissions can differ between markets. Exact service entitlements remain unverified. | `copenhagen-dcp`, Part 5 §§2–3. Sufficient for the certificate-versus-admission distinction; F04 causes an inconsistent block when the same snapshot date is repeated as business date. |
| “Oslo's substitute liquidity bank guarantees funding if the primary bank fails?” | The reviewed Section 10 expressly does not impose a duty to supply liquidity. It describes the substitute account arrangement and optional funding. The linked edition retains its supervisory-approval reservation and Norwegian-language authority. | `oslo-liquidity` plus `oslo-edition`, PDF 1/17–18. Sufficient as a qualified description, not unconditional current legal assurance. |
| “Porto's manual proves semt.07 is the full production schema identifier?” | No. Preserve the printed anomaly, identify the stated report families and obtain the governing current message specifications. Do not invent a corrected production identifier. | `porto-report-families`, operational manual PDF 66 and ISO15022 overview PDF 3. Sufficient to identify the limitation; complete schema answer is unsupported. |

Primary authorities for these comparisons are the saved [T2S June UDFS](../../sources/documents/aa3d5a3b94c9-T2S-User-Detailed-Functional-Specifications-R2026-JUN-UDFS.pdf) and the local/legal sources identified by each section's citations. If a filename differs in another installation, use the source register and content hash rather than guessing a replacement.

What this inspection establishes: the repaired excerpts can support useful, bounded explanations and refusals. What remains unproved: reliable natural-language routing, faithful generated answers across the wider 83-question backlog, and production-level operational completeness.
