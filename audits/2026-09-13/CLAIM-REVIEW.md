## A bounded cross-CSD explanation for the knowledge base

This is an illustration, not an executable settlement instruction. Assume one eligible ISIN, a direct valid link between two CSDs both participating in T2S, seller at the issuer CSD, buyer at the investor CSD, both banks indirectly connected through their CSDs, EUR DvP for 100 shares and EUR 1,000, and separate eligible cash accounts with sufficient resources. No external CSD, multi-issuance, alternative chain, hold or partial settlement is assumed. These assumptions must be checked for a real transaction. The investor CSD holds through an omnibus account at its technical issuer; more complex chains are possible. [T2S User Detailed Functional Specifications R2026.JUN (UDFS)](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/target/target-professional-use-documents-links/t2s/sdd/shared/pdf/T2S_UDFS_R2026.JUN_clean_20260122.en.pdf) — §1.6.1.10 PDF 375–376,385–387.

1. The banks or their agents send instructions through their local CSD interfaces. For this ICP scenario the CSDs submit native T2S instructions. DCP changes the technical message path; it does not automatically replace CSD membership or the cash-bank arrangement. [Instructions to Settlement Service and related instrumental activities - in force as of 30 June 2025](https://www.euronext.com/sites/default/files/2025-07/03%20Settlement%20Service%20Instructions_30062025_WITHOUT%20EVIDENCE.pdf) — §1.1 PDF 7–8; [INTERBOLSA Regulation 1/2016](https://www.euronext.com/sites/default/files/2023-07/regulamentoib.2016.01.consolidado.en_.pdf) — Article 2(4) PDF 2.
2. T2S validates and matches compatible instructions. Matching can precede the intended settlement date and does not establish the availability or transfer of cash/securities. For Milan, completed validation is SF1; matching is SF2 subject to the bilateral-cancellation qualification. [T2S User Detailed Functional Specifications R2026.JUN (UDFS)](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/target/target-professional-use-documents-links/t2s/sdd/shared/pdf/T2S_UDFS_R2026.JUN_clean_20260122.en.pdf) — PDF 267–271,303–304; [Regulations as of 26 January 2026](https://www.euronext.com/sites/default/files/2026-02/01%20REG_UNICO_ENG_WITHOUT%20EV_26012026.pdf) — Articles 69–72 PDF 50–51.
3. T2S determines the realignment chain from configured CSD/security/account links and generates the necessary instructions. These use the relevant omnibus, mirror and (where applicable) inter-CSD accounts; generation is not a completed ledger transfer. Business and generated realignment instructions settle all-or-none. [T2S User Detailed Functional Specifications R2026.JUN (UDFS)](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/target/target-professional-use-documents-links/t2s/sdd/shared/pdf/T2S_UDFS_R2026.JUN_clean_20260122.en.pdf) — PDF 373–376,385–387,753.
4. When due, eligible and adequately resourced, securities and cash are posted. As an explicitly labelled economic illustration, the seller’s securities holding decreases by 100, the investor CSD’s omnibus holding at the issuer increases by 100, and the buyer’s holding at the investor CSD increases by 100, with corresponding technical mirror entries maintaining the chain. The linked cash movement decreases the buyer-side eligible cash account by EUR 1,000 and increases the seller-side account by EUR 1,000; cash agents may own those accounts. These are layers of the same 100-share position, not a second issuance. Exact SAC/DCA identifiers and technical posting signs require the actual configured chain and diagrams; they were not certified here. [T2S User Detailed Functional Specifications R2026.JUN (UDFS)](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/target/target-professional-use-documents-links/t2s/sdd/shared/pdf/T2S_UDFS_R2026.JUN_clean_20260122.en.pdf) — §1.6.1.8 PDF 303–304; §1.6.1.10 PDF 375,385–387.
5. Native message families in the reviewed R2026.JUN overview include inbound sese.023.001.11; outbound sese.024.001.12 status, sese.025.001.11 settlement confirmation and sese.032.001.11 realignment notification. A realignment notification does not itself prove the trade settled; status and confirmation semantics matter. Local participant relays are service-dependent. [T2S User Detailed Functional Specifications R2026.JUN (UDFS)](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/target/target-professional-use-documents-links/t2s/sdd/shared/pdf/T2S_UDFS_R2026.JUN_clean_20260122.en.pdf) — PDF 753 and 778.

For a Monday trade with Wednesday ISD, assuming three applicable consecutive business days, the Wednesday T2S day can open Tuesday evening, no earlier than 18:45 after the previous day completes. The current baseline gives NTS at 20:00. Eligibility, holidays and operational incidents can change actual execution; a match on Monday is not Wednesday settlement. In Milan the reviewed Art 72 defines SF3 at the relevant cash debit, or securities debit for FoP. [T2S User Detailed Functional Specifications R2026.JUN (UDFS)](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/target/target-professional-use-documents-links/t2s/sdd/shared/pdf/T2S_UDFS_R2026.JUN_clean_20260122.en.pdf) — PDF 157,160,163,165,168; [Regulations as of 26 January 2026](https://www.euronext.com/sites/default/files/2026-02/01%20REG_UNICO_ENG_WITHOUT%20EV_26012026.pdf) — Article 72 PDF 51.

This single-ISIN DvP example does not establish atomic exchange of two securities. That question needs the proposed two-leg structure, supported linking conditions, both securities’ eligibility and applicable legal finality. The audit found no reviewed production specification supporting such a general claim.
