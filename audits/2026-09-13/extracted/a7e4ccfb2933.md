1.         Scope
1.1         These T2S – DCP Service Rules (the “DCP Rules”) being part 5 of the VP Rule
            Book apply to the DCP Services (as defined below.
1.2          In the event of any inconsistency between the DCP Rules and other parts of the
               Participation Agreement, the DCP Rules shall prevail.
1.3         Terms defined in Part 1 of the VP Rule book (Definitions)  have the same
            meaning when used in these DCP Rules, unless otherwise stated herein or the
              context otherwise requires.


2.          Authorisation and Certification
2.1        From the date VP decides to give the Participant access to the T2S system, VP
              authorises the Participant with direct access – i.e. a direct network connection
                - to the T2S platform, in respect of the securities related services, privileges
            and T2S functions as described in the User Guidelines (the “DCP Services”),
              unless otherwise agreed. Further, the Participant will be authorised to use the
            message-types and transaction types as specified in the form “Request for DCP-
             access to T2S”.
2.2         The Participant acknowledges that being a DCP via VP might differ in terms of
               available services and functions, and acts and tests to be conducted, compared
              to other markets.
2.3         The Participant must be in the possession of a valid T2S certification received
            from the Eurosystem. According to the framework agreement entered into
            between the Eurosystem and each CSD participating in T2S (the “Framework
             Agreement”), the T2S certificate aims to provide evidence that the adapted IT
              platform of a Participant does not harm the T2S system as the result of
              inappropriate technical communication or procedures. For that purpose, the
               Participant must perform such Eurosystem certification tests and other acts as
              required by the Eurosystem from time to time, cf. clause 4. The test scenarios
             are identical for all markets and are solely defined by the Eurosystem, but may
             vary depending on the connectivity channel used, the Participants plans to
             connect to multiple CSDs, etc.
2.4             It  is solely the  Participant’s responsibility to obtain and maintain a T2S
                certificate.
2.5        VP is obliged to set-up the access for the Participant in the T2S system in
             accordance  with  the  authorisation  described  in  this  section. A  further
              description of the set-up process, is contained in the User Guidelines.


3.          Technical and operational requirements, legal requirements, etc.
3.1         The Participant must have signed a Participation Agreement with VP on access
             as Settlement Participant to the central securities depositary services provided
            by VP, and will, in addition to these DCP Rules, be bound by the same terms as
            a Participant only acting as an ICP, unless these DCP Rules provides otherwise.
3.2         The Participant must connect to the T2S platform by using the technical
              connection, communication interface, etc. as set forth by the Eurosystem or VP
            from time to time.
3.3         The Participant must perform all such acts and tests as may be reasonably
             requested by VP or the Eurosystem from time to time. The tests must be
            performed successfully to a level satisfactory for VP or the Eurosystem, as the
             case might be.

3.4         The Participant might want to perform  its own tests, which could require
              assistance from VP. VP will assist the Participant in performing such tests on a
              best effort basis.


3.5          VP’s set up to the T2S platform has certain VP specific technical characteristics,
             which the Participant must comply with. The VP specific technical characteristics
             are further described in User Guidelines.
3.6         With the purpose to ensure that the IT platform of a Participant does not harm
             the T2S system as the result of inappropriate technical communication or
             procedures, and to ensure that the T2S system operates safely and efficiently,
           VP may request such  information, documents and assurances from the
               Participant, which VP may reasonable require from time to time.


4.          Instructions from the Eurosystem
4.1         The Eurosystem may interact directly with the Participant for the purposes of
             the T2S certification, managing the technical connections to the T2S platform,
                 crisis management, etc. In all such cases the Participant is obliged to comply
              with the instructions given by the Eurosystem, and to pay any and all costs
              associated therewith.


5.          Instructions from VP
5.1        VP has a right to monitor the Participant’s access to and use of the T2S system.
5.2         The Participant must adapt to any technical and operational change instruction
            from VP, in respect of a change, which either VP is obliged to implement
             according to the Framework Agreement, or VP, in its own discretion, finds
             reasonable or necessary to implement. To the extent possible VP will give the
               Participant due notice of such changes, though the Participant acknowledges
              that VP must reflect any notice given by the T2S system to VP and that under
               certain circumstances it may be with immediately effect, if it is necessary to
             ensure that the T2S system operates safely and efficiently.


6.          Instructions from the Participant
6.1        A Participant may only instruct via the direct access to T2S in respect of its T2S
             Accounts.


7.         Data from the Eurosystem
7.1         As a result of the direct connectivity to the T2S platform, the Participant will
              receive data directly from the T2S system.
7.2         The T2S system will deliver the data at such times, in such formats and through
             such communication channels as decided by the Eurosystem.
7.3         Any use  of the data by the  Participant  for a  particular purpose  is the
               Participant’s own risk.
7.4        VP is not responsible for any data provided by the T2S system, including any
             delay or failure by the T2S system to provide the data.
7.5         The Participant acknowledges that VP receives copies of the data sent between
             the T2S system and the Participant.

8.         Data from VP
8.1         The Participant will only receive data in respect of the DCP Services from the
           T2S system, and will not receive data from VP unless specified in these T2S
          DCP Service Rules, the User Guidelines, or otherwise agreed with VP.


9.         Data from the Participant
9.1         The Participant is solely responsible for the correctness and completeness of all
               instructions, information, etc. provided by it to the Eurosystem. This includes
              that the Participant is responsible for completing all relevant fields, data boxes,
               etc. as further described in the User Guidelines and T2S Guidelines, correctly.


10.         Technical problems
10.1            If  the  Participant  identifies an  error  or an  inconvenient matter  in  the
              connectivity to the T2S platform, the Participant must immediately notify VP.
10.2            If VP finds  it relevant, VP will enter into a good faith discussion with the
               Participant with a view to whether and how the error / inconvenient matter
             should be solved. VP may, however, in its own discretion, decide whether the
               error / inconvenient matter needs to be solved, and which actions need to be
            performed by VP and/or the Participant.


11.          Availability, Suspension and Technical Disconnection
11.1       VP takes no responsibility in respect of the availability of the T2S system for
             the Participant during the opening hours of T2S.
11.2        The Participant entitles the Eurosystem and/or VP (as the case might be) to
            suspend and technically disconnect the Participant from the T2S system. Such
             suspension or technically disconnection may happen with immediate effect if,
                in the Eurosystem’s and/or VP’s reasonable opinion, the technical connection
               of the Participant to the T2S system represents a threat to the security or
               integrity of the T2S system or VP’s system. The Participant agrees in this
              respect, that only to the extent possible, the Eurosystem and/or VP (as the
             case might be) will provide a reasonable prior notice of the imminent technical
              disconnection of the Participant.
11.3         According to the Framework Agreement, the Eurosystem is in certain situations
               entitled to suspend and technically disconnect VP from the T2S system with
            immediate effect. Suspension and disconnection (as the case might be) of VP
            might have an effect of the availability of the T2S system for the Participant.
            The Participant, however, acknowledges that VP shall have no responsibilities
             towards the Participant in this respect.


12.         Reliance
12.1        The Eurosystem shall be entitled to rely on any instructions or other kind of
              information provided by the Participant via the technical connection to the T2S
            system or in any other form or through any other communication channel, if
             the person providing such instruction / information is a person whom the
            Eurosystem reasonably believes to be acting with authority on the Participant’s
               behalf. It is solely the Participant’s responsibility to ensure that any of the
               Participant’s employees or other representatives that give instructions or
             otherwise communicate with the T2S system has the authority to do so.

13.         Right to disclose
13.1        The Participant authorises VP, on the Participant’s behalf and at the discretion
               of the Participant, to contact and disclose any relevant information about the
               Participant related to the DCP Services, (i) to the Eurosystem, if such contact
              or disclosure is in accordance with the scope of the Framework Agreement, and
                    (ii) to any governmental or other regulatory authority or court or other
              authority of competent jurisdiction, if such contact or disclosure is required by
              law.


14.          Liability Rules
14.1         VP’s liability under these T2S DCP Service Rules is subject to the liability rules
             contained in Part 2 of the VP Rule Book (General Terms and Conditions). The
                  liability rules shall, however, be modified in accordance with the terms set out
                in these T2S DCP Service Rules.
14.2        The Participant shall be liable for any loss, expense or claim, which is a result
               of an  incorrect incomplete information,  instruction,  etc. provided by the
               Participant or a failure by the Participant to provide information to the T2S
            system or VP in a timely manner.
14.3       VP shall in no event be liable to the Participant for any loss which is caused by
            an act or omission made by the T2S system. However, if the Participant has
              incurred a loss due to an act or omission made by the T2S system, which the
            Eurosystem is liable to VP for, VP shall claim the loss against the Eurosystem,
            and shall pass on any proceeds received in this respect to the Participant.


15.         Indemnification
15.1        The Participant shall reimburse, indemnify and hold harmless VP, for losses
             which relates to the DCP Services provided to the Participant, unless such loss
           was caused directly by VP’s wilful act or gross negligence.


16.         Costs
16.1       VP charges fees for the DCP Services provided to the Participant. All fees are
             charged in accordance with the terms contained in Part 2 (General Terms and
              Conditions).


17.         VP’s termination and suspension right
17.1         In addition to the termination and breach terms contained in Part 2 of the VP
             Rule Book (General Terms and Conditions), but subject to the rules contained
                in Part 4 of the VP Rules Book (Settlement Rules) on settlement in the event of
             Insolvency Proceedings, if any of the following events occur in respect of the
               Participant, VP has a right (but not an obligation) to suspend or terminate the
          DCP Services with immediate effect by given notice to the Participant:
               A.   The Participant does not maintain the T2S certificate described in clause 2;
               B.   The Participant does not successfully perform such acts and tests as
                    described in clause 3.3.
               C.  A material or repeated breach under these T2S DCP Service Rules is caused
                  by the Participant, unless such breach is remedied (provided that  it is
                    capable of being remedied) no later than five (5) VP Business Days after
                 VP has given notice to the Participant in respect of the breach.

17.2       VP may terminate the DCP Services with six (6) months prior written notice to
             the Participant.
17.3         Termination  of  the  business  relationship  with  the  Participants  technical
              providers (e.g. SWIFT, SIA/COLT, etc.) due to a termination of the DCP
              Services  is solely the responsibility of the Participant, and the Participant
            acknowledges that VP shall have no responsibilities towards the Participant in
                this respect.


18.         Participant’s termination right
18.1         In addition to the termination and breach terms contained in Part 2 of the VP
             Rule Book (General Terms and Conditions), but subject to the rules contained
                in Part 4 of the VP Rules Book (Settlement Rules) on settlement in the event of
             Insolvency Proceedings, if any of the following events occur in respect of the
             VP, the Participant has a right (but not an obligation) to terminate the DCP
              Services with immediate effect by given notice to VP:
               A.  A material or repeated breach under these T2S DCP Service Rules is caused
                  by VP, unless such breach is remedied (provided that it is capable of being
                   remedied) no later than five (5) VP Business Days after the Participant has
                    given notice to VP in respect of the breach.


18.2        The Participant may terminate the DCP Services with six (6) months written
              notice to VP.


19.        Automatic Termination
19.1        The DCP Services automatically terminates as per the date VP’s participation in
             the T2S system might cease.
