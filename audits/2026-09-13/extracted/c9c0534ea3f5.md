1.3.9  MERGER / DEMERGER


A merger is used where a company acquires another company. The shareholders of the acquired
company are allocated the correct number of shares in the acquiring company. Holdings in the
acquired company are simultaneously redeemed. Pre-merger information on the acquired company
remains on file at Euronext Securities. A demerger is utilized where a company is divided into two or
more companies. The company’s shareholders are allotted shares in the new company in addition to
the shares they already own.
