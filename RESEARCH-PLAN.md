# Research plan

As-of date: 11 September 2026. Scope: public official materials for all Euronext Securities CSDs and their EU/EEA regulatory and settlement infrastructure context.

1. Map the group, five local CSDs, canonical documentation hubs, and current change programmes.
2. Follow official document links; retain source URLs, retrieval results, dates, versions, and local originals. Distinguish current, future, historical, and uncertain applicability.
3. Review core rulebooks and operational material for CSD functions, participant requirements, settlement, asset servicing, risk, connectivity, and fees.
4. Add primary regulatory sources: CSDR and its amendments/technical standards, ESMA, ECB/T2S, DORA, settlement finality, T+1, and DLT where relevant.
5. Produce a prioritised reading guide, structured source catalogue, sourced synthesis, and explicit coverage gaps for building a knowledge base.

This is a research and document acquisition project. Downloading or extracting a document does not imply a full substantive or visual review. Public source completeness and document review depth are tracked separately.

## Completion

All five steps completed for the defined public-source snapshot. The source catalogue, curated reading guide, functioning synthesis, role-specific requirements map, currentness/conflict register, ingestion guidance and 21-source starter ZIP are written. File integrity and linked local paths were checked; no failures were found. Full substantive review, client-only documents and production RAG deployment remain outside the completed scope.
