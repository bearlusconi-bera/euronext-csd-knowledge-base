Dear Client

We wish to inform you that the production release of T2S Release R2026.JUN has been confirmed
and will take place on 12 June, effective from 15 June 2026.

Euronext Securities Milan has successfully completed testing of all defects and change requests
applicable to its operational model in the T2S UTEST test environment and the ES-MIL TE
environment.



Production release plan

The deployment of release R2026.JUN will follow the so-called “option 2” defined in the MOP,
version R2025.JUN, chapter 5.2.2.

The reopening of ES-MIL services is scheduled for Saturday 13 June and is subject to confirmation
from the Operations Support team.

The Operations Support team will also provide email updates regarding the achievement of the
most significant stages of the release process:

• Friday 12/06 at approximately 20:00, confirming the start of release procedures and the closure
of inbound and outbound communication channels to/from T2S (A2A and U2A)

• Saturday 13/06 at approximately 16:00, notification of the reopening of A2A - U2A
communication channels and execution of an initial RTS cycle
