We would also like to inform you that Euronext Securities Milan will switch to "future mode" on
05/10/2026. Further information regarding the testing schedule will follow.




Some detailed information also regarding CA4U:

Until 11 September, the collections to be considered for CA4U testing will remain those from
the SR2025 version, as is currently the case. From 12 September onwards, they will be
replaced by the SR2026 version, which will then become the official reference source (golden
source) to be used in future.

In addition, there will be a freeze period for corrections to the collections from 24 August to
11 September, in order to allow for migration activities from the SR2025 version to the SR2026
version.
