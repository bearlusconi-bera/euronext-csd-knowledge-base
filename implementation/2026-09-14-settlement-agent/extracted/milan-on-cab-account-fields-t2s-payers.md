Introduction of CAB and Account
Number fields for T2S payers

   To the attention of:                           Issuers, Service Providers, Intermediaries


   Priority:                                      Medium


   Topic:                                         Availability of CAB and Account Number fields
                                                   in MTX instructions for T2S payers


Dear Clients,

we would like to inform you that Euronext Securities Milan has introduced the possibility for
T2S payers to populate the CAB and Account Number fields in MTX instructions, in line with
the functionality already available for T2 payers.

Please note that these fields are optional for issuers, although they provide significant benefits
to intermediaries by supporting reconciliation activities and the correct routing of payments.

The functionality will be available in production as of Monday, 9 February 2026.
