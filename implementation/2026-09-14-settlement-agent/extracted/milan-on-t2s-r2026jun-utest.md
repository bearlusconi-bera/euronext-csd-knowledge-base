Dear Client,

The following is a statement from the European Central Bank regarding T2S Release R2026.JUN:



Test plan

Please note that the upcoming R2026.JUN release of T2S will be available in external test
environment (UTEST) on 30 March 2026.

The release consists of 6 change requests and the resolution of 59 defects.

Release R2026.JUN of T2S will be deployed into production on 12 June, effective as of 15 June
2026.

External testing by participants using ES-MIL systems may be performed between Monday 30
March and Wednesday 27 May 2026.

DCPs are invited to re-test all defects and change requests applicable to their own operating model,
indicating in the relevant “R2026.JUN PBI and CRs content incl declaration to test v0.1.xls” form
whether they wish to proceed with re-testing, and reporting the progress of activities on a weekly
basis to the address MT-T2S-test@euronext.com.

Test schedule

ES-MIL tested all the defects and change requests applicable to its operating model in the EAC test
environment reserved for CSDs and will re-run the tests as of Monday 30 March in the UTEST
environment.

In accordance with the plan produced by the European Central Bank, DCPs are requested to send
their final feedback on the outcome of the CR and PBI tests by 10:00 on 26 May 2026 to the email
address: MT-T2S-test@euronext.com. This feedback will be included in the report sent to the T2S
decision-making bodies on that day.

Testing activities may continue after that date until 15 June 2026. Any additional evidence will be
reported on an ongoing basis to the above-mentioned decision-making bodies for the purpose of
verifying the stability of the release in question.
