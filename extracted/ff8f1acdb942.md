Collateral Accounts
We offer a secure and customised tri-party solution.
With a collateral account, you will avoid the risks, difficulties and expenses regarding collateral for financial transactions. We offer you a secure and customised tri-party solution whereby the management of operational and market risks is outsourced to us.
We ensure that the transactions initiated by either party comply with the tri-party agreement and we also calculate the value of the collateral. This releases management resources and minimises the risk for both borrower and lender.
