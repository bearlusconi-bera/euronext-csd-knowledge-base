1. GENERAL PRINCIPLES



▪   Application Services fees relate to Charges for the network configuration and management of
    unicast and multicast services.
▪   Clients should review the documentation covering the Connectivity Services offering to ensure
   the necessary requirements are understood.
▪   Clients are charged for Application Service fees based on the services to which they subscribe.
▪   Application Services fees are applied to Members via WAN, Service Providers via WAN and
   Services Providers via Service Providers.
▪  Fees for Market Data Application Services (multicast and unicast) are per service and per Port,
    in the case of CMC Services, or per service and per circuit, in the case of EMC Services. In the
   case of a Service Provider behind a Service Provider, Market Data Application Services fees are
    applicable per Client Site.
▪  Trading/DropCopy/RiskGuard Application Services (unicast) fees, applicable to Members, are per
    Client Site.
▪   Unicast Participant access fee for the Order Entry Gateway (Trading, DropCopy and RiskGuard)
     is applicable to Service Providers per connected End User, regardless of the connected Service
   Provider Sites.
▪   Clearing Application Services (unicast) fees are per Client Site and based on the role of the End
   User with regards to Clearing Services (General Clearing Member, Individual Clearing Member
   and Non-Clearing Member).
▪  Euronext Securities Application Services (unicast) fees are per Client Site and based on the
   number of Euronext Securities CSDs to which the client has access. Clients interested in accessing
   any Euronext Securities services via CMC or EMC are invited to contact the relevant CSD directly
    in advance to determine if such Euronext Securities services are available via CMC and EMC, and
    to enter with the relevant CSD into the respective Other Euronext Agreement to obtain the
   Euronext Securities service.
▪   In the case that Clients are connected to the DR Data Centre, no Application Service fees are
   due on that Access Port/circuit.
▪   Billing will be automatic, regardless of whether the Connectivity Services are used by the Client
    or not. Full payment is due for every calendar month.
▪  Euronext may at its sole discretion allow its Clients to subscribe on a non-discriminatory basis to
   temporary validation tests or comparable undertakings regarding new or ancillary Connectivity
    Services, which may give rise to different Charges as set forth herein.
▪   Detailed Connectivity Pricing is described in this Price List.
▪  Charges set out herein that are not amended by means of this updated Price List may be
   amended by Euronext at a later stage in 2026.
▪   Prices are indicated in EUR and are excluding any applicable Taxes (including VAT).

2. APPLICATION SERVICES FEES FOR
  MEMBER



A “Member” is an entity that has been approved as such by Euronext under the Rules and is
authorised to access the matching engine subject to its own risk management processes. Any entity
that trades via a broker or a clearing firm will, for pricing purposes only, be considered a “Member”
hereunder.

It is understood that for Members receiving MDG Lite only, the fees for up to 100 Mbps
bandwidth are applicable, as described in section 2.1 below, “Market Data Application
Services fees for Members”.


An “ISV (Independent Software Vendor)”  is an entity that has been approved as such by
Euronext under the Rules. ISVs are authorised to access the EUA environment for development
purposes only. As a consequence, no ASFs are due for Clients qualifying (only) as an ISV.

3. APPLICATION SERVICE FEES FOR
   SERVICE PROVIDER


A “Service Provider” means a Client providing Application Service Provider (ASP) Services or
Extranet Service Provider (ESP) Services. Any (re)distributor of Data authorised by Euronext or any
of its Affiliates to do so under a Data Licence will, for pricing purposes only, qualify as a Service
Provider hereunder.

Please note that clients wishing to be approved by Euronext as an accredited Service
Provider, which can offer physical access to the system through their own infrastructure
and network, are required to complete the Service Provider’s application questionnaire and
conclude a Service Provider Accreditation Agreement.

The Service Provider’s response, once completed and submitted to Euronext by the Client, will allow
Euronext to make a fully informed assessment of the Client’s proposed SP Services and determine
if the Client and the SP Services are fit for purpose. For more information, please refer to the Service
Provider Accreditation Agreement.

It is understood that Cash or Derivatives Market Data Application Services fees are
applicable also to Service Providers receiving MDG Lite only.
