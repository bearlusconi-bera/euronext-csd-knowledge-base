Information for Account operator
How to become a client
As a Norwegian Central Securities Depository (CSD), Euronext Securities Oslo (ES-OSL) offers an entire range of services regarding securities such as issuance, safekeeping, tax services and data services. And of course we provide our core activity of operating a book-entry system and a securities settlement system.
The Norwegian Act on Central Securities Depository and ES-OSL Rules for Registration of Financial Instruments and VPO NOK Rules regulates which companies are able to join our securities settlement system as an Account Operator and Settlement Participant.
If you want to become a client at ES-OSL, you must complete and e-mail us an application for participation as further described in the following appendix:
“Application for participation”.
When ES-OSL have received a completed application, our internal approval procedure begins. You can expect an answer to your request within a maximum of 30 days.
As soon as your request has been approved, we will forward the Participation Agreement and we can begin the process for onboarding.
You can contact us at clientrelationsandsales@euronext.com if you want further information about becoming a client.
Segregation of securities
ES-OSL must offer an account structure that makes it possible to separate securities belonging to one participant from securities belonging to other participants, and segregate securities belonging to the participants’ clients. ES-OSL operates an end-investor account model by which we offer all levels of segregation.
ES-OSL shall publicly disclose the levels of protection and the costs associated with the different levels of segregation that we provide. You can read more about the different levels of segregation below.
