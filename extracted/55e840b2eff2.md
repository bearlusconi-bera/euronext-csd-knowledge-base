Cross Market Documents
Euronext Securities Milan (ES-MIL) has established links with several foreign CSDs, which operate in T2S and out of T2S, to allow clients to move securities between their ES-MIL’s account and an account on a different CSD.
The T2S Gateway - ES-MIL Settlement Manual provides information to manage such transfers.
