Processing of Personal Data
The purpose of this Policy is to describe how VP Securities A/S (Euronext Securities Copenhagen) (hereinafter called “VP”, “we” or “us”) process the personal data that we as data controllers receive about investors, our customers and our business partners. VP is fully owned by Euronext N.V and is part of the Euronext Group (the Group).
PRIVACY STATEMENT
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE OF THE STATEMENT
Euronext Securities Copenhagen (“VP, “ES-CPH”, “we”, “us”) is strongly committed to protecting your personal data and upholding your right to privacy. This Privacy Statement describes how we collect and process your personal data and provides an overview of your rights in relation to your personal data. It applies to information which you may provide directly to us or via third parties.
Euronext Securities Copenhagen is part of the Euronext Group (“Euronext”) which consists of Euronext N.V, a Dutch holding company, located at Beursplein 5, 1012 JW, Amsterdam, the Netherlands, and its affiliated companies.
2. APPLICATION OF THIS STATEMENT
Euronext Securities Copenhagen holds a licence to operate as a central securities depository. As a central securities depository, we keep records of holders of the securities (for instance shares, bonds and investment fund units) issued by us, and we settle (i.e. transfer from one securities account to another) securities transactions.
Euronext Securities Copenhagen may assume different privacy roles depending on the activity.
Euronext Securities Copenhagen as a Data Controller
Euronext Securities Copenhagen is the data controller for the personal data that we receive in our capacity as a central securities depository and some of our ancillary services and as a company with many business partners.
Euronext Securities Copenhagen is the data controller for personal data collected through our website, including when you browse, communicate with us, or engage with our services. As the Data Controller, Euronext Securities Copenhagen determines the purposes and means of processing such personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and the Danish Data Protection Act.
Euronext Securities Copenhagen as a Data Processor
In some cases, Euronext Securities Copenhagen may act as a Data Processor. This occurs when we process personal data on behalf of our customers or partners in accordance with contractual agreements. In these situations, the customer or partner is the Data Controller, meaning they determine the purposes and means of processing the data. Euronext Securities Copenhagen processes the data strictly in accordance with their instructions and the applicable data processing agreements signed between the parties.
This Privacy Statement applies to the way we collect and process your personal data. Personal data will be collected and processed by us when you or a company with which you are connected engage with us and/or avail of our services, e.g.:
via our websites or other selected domains, including any linked pages owned and operated by Euronext or any successors thereof, or any application made available by Euronext for use on a tablet, mobile phone or other mobile device (individually and collectively, the " Euronext websites"),
in the framework of the execution of a contract with a company with which you are connected (e.g. when we have a contract with your employer and the latter provides us your contact data in the execution of that agreement)
by participating to one of our events
We will collect personal data:
from you: for example through Euronext websites, when you avail of our products and services, from your securities market activity via your chosen financial institute when you attend one of our events or an event hosted at our premises, when you correspond with us, and
from third party sources such as but not limited to Euronext Securities Copenhagen vendors, Euronext Securities Copenhagen providers, and/or from publicly available resources (e.g. registration offices, newspapers and other publications).

3. THE PERSONAL DATA WE PROCESS
Personal data is information or assessments that can be linked to you as a person. The personal data we may process, which we may collect either directly from you or a company with which you are connected or from a third party, may include some or all of the following depending on the nature of the processing activity.
We do not process sensitive personal data such as health details.
DEVICE INFORMATION
We collect information about your computer/device browser via cookies used on Euronext websites. You can read more about our use of cookies in Section 4.
CONTACT AND COMPANY INFORMATION
Information that enables us to have a dialogue with you, such as your address, phone number, and email address.
IDENTITY INFORMATION
Information about your identity, such as name, social security number, citizenship. Proof of identity documentation, for example passport or driver’s licence, including photo.
AML INFORMATION
In certain cases, Euronext Securities Copenhagen processes personal data about individuals, who are related to existing or prospective customers to comply with current anti-money laundering legislation. This may include your position as a director, officer or beneficial owner of a company, copy documentation confirming your identity (such as your passport or identity card) and copy documentation showing proof of your address and information collected through AML software and screening tools. We may process publicly available sensitive personal data, for example about politically exposed persons.
INFORMATION COLLECTED IN CONNECTION WITH LISTING ON AN EURONEXT MARKET
This may include information about the issuer of the financial instruments, the board of directors, the management and the shareholders, collected from the documentation filed by the company or from other sources, such as information about criminal offences and convictions, as defined by the listing rules of the marketplace.
MAR INFORMATION
Euronext Securities Copenhagen assists specific persons working in complying with the EU Market Abuse Regulation.
This may include general personal data about who you are such as your name, your contact details, your job title, identification number, position in a company and reason for being an insider as defined by law.
MIFID II INFORMATION
This includes all personal data this regulation requires Euronext to process.
MIFIR INFORMATION
This includes all personal data this regulation requires Euronext to process.
CRM INFORMATION
This may include your correspondence and communications with us and details of your connections with our staff and of your visits to our premises.
SRDII INFORMATION
This includes all personal data that this regulation requires Euronext Securities Copenhagen to process in line with the SRDII.
PUBLICLY AVAILABLE INFORMATION
This may include information available: (i) on the websites or promotional material of companies or organizations with which you are connected; (ii) in news articles; or (iii) from registries such as the companies registration offices.
MARKET SURVEILLANCE DATA
In connection with trading of financial instruments, we may collect your personal data from issuers, members, securities registers and public authorities in order to fulfil our legal duties to monitor market activity.
INFORMATION IN CONNECTION WITH EURONEXT MEETINGS AND/OR EVENTS
We may collect specific information about you so that we may host you at our premises or at any event or seminar which we organize. We may also collect images (such as photographs and video footage) at IPO announcements and other Euronext events and CCTV footage when you visit our premises.
OTHER INFORMATION
We may also process any personal data which you or the company with which you are connected choose to provide us.

7. TRANSFER OF YOUR PERSONAL DATA
We store and process your personal data on servers located within the European Economic Area (the "EEA"). We only transfer your personal data outside the EEA where the European Commission has decided that the third country in question ensures an adequate level of protection in line with EEA data protection standards or where there are appropriate safeguards in place to protect your personal data.
We may transfer the personal information we collect about you to recipients in countries other than the country in which the information originally was collected. Those countries may not have the same data protection laws as the country in which you initially provided the information. When we transfer your information to recipients in other countries (such as the U.S.), we will protect that information as described in this Privacy Policy and will comply with applicable legal requirements providing adequate protection for the transfer of personal information to recipients in countries other than the one in which you provided the information.
If you are located in the European Union (and Iceland, Liechtenstein and Norway) (“EU”), United Kingdom (“UK”) or Switzerland, with respect to transfers of personal information to the U.S., Euronext US Inc. and its subsidiaries Euronext FX Inc., Euronext Markets Americas LLC, and Euronext Market Services LLC are certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework administered by the U.S. Department of Commerce, the European Commission, the UK Information Commissioner’s Office and the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, respectively, regarding the transfer of personal information from the EU, UK and Switzerland to the U.S. Click here to view our Data Privacy Framework Privacy Policy.
8. RETENTION OF YOUR PERSONAL DATA
Euronext Securities Copenhagen retains personal data as long as necessary and reasonable to fulfil the purpose for which the data are collected and processed.
Generally, as a central security depository, we shall retain your personal data for a period of ten years after the closure of your securities account. We store other personal data for five years in accordance with anti-money laundering legislation and the Danish Bookkeeping Act.
However to determine the appropriate retention period for personal data, we consider the amount, nature and sensitivity of the personal data, the potential risk of harm from unauthorized use or disclosure of your personal data, the purposes for which we process your personal data and whether we can achieve those purposes through other means and the applicable legal requirements.
9. SECURITY OF YOUR PERSONAL DATA
Your personal data are dealt with in confidence and Euronext Securities Copenhagen has taken appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against loss, misuse, alteration and/or destruction. These include, but are not limited to:
Installing a firewall to prevent unauthorized access to our systems;
Using secure servers;
Ensuring the Personal Data are stored in physically secure accommodation.
Please be aware that the security measures mentioned above apply to systems under the control of Euronext, but that Euronext cannot ensure the security nor confidentiality of any information during its transmission when transmitted via means not under its control, notably the Internet, and that you are aware that data transmitted via such means may be intercepted by third parties.
If Euronext Securities Copenhagen becomes aware of any security breach which results in unauthorized access of your personal data, Euronext Securities Copenhagen will endeavour to inform you.
Euronext websites may provide hyperlinks to websites from other companies or persons. This policy does not apply to the use of such websites, and we are not responsible or liable for any processing of your personal data via these third party websites.
In addition, if you share content from the Euronext websites via social media, your personal data will be visible to those visiting your personal pages on these social media websites. Euronext Securities Copenhagen is not responsible or liable for any processing of your personal data via such media. This policy does not apply to the use of such social media.

Such Personal Data will be retained in accordance with our Data Privacy Notice and Retention Policy (which will be available to you when you commence employment).
Where you are an unsuccessful job applicant, we will retain your Personal Data for a period of up to 12 months after it is determined that your application has been unsuccessful for the purposes of both contacting you in relation to future vacancies within Euronext which we think may be of interest to you and, for the purpose of defending any potential employment equality claims.
Please note that in certain circumstances, we may hold your data for a longer period, for example, if we are processing an ongoing claim or believe in good faith that the law or a relevant regulator may reasonably in our view expect or require us to preserve your Personal Data.
