Securities Transfer
For the purpose of physical settlement of operations as well as for the mere transfer of securities, Euronext Securities Porto allows Financial Intermediaries and other similar Entities the following securities movements:
Within the same account for Blocking, Reservation, Earmarking of securities
Between accounts of the same Financial Intermediary of the same holder and for internal regularization
Between accounts of different Financial Intermediaries of different holders
.
For further Information, refer to Settlement Systems – Real-Time Settlement System.
