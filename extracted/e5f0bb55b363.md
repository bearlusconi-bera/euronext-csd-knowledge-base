Strategic projects
Euronext Securities is shaping the future of post-trade in Europe by enhancing integration, connectivity and innovation. With our key initiatives, European Offering and The Convergence Programme, we are redefining the role of a CSD, creating a more seamless, efficient and future-proof financial ecosystem.
Building the future of capital markets
European Offering
Expanding Market Access
Euronext Securities' European Offering is broadening market access by centralising settlement and safekeeping for equities and ETF beyond our current scope. By aligning operational models and increasing accessibility, we are building a more connected and liquid marketplace for issuers, intermediaries, and investors.

Convergence project
Unified client experience
The Euronext Securities CSD Convergence Programme will deliver a unified client experience, leveraging fully harmonised services supported by a state-of-the-art common platform across the four Euronext Securities CSDs (Copenhagen, Milan, Oslo and Porto).
To find out more information regarding our Convergence Project, please use the links below: Convergence News article
Convergence Programme
Corporate Events services
Corporate Events services is part of our Convergence programme. Aligned with the European Central Bank's AMI-SeCo standards, this initiative ensures consistency for Corporate Events services for all asset classes across our CSDs while simplifying processes for our clients.
