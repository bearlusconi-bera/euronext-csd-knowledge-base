1. Executive summary


As the Issuer or Investor Central Securities Depository (CSD) for Belgian equities, Euronext
Securities Milan provides services related to Belgian registered securities. Belgian issuers
may issue shares in two different forms:

    •  Dematerialised Securities

    •  Registered Securities

The choice of security form is determined by the investor unless the Issuer has decided to
issue securities only in registered form.

Euronext Securities Milan provides tailored services for Issuers, including a service that
allows Issuers to outsource the management of their share register.

Additionally, Euronext Securities Milan facilitates for Issuers and Investors the conversion
between dematerialised and registered securities, offering a comprehensive service for
managing Belgian registered securities.

Note this document does not cover any aspect related to Tax, as tax services are described
in other documents.

2. Context


In accordance with the Belgian dematerialisation legal framework, as from 1 January 2008,
Belgian Issuers are no longer allowed to issue or maintain securities in bearer form.

However, Issuers under Belgian law can issue securities as either registered securities or
dematerialised securities. The Investors can then choose in which form they want to hold
the securities unless the Issuer has decided to issue securities only in registered form.

For registered securities, the Issuer must maintain a dedicated share register, whereas
dematerialised securities are maintained in the books of the Issuer CSD. The Issuer may
choose to outsource the share register to a third party, such as the CSD by signing an
agreement with the CSD.

This document covers:

    •  The Share Register Service (SRS) offered by Euronext Securities Milan for
       Belgian registered securities.

    •  The  management  of  the  conversion  of  registered  securities  into
       dematerialised securities and vice versa.

Please note that the Share Register Service (SRS)  is only available when Euronext
Securities Milan is acting as an Issuer CSD. The conversion of securities service is available

       7.1.1.   Maintenance of the share register

If issued in registered form, Belgian equities are represented solely by entries in the share
register maintained by the Issuer or a delegated third party, such as a Central Securities
Depository (CSD).

On the request of the Issuer, Euronext Securities Milan can provide this service, as a
delegated party in accordance with Belgian law.

As part of this service, Euronext Securities Milan maintains the register containing detailed
information about each shareholder, including:

    •  name and address of each shareholder (for natural persons), or name, registered
        office, and registration number (for legal entities)

    •  number and class of shares held by each shareholder

    •  amounts paid up on each share

    •  any restrictions on the transferability of shares foreseen in the articles of association
        or, upon request of one of the parties, foreseen in any agreements or the issuance
       conditions

    •    all share transfers and the dates thereof

    •  the voting rights, and rights to profits and to liquidation proceeds (if these differ
      from the profit rights), attached to each share.

The share register serves as the primary record of ownership for registered shares, and all
changes in ownership or rights must be promptly and accurately reflected in the register
(see transaction on register section).

   7.2. Updating the share register


As part of its role in maintaining the register, Euronext Securities Milan also processes
updates to the share register. Four types of operation result in a need to update the share
register:

    •  Trade and Transfer Operations: Purchases and sales of registered shares are
      processed  through   financial   intermediaries,  who  submit  the  necessary
      documentation to the CSD for register updates. The next section will focus on the
       trade flow for registered securities. For dematerialised shares, transfers occur via
       book-entry movements between securities accounts. The register is amended to
        reflect changes in ownership, and confirmations are sent to relevant parties. Off-
      exchange  transfers, such  as  gifts  or  inheritances,  require  supporting  legal
      documentation and result in corresponding register updates.

    •  Shareholder Security Register Maintenance: The CSD, acting on behalf of the
       Issuer under an outsourcing agreement, maintains the register for all registered
       shares. This includes recording new shareholders, updating ownership following
       trades, and reflecting changes due to events such as pledges, inheritances, or
      changes in shareholder details (e.g., name, address, bank account). The register
      must contain all legally required information, including shareholder identity, share
       quantities,  class, paid-up amounts,  transfer  restrictions,  transfer  dates, and
       associated rights.

       7.2.1.   Stock exchange trades

              7.2.1.1.1.   Buy trade processing

The purchase of shares on the stock exchange is processed through a financial institution
chosen by the investor (credit institution or brokerage firm). Any new shareholder will be
created in the Register based on the buying power completed by the financial institution.

Purchase of shares

The order to purchase the shares (buy order) is placed on the stock exchange by the
financial  intermediary. The  latter sends  the completed and  signed SRS Template
electronically directly to the CSD in charge of maintaining the register. The Issuer CSD will
confirm the registration of the purchase to the financial intermediary.

The various cases are described in the following sections.

   8.1. Scope of conversion services


Holders of dematerialised securities can request conversion to registered securities and
vice versa, resulting in a complete transformation of the security's legal nature. There are
no specific overall legal requirements for the communication method for such requests
defined  in the Belgian dematerialisation legal framework, as from 1 January 2008.
However, based on the Article of Association, Issuers can specify requirements and publish
procedures on their websites.

Euronext Securities Milan can serve as either the place of issuance (Issuer CSD) or the
place of safekeeping (Investor CSD), with Euroclear Belgium as the Issuer CSD. The
scenarios based on these roles are:

    •  Conversion from registered to dematerialised securities when Euronext
      Securities Milan is Issuer CSD:

         o  Shareholder via the Issuer (Agent) or a Euronext Securities Milan participant
            sends a request to Euronext Securities Milan to deregister, and a CSD
               participant instruction is sent to credit the dematerialised securities.
