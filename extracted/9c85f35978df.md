1. Purpose & objectives of testing

1.1 Context


The European Offering is Euronext’s initiative to extend its post-trade services beyond domestic markets,
enabling issuers and market participants to access a harmonized, pan-European CSD offering. This initiative
introduces new operational, legal, and technical capabilities that support cross-border issuance, settlement,
and related post-trade services, while aligning with applicable market practices and regulatory requirements.


As part of this evolution, changes are introduced across systems, processes, and participant interactions. A
structured and coordinated testing approach is therefore required to ensure that these changes can be
adopted by market participants in a controlled manner, without disruption to live operations.

1.2 Purpose of this document


This document defines the high-level approach under which the European Offering will be tested and validated
with clients and market participants prior to go-live across the value-chain. Its purpose is to ensure that the set
of changes delivered as part of the European Offering is implemented consistently, operates as intended, and
can be safely deployed into production.


In addition to this document, specific test documentation will be published by Euronext Markets, Euronext
Clearing, and Euronext Securities, applicable specifically to each entity’s relevant clients and market
participants as the scope of testing differs for the various entities. You can find relevant links to this
information in this document.

1.3 Objectives of testing


The objectives of testing under this guideline are to:

    •   Confirm that the final solutions are fully implemented and meets the agreed business requirements,
         applicable market practices, and client expectations.
    •   Validate that end-to-end processes operate in a stable, correct, and robust manner across all relevant
         scenarios.
    •   Ensure that the delivered solution produces the expected operational and business benefits for all
        impacted participants.
    •   Build a high level of confidence among users and customers in the quality of the solution and in the
         rigor of the applied testing and control approach.
    •   Support client readiness by providing sufficient transparency, validation opportunities, and assurance
       ahead of go-live.

2. Target audience


This high-level test approach document is intended for all market participants involved in, or impacted by, the
European Offering. It provides a common reference for understanding the objectives, scope, and organization
of testing activities ahead of go-live.


This document applies to all actors participating in the European Offering ecosystem. Differences in testing
scope, obligations, and expectations are described on a high-level in this document and further detailed in the
test documentation published by Euronext Markets, Euronext Clearing, and Euronext Securities, respectively.


The key stakeholders of this document include the following participant categories:

    •   Trading Members
    •   Euronext Trading Venues
    •   Clearing Members
    •   Central Counterparties (CCPs), including:
         o   Euronext Clearing
         o   Preferred CCPs
    •   Settlement Agents
    •   Central Securities Depositories (CSDs), including:
         o   Euronext Securities
         o   Alternative CSDs
    •   Custodians
    •   Issuing Agents, Issuers, and Registrars


Each participant category mentioned above is expected to use this document, together with the entity specific
test documentation, as references to understand how testing is structured, what is expected from them, and
how readiness for the European Offering will be assessed, in line with their specific role and services used.


Note for Issuing Agents and Issuers
Due to the diversity of service configurations, timelines, and onboarding models applicable to these
stakeholders, testing with Issuing Agens and Issuers is handled on a case-by-case basis. Issuing Agents and
Issuers wishing to test services offered by Euronext Securities are requested to contact Euronext Securities
(CSD.onboarding@euronext.com) to initiate tailored onboarding and testing activities aligned with the specific
needs of each Issuing Agent or Issuer.


3. Scope of testing


The scope of testing for the European Offering covers the key functional and operational changes introduced
across the post-trade value chain. Testing is designed to validate that these changes operate correctly on a
standalone basis and, where relevant, end-to-end across multiple actors. The overall scope includes services
covering issuance, trading, clearing, settlement, safekeeping, corporate actions, and fiscal services. Testing
focuses on areas where new services are introduced, or interfaces and message flows are materially impacted.

service-by-participant matrix for Euronext clients is provided below, indicating the expected level of testing per

Mandatory to Test
Testing is mandatory. Participants must execute the relevant tests and provide a formal sign-off document as
evidence of successful testing and operational readiness.


Recommended to Test
Testing is not mandatory but is strongly advised for participants seeking to reduce operational risk or gain
confidence in using the new service. No formal sign-off is required.


Conditional to Test
Testing is mandatory only for participants of Euronext Securities who are impacted by the change or who have
explicitly requested the service.


These areas broadly define the functional perimeter of the testing activities. Detailed test scenarios per service
and per client typology are defined separately and published by each Euronext entity.


4. Testing phases & milestones


Testing for the European Offering is structured into two main phases: Test Preparation and Test Execution. This
phased approach ensures that all prerequisites are in place before testing starts and that execution is
conducted in a controlled, transparent, and risk-focused manner. Each phase includes clearly defined
responsibilities for Euronext and for market participants, with timelines and milestones communicated in
advance.

4.1 Test preparation phase


The test preparation phase focuses on establishing the technical, operational, and organizational readiness
required to start testing efficiently and without delay.


What clients can expect from Euronext ahead of testing
Ahead of the testing start, the respective Euronext entity will ensure that the following elements are in place:

    •   Detailed test documentation for all relevant services in scope of testing
    •   Test environments ready and available, including:
         o   Euronext Markets: Next EUA
         o   Euronext Clearing: EUA
         o   Euronext Securities: EUA (connected to T2S UTEST)
    •    Installation of the relevant services and functionalities in the test environments
    •   Test data availability, including a defined list of ISINs and reference data to be used during testing
    •   Upload of balance of selected ISIN in scope into omnibus account of clients to facilitate test scenario
    •    Availability of relevant connectivity
    •   Processes in place for:
         o   Test progress dashboard
         o   Defect logging and follow-up
         o   Status reporting
         o   Structured communication set-up (e.g., named contacts, coordination meetings, standard
                 reporting and support channels)

Client preparation responsibilities
Clients are expected to complete the necessary preparation activities applicable to them, as defined by the
detailed client test documentation provided by Euronext, ahead of test execution phase. Timely completion of
these actions is critical to ensure that testing can start as planned. Please refer to client test documentation
published by the relevant Euronext entity for more information about required client preparation.

4.2 Test execution phase


The test execution phase covers the execution of test scenarios as defined in the detailed client test
documentation published by each Euronext entity. During this phase clients execute the defined test scenarios,
defects are logged and resolved by Euronext, and regular status updates/coordination meetings are held to
monitor progress and address issues.


The test execution phase will be run by each individual Euronext entity for the relevant test scope as previously
defined in this document. For more information regarding test execution per Euronext entity, please refer to
the client test documentation published by the relevant Euronext entity.

5. Detailed test documentation


To support structured and consistent testing, each Euronext entity will publish relevant client test
documentation and/or communication specifically for their clients and services. The client test documentation
includes will typically include:

    •   Guide to support test execution
    •   Test scenarios and test sets
    •   Test data and reference information to be used across test environments, where applicable
    •   Relevant readiness and sign-off forms to be completed, where applicable


The detailed client test documentation from each Euronext entity can be found by following the links below:

    •   Euronext Markets: Connect - Euronext Securities European Offering
    •   Euronext Clearing: Connect - European Offering / Choice of CSD
    •   Euronext Securities: European Offering Documentation


The test documentation may be updated during the testing lifecycle to reflect clarifications or refinements to
existing scenarios, defect-driven adjustments requiring re-testing, and/or alignment with finalized service
configurations. Any material updates to the catalogue will be communicated to participants through the
established testing communication channels.


6. Client support model


The client support model for the European Offering is designed to provide clear points of contact, structured
coordination, and predictable governance throughout the testing period. Its objective is to ensure that
participants receive timely support, that issues and questions are addressed efficiently, and that testing
progresses in a controlled and transparent manner.

6.1 Support structure


Testing activities are supported through a dedicated and structured support set-up. Key elements of the
support structure include:

    •   Client test documentation
    •   Daily assistance from onboarding and support teams during the testing period
    •   Continuous monitoring of requests and issues to ensure timely response and follow-up


Throughout the testing period, Euronext will make dedicated expertise available to support you as needed. This
support will provide a strong mix of testing and content expertise and includes test experts and test analysts
responsible for coordinating and supporting test execution, and subject matter experts from Product, Business

6.3 Test governance and regular meetings


Testing governance is supported through a structured meeting framework that runs throughout the testing
period defined earlier in this document.


Test Webinar
Euronext has already completed two market-wide webinars in 2025 with a dedicated focus on testing. Building
on this, a further test webinar will be held on January 29th 2026, with the specific objective of getting the
market prepared for the start of testing.


The webinar will be jointly hosted by Euronext Markets, Euronext Clearing, and Euronext Securities, ensuring a
consistent, end-to-end view across the value-chain. The session will focus on explaining the overall test
approach as described in this document, the structure and objectives of the test phases, the test scenarios to
be executed towards each Euronext entity, and what market participants should expect in the coming months.


In addition, the webinar will outline what is expected from market participants in terms of preparation,
participation, and readiness, including practical next steps following the session. The webinar is intended as the
main market-wide entry point into the testing phase and a baseline for subsequent, more detailed discussions.


Preparatory Test Meetings
Following the test webinar and ahead of the start of test execution in March 2026, each Euronext entity will
organize a series of preparatory test meetings with the relevant stakeholder groups. These meetings are
designed to support a two-way dialogue with the market after participants have had time to review the test
documentation, alongside other topics when relevant. The focus will be on addressing questions and
clarifications, and confirming the understanding of the test approach.


The preparatory test meetings will also be used to align expectations, discuss any identified constraints or
dependencies, and ensure that all parties are operationally ready before entering full test execution mode. As

7. Readiness sign-off


Readiness sign-off is used to formally confirm that testing has been completed in accordance with the
applicable test plan and that the participant is ready to proceed to go-live.


Sign-off requirements are only applicable to front-to-back testing (see deadline in Section 4), for which all
relevant stakeholders must provide sign-off to the relevant Euronext entity. Sign-off forms will be made
available by each Euronext entity, specifying the relevant details for each client typology. The forms can be
found following these links:

    •   Euronext Markets: Connect - Euronext Securities European Offering
    •   Euronext Clearing: Connect - European Offering / Choice of CSD
    •   Euronext Securities: European Offering Documentation


Throughout the testing period, Euronext will ask statuses on sign-off readiness from relevant stakeholders to
have a better understanding of where we are in terms of overall readiness.


Participants required to provide a sign-off are responsible for ensuring that the relevant testing has been
completed in line with the applicable test plan, reviewing the defined readiness criteria, and completing and
submitting the sign-off form within the requested timeframe. Completed sign-off forms serve as formal
evidence of testing completion and operational readiness for the relevant service and participant role.

The go-live decision for the planned change of Place of Settlement (PSET) scheduled for September 21st 2026
will be taken based on an overall assessment of market readiness and operational stability across the
ecosystem, and will therefore not be solely dependent on individual client readiness sign-off.
