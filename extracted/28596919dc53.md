INTERBOLSA REGULATION No. 1/2017 – Revocation of Interbolsa Regulation no. 6/2005




On April 1, 2017, INTERBOLSA's new Fee Books enter into force, which include commissions owed to

INTERBOLSA by the issuers with securities registered in the centralized securities systems, as well as by the

affiliated financial intermediaries and other entities, related to the services provided by this management entity.


Therefore, under the dispositions set forth in article 18, applicable by virtue of article 46, paragraph 1, both of the

Decree-Law no. 357-C / 2007, of October 31, the Board of Directors of INTERBOLSA – Sociedade Gestora de

Sistemas de Liquidação e de Sistemas Centralizados de Valores Mobiliários, SA, approved the following Regulation:


                                               Single Article

                                        Revoking rule

INTERBOLSA Regulation no. 6/2005, related to Pricing, and its subsequent amendments, is hereby revoked with

effect from 1 April 2017.
