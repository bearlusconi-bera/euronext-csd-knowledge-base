Registration and Administration
Easy administration of all aspects related to managing Debt, Funds and ETPs
Our registration and administration services provides Account Operators with administrative tools to register and maintain all aspects related to managing securities and corporate action in the Euronext Securities Oslo-register.
By issuing your financial instrument with Euronext Securities Oslo, the interest of both holders of the security and the issuer is protected and provided access to a wide range of products and services that addresses your needs related to the administration of your securities.
Euronext Securities Oslo offer its issuance services through a network of Account Operators. In order to issue securities in Euronext VPS you need to contact of our affiliated Account Operators .
