1. Executive summary


This document outlines the Euronext Securities Copenhagen Withholding Tax Relief at Source
(RaS) service available for selected Finnish equity securities distributed through Euronext
Securities Copenhagen.

The service supports the application of reduced withholding tax rates on eligible dividend
distributions at payment date, subject to applicable tax regulations, double taxation treaty (DTT)
requirements, participant eligibility conditions and operational agreements established between
the involved parties.

The Euronext Securities Copenhagen RaS service currently operates under a segregated
account model and applies only to participating issuers and eligible participants onboarded to
the service.

The service may include:

    •   participant onboarding and set-up

    •    eligibility validation

    •   operational controls

    •   reporting preparation

    •   coordination with issuers and issuer agents in connection with eligible dividend events.

For Finnish securities, participants will be required to maintain valid Authorised Intermediary
(AI) registration with the Finnish Tax Administration (Vero), where applicable.

The service is operational in nature and does not constitute legal, tax, regulatory, financial or
any other type of professional advice. Participants remain responsible for assessing their own
tax obligations, eligibility status and compliance requirements.

The service is currently delivered through operational processes and agreed communication
channels between Euronext Securities Copenhagen, issuers, issuer agents and participating
clients.

2. Introduction


Cross-border dividend distributions may be subject to withholding tax requirements and
reduced tax treatment based on applicable tax regulations, double taxation treaties (DTTs), and
market practices.

Euronext Securities Copenhagen provides a Withholding Tax Relief at Source (RaS) service
supporting dividend distributions for eligible Finnish securities.

7. Service lifecycle


The Euronext Securities Copenhagen Withholding Tax Relief at Source service follows a
controlled operational lifecycle to ensure the correct application of withholding tax relief on
eligible dividend events.

The lifecycle consists of the following key stages:


7.1 Service onboarding and eligibility validation

Participants must satisfy the applicable onboarding and eligibility requirements
before benefiting from the RaS service.

This includes:

    •   execution of the required contractual agreements
    •   participant set-up within Euronext Securities Copenhagen operational systems
    •   for Finnish securities where applicable, registration as an Authorised Intermediary (AI)
       with the Finnish Tax Administration (Vero).

Euronext Securities Copenhagen performs ongoing controls to validate:

    •  agreement status

    •   operational set-up accuracy.


7.2 Eligibility and operational controls


Euronext Securities Copenhagen performs operational controls throughout the service lifecycle
to ensure continued participant eligibility and service accuracy.

Controls may include:

    •   verification of AI registration status with Vero

    •   validation of participant agreements

    •   confirmation of operational set-up within Euronext Securities Copenhagen systems

    •   review of special cases such as mergers, bankruptcies or service removals.


7.3 Dividend payment reporting and validation


Euronext Securities Copenhagen performs final validation on the dividend payment calculations
and participant eligibility data prior to making the reports available to the Issuer.

Corrections may be applied where necessary to ensure:

    •   reporting accuracy

    •   correct withholding tax treatment

    •  compliance with operational and regulatory requirements.



7.4 Reporting to Issuer


Following completion of operational controls and validations, the final reporting output is
delivered to the Issuer in xlsx format within the agreed timeline for the relevant dividend event
via email.
