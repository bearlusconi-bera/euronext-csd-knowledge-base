1. About this document


This Service Description Document provides a high-level overview  of the products,
services, and data delivery capabilities offered by Data Services. Its purpose is to describe
the scope of the services, the available delivery options, and the flexibility provided to
clients in relation to data consumption and reporting.

This document reflects the status of the initiative as of July 2026 and may be subject to
further amendments based on continuing discussions with clients, regulatory changes and
future detailed definition of processes. Any amendment to the document will be formally
approved by Euronext  Securities and  properly communicated  to  clients. Euronext
Securities clients will also be updated about the progress of the initiative and the impact it
will have on them throughout the project. Updates will be provided through a new version
of this document, highlighting the main changes.

2. Service Overview


2.1 Service Model


Our CSD Data Products provide access to a broad range of data, analytics and reporting
services designed to support market participants with reliable and actionable insight. Data
Services offers a common report catalogue with standardised data structure, where each
report has a defined field set that CSD clients or other stakeholders can subscribe to in full
or as a filtered subset. The reports are designed to support a broad range of client
requirements across the  financial services ecosystem, such as regulatory reporting,
operational reconciliation, shareholder/issuer transparency or investment analysis.

This approach enables us to continue delivering reports to meet existing requirements, but
with new data structure that  is aligned across  all CSDs with associated commercial
arrangements and the benefit of access to additional data attributes where required.


2.2 Business Applications


The Data Services portfolio  is designed to support a variety of use cases, including
commercial, operational efficiency, product development, shareholder identification, and
issuer transparency.

By delivering high-quality and actionable data, the service enables clients to gain deeper
insight into investor behaviour, ownership structures, and market activity.

2.3 Service Integration


The  service  is designed  to  integrate with  clients'  existing processes and systems,

3. Service Scope and Data Coverage


3.1 Overview


Data Services provides a range  of datasets derived from Euronext  Securities' CSD
operations and activities. The service encompasses data related to securities holdings,
investor activity, settlement processes, issuer services, reference data, and regulatory
reporting.

Datasets are maintained and distributed in structured formats to support operational,
analytical, regulatory, and reporting requirements.


3.2 Ownership and Investor Data


Datasets related to securities of ownership and investor activity, including shareholder
structures, ownership changes,  investor demographics,  concentration  analysis, and
investor behaviour. These datasets contain personal data and are therefore subject to
GDPR. Access is restricted to entitled parties only, and data is anonymised or aggregated
where required before distribution.

3.2.1 Holding reports


The Holdings Report provides a consolidated view of securities holdings registered with
Euronext Securities' CSD as of a specified reference date. The report combines position
data  with  related  instruments,  accounts, and  investor  information  to  provide  a
comprehensive overview of holdings maintained within the CSD.

The report is delivered as a point-in-time snapshot reflecting holdings at the selected
reporting date. Depending on the client's entitlement and service configuration, the report
may include information at investor, account, nominee, participant or aggregated holding
level.

Data is maintained as part of the CSD's operations and activities and covers both listed
and unlisted securities. The dataset includes holding-related information such as quantity

3.3 Settlement Data


The Settlement Data reports is a complementary dataset to the mandatory data sent to
the CSD participants, for commercial purposes. Datasets derived from processing and
settlement activities, covering settlement instructions, settlement efficiency metrics, failed
settlements, liquidity indicators, and transaction monitoring. Content may vary based on
the client's entitlement and service configuration, but typically includes transaction status,
settlement dates, financial instruments, counterparties, and pending quantities or values.

These datasets are intended to support settlement monitoring, operational reconciliation,
risk management, and settlement efficiency analysis.


3.4 Issuer Services Data


Datasets  include  shareholder  identification,  ownership  reporting,  corporate  action
participation, securities balances, and related reporting services. The reports are designed
to support specific stakeholders, like issuers and issuer agents.

These reports may be used to support investor-relations and shareholder engagement,
general-meeting and voting preparation, or monitoring  of ownership  structure and
concentration.


3.5 Transaction Reports


The Transaction Reports provides supplementary information for commercial purposes on
securities transactions recorded within Euronext Securities' CSD. Depending on the client's
entitlement, the data may be presented from different analytical perspectives, for example,
own account or aggregated at CSD level.

The dataset is maintained as part of the CSD's operations and activities and includes
transaction and settlement information, together with daily metrics such as the number
and value of settled instructions, settlement activity, market values, and assets under
custody (AuC) for the selected reporting date.

3.6 Corporate Actions Reports


The Corporate Actions Reports provides information on corporate action events processed
within Euronext Securities' CSD.

The report includes details related to corporate action events such as redemptions,
dividends, allotments, splits, and other security lifecycle events. Depending on the event
type and client entitlement, the report may be provided at a broader market level -
covering events across instruments registered in the CSD — or limited to the client's own
accounts and holdings. Depending on the event type and entitlement, the report may
contain account, instrument, holding, entitlement, and payment-related information.


3.7 Handling Fee Reports


The Handling Fee Reports provides an issuer-level overview of holdings, transaction
activity, and associated service fees recorded within Euronext Securities' CSD.

The report contains aggregated information relating to securities holdings, transaction
volumes, and applicable fees charged  for CSD  services. Depending on the  client's
entitlement and service configuration, the report may include summaries by issuer,
participant, or registered account operator.

The dataset also includes applicable event ratios, such as change ratios and allotment
ratios, associated with forthcoming corporate action events. The report may be used to
support corporate action processing, reconciliation, portfolio administration, and related
reporting activities.


3.8 Securities Reference data


Reference datasets covering financial instruments and associated static data. Coverage
includes equities, bonds, corporate actions, prospectuses, instrument characteristics, and
market reference information required for operational processing and data enrichment.

Instrument  Reference Data  provides  structured  reference  information  for  financial
instruments  registered  with Euronext  Securities,  including both  listed and  unlisted
securities.

Data includes static instrument attributes and reference information required for securities
processing, reporting, and data management activities. The dataset covers a broad range
of instrument types, including equities, bonds, and other securities registered within the

The dataset is maintained as part of the CSD's operations and activities and is updated

3.9 TradeLog


TradeLog is designed to support regulatory and compliance-related activities, including
employee trading controls, account eligibility verification, transaction surveillance, and
other monitoring requirements. The data is delivered through TradeLog, a web-based SaaS
compliance platform provided by Euronext Securities.

The platform supports employee trading compliance processes including pre-clearance
workflows, transaction monitoring, conflict of interest screening, and case management.
Employees  can   register  personal  shareholdings,  board  memberships,  external
appointments, and  trading  activities through a dedicated user  portal.  All reported
information and compliance activities are maintained in an auditable record to support
regulatory and internal governance requirements.

Availability may vary depending on market, jurisdiction, and applicable regulations.


3.10 Liquidity and Fixed Income Data


Datasets covering fixed income markets and  liquidity characteristics, including bond
issuance information, default  statistics, market  activity  indicators, and transparency
reporting. These datasets support analysis of market conditions, instrument-level liquidity,
and fixed income market structure.

4. Data Access and Entitlements


Euronext Securities processes and maintain large volumes of securities, account, investor,
and transaction-related information as part of its core services. The availability of Data
Services products and datasets  is therefore subject to applicable laws, regulations,
contractual obligations, and data governance requirements.

Access to data is determined based on the client's relationship with Euronext Securities,
the nature of the requested dataset, and the  client's legal basis  for accessing the
information. Certain datasets may only be made available to clients in relation to their own
accounts, securities, transactions, or underlying investor populations. Other datasets may
require  specific authorization, entitlement or documented consent where applicable.
Personal data is anonymised or aggregated unless provided to the data owner.

Prior to the provision of any dataset, Euronext Securities assesses data access rights and
applicable usage  conditions  to  ensure  compliance  with  relevant  legal,  regulatory,
confidentiality, and data protection requirements. As a result, the availability, scope, and
level of detail of delivered data may vary between client categories and jurisdictions.
All data products are subject to applicable licensing terms, data usage restrictions, and
entitlement controls as defined by Euronext Securities and the relevant market regulations.

5. Service Delivery and
Configuration


5.1 Data Delivery


Datasets are delivered in structured, system-readable formats to facilitate integration with
client systems and internal processes.

Standard delivery is provided through Secure File Transfer Protocol (SFTP), with CSV being
the primary file format. Additional delivery mechanisms, formats, or reporting solutions
may be provided where supported by the service and agreed with the client.


5.2 Delivery Frequency


Delivery frequencies may be configured according to the specific dataset and service
agreement and can include:
    •   Daily deliveries
    •  Weekly deliveries
    •   Quarterly deliveries
    •  Monthly deliveries

5.3 Service Configuration Options


The service supports a range of configuration options to accommodate client requirements.
Configuration (field selection, filters) is applied in the report engine and is performed by
the Data Services team. Depending on data set, filtering options may include:
    •  Data field selection and reporting content
    •  Market and instrument coverage
    •   Delivery frequency
    •   Delivery channel and file format
    •   Licensing and distribution permissions


5.4 Client-Specific Configuration


This configurable approach enables clients to receive data aligned with their operational,
analytical, and regulatory requirements while maintaining consistency with  existing
integration and reporting processes.
