  1.  Executive summary


The French FTT was introduced in the French tax regulation pursuant to Article 5 of the
French Amended Finance Bill of 14 March 2012.



The French FTT is applicable to the acquisitions (which may also result from corporate
actions) of shares traded on a regulated market, French or foreign, issued by French
entities (headquartered in France) with a market capitalisation of more than €1 billion
on 1 December of the previous year.



   a) Euronext Securities Milan as Investor CSD



Euronext Securities Milan provides an FTT service as an Investor CSD for French
equities. Information about this service is currently available via the following link
(under T2S Markets > France > Financial Transaction Tax). In addition, in Annex A you
will find a visual flow to illustrate the processing of FTT when Euronext Securities Milan
is Investor CSD.



   b) Euronext Securities Milan as Issuer CSD



Euronext Securities Milan is authorised by the French authorities to act as Issuer CSD
for French equities through the mechanism provided in CSDR. However with regard to
French FTT as an Issuer CSD established outside of France, it is currently bound by the
following extract in the French FTT legislation:





“Central depositary is established outside France The taxpayer must send return
No. 3374-SD along with payment to the Large Corporations Division before the 25th of
the month following settlement of the securities. This rule does not apply to the
acquisition of a depositary receipt during its creation process. In this situation, the tax
is reported and, where applicable, paid under the conditions stipulated for a central
depositary established in France if the receipt evidences the transfer of the share it
represents. In situations other than creation or cancellation (see §148 et seq.), the
acquisition of a depositary receipt is reported and, where applicable, payment is made
to the Large Corporations Division before the 25th of the month following the
acquisitions of taxable securities (BOI-TCA-FIN-10-40-20150304, §120). The taxpayer
may also opt to report and, where applicable, make payment to the Euroclear France
member of its choice.”                                                               Commented [SC1]: This section has been removed
                                                                                              as it refers to depositary receipts, which are not

  2. In Practice


As a practical consequence of the current wording in the French FTT law, taxpayers
holding French shares subject to FTT for which Euronext Securities Milan is the Issuer
CSD must file FTT declarations and payments directly to the French tax authorities, as
detailed below.

CSD participants are required to send their FTT declaration (No. 3374-SD) and related
payments directly to the French Authorities (payment is made to the Large Corporations
Division before the 25th of the month following the acquisitions of taxable securities
(BOI-TCA-FIN-10-40-20150304, §120).

As a reminder:

The French FTT is applicable on the acquisitions of shares traded on a regulated market
(French or foreign) issued by French entities (Headquarters are in France) with a market
capitalisation of more than €1 billion on 1 December of the previous year (acquisitions
may also result from corporate actions).

Only acquisitions are taxed, while sales are not in scope. The list of French companies
with a market capitalisation value on 1 December of each year greater than EUR 1 billion
will be published before 31 December of the same year of the same year on the French
Tax Authority portal (link to the FTA's website).
