Working Days & Operating Hours
2026
The working calendar of Euronext Securities Porto is based on the calendar defined for the TARGET2 System (Trans-European Automated Real Time Gross Settlement Express Transfer System).
On May 1, 2026 (Labour Day) EURONEXT SECURITIES PORTO systems will be open for the settlement of free of payment instructions, according to TARGET2-Securities (T2S) calendar.
Thus, the services managed by Euronext Securities Porto, during the year 2026, will not be available on Saturdays and Sundays and on the days outlined on the table.
Please note that the working calendar for 2026 is available in the Notice No. 25/1162 Closing days in 2026.
This information does not replace the necessary consultation of the Regulations in force.
