Post Trade Services
What we offer
Settlement in DKK and EUR through TARGET2-Securities.
SEK settlement in Central Bank money, enabling our customers to settle trades cross-border and domestically across asset classes.
The choice of using segregated accounts.
Access and communication is provided through international standard formats.
Euronext Securities supports international clients and other market infrastructures to meet the complex operational, regulatory and risk mitigation challenges. As a reliable Post Trade Services provider, we offer fully integrated and flexible solutions to the Danish market infrastructure. Operating as the Central Securities Depository (CSD) for Denmark and as an Asset Servicing provider across Nordic markets, we deliver easy access to safekeeping services for international clients and other market infrastructures such as CSDs and CCPs.
