Structured Investment Products
One hub for issuance and settlement of warrants and certificates.
Facts
Simpel process for issuers
Short time to market
Structured Investment Products (SIP) follow the same simple process for issuers across the Nordic countries, regardless of whether issuance or settlement is in DKK, SEK or EUR. To service the instruments, Euronext Securities provides three different processes: a manual, a semi-automated and a fully-automatic process. All with the same price structure.
Time to market is short, and often below ten minutes from idea to launch, so that if an instrument closes for some reason, the issuer can launch a new product within a short time.
SIPs give investors access to invest in different warrants and certificates with varying characteristics. Some of them are geared, and some are based on different underlying assets, such as local and foreign shares, indexes, commodities and currencies.
