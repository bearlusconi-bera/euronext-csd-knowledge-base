Investor Portal
Full overview of your VPS accounts and holdings
VPS Investor Portal is the portal accessible for the end investors. The portal gives the investor overview of all your customers Euronext Securities Oslo-registered accounts, transactions and holdings. Information regarding corporate actions and the possibility to execute shareholder rights can be done via the portal.
VPS Investor Portal is distributed via most Norwegian banks, fund managers and brokers.
