Notes to Mod. 19-RFI

The form 19-RFI must be presented per each beneficial owner and per each ISIN code.

It must be printed double face and duly filled in all sections without abbreviations,

cancellations or comments outside the boxes.

Considering the Portuguese Market peculiarities we suggest you to send a copy of Mod. 19-

RFI via e-mail in order to verify it with our foreign correspondent.
