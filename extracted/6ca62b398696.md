Corporate Actions
Flexible solutions supporting a wide range of corporate actions
Our corporate action solutions provide you with great flexibility concerning the corporate actions your company is planning to execute, and covers both calculation and registering of results. Your account operator will work closely with Euronext Securities Oslo to plan, prepare and execute the relevant corporate action. After a successful processing of the corporate action, you will get full access to both the corporate action itself, and the update on a shareholder level giving you a full information about the impact of the corporate action
