1.  Executive summary

As the issuer or investor CSD for French equities, Euronext Securities and its participants
need to manage specific requirements related to French registered securities. French
equities can exist in different security forms:

    •  Bearer securities
    •   Registered securities:
         o  Administered Registered Securities (ARS)
         o  Pure Registered Securities (PRS).

The security form determines where the securities are kept (e.g., in an account at the level
of the issuer’s register or in a securities account with the participant only) and the
corresponding requirements. Depending on the issuer’s decision or in order to comply with
French legislation, shares may be issued as occasionally registered (VON) or essentially
registered (VEN), also referred as “fully registered”.

4.2.  Information on Registered Investors (IRI)

The Information on Registered Investors (IRI) is a set of information used to manage the
accurate identification of shareholders by the issuer, and to facilitate the exchange of
shareholder information between Custodians and Issuers/Registrars in a set format. It
plays a central role in enabling seamless communication and ensuring that shareholder
data is transmitted and recorded accurately.

IRI must include all the necessary information required by the Registrar to identify the
shareholder and to either create or update a Compte Courant Nominatif (CCN) in the
Issuer’s shareholder register. This ensures that the register remains up to date and
compliant with regulatory and operational requirements.

Specificities: In certain cases (Corporate actions, etc.), the IRI can also serve as a trigger
for settlement instructions at the CSD level. This additional functionality reinforces the role
of IRI as a key component in the end-to-end processing of registered securities, linking
registration and settlement processes more efficiently.

4.3.  Reconciliation

The reconciliation process applies to French Registered Securities accordingly with the rules
set forth by the Autorité des Marchés Financiers (AMF) in France (AMF Regulation). It aims,
for each ISIN code, to maintain consistency between the quantity of securities held by a
shareholder with its Custodian and the quantity of securities registered in the register for
each shareholder.

In current market practice, reconciliation is performed in two ways:

    i.   Automatically by the IRI platform for each eligible transaction (‘flow-
     based reconciliation’)

In order to perform a flow-based reconciliation, the settlement instruction must be
matched with shareholder information transmitted through the IRI platform. To support
this, a dedicated identification of settlement instructions related to registered securities is
performed at the Euronext Securities Milan level.

In the case that reconciliation cannot be performed due to discrepancies between IRI and
transactions, or missing information (IRI or Settlement instruction not sent), penalties may
be applied.

The reconciliation process on flows can be performed in different cases:

Change of shareholder details: this applies to cases where only shareholder
details (such as address, phone number, etc.) are updated, without involving any
related settlement transaction:

   o  For these changes, only a compliance check by the IRI platform with the deadlines
        for transmission of the acceptance or rejection is required. Failure to comply with
       these deadlines may result in reminders and penalties to the Registrar.

5.3.3. Impacts of blocking/earmarking available securities balance

Blocking impacts the available security balance (AWAS) in several ways:

    1. Restriction on use: when securities are blocked, they are designated for a specific
      purpose and cannot be used for other transactions. This reduces the available
       balance in AWAS, as the blocked securities are effectively withheld from general
       use.

    2. Settlement instructions: blocking ensures  that only non-blocked  securities
       positions are available for settlement. This preserves the integrity of the blocked
       assets and ensures they are used only for their intended purpose.

    3. Impact on positions: the security balance in T2S and Euronext Securities Milan
      systems reflects both the available securities (AWAS) and any blocked quantities.

On the contrary, earmarking does not reduce the available balance (AWAS), as the
earmarked quantity remains fully eligible for settlement.

Rather, these securities are flagged for a specific purpose and identified by the Euronext
Securities Milan corporate action solution, enabling their exclusion from entitlement
calculations when necessary. Both T2S and Euronext Securities Milan display earmarked
quantities alongside the available balance, providing full transparency without restricting
settlement activity.

5.3.5. Process of blocking for elective corporate action processing

The process of reservation for French Registered Securities during elective corporate
actions involves the following steps:

    1. Instruction submission: participants submit their option choices via the Euronext
       Securities Milan U2A interface or using ISO/SWIFT formats 15022 or 20022.

    2. Instruction control: corporate action instructions are checked against predefined
       rules to ensure data completeness, correctness, and compliance with market
       standards.

    3. Status advice generation: based on the  initial checks, a corporate action
       instruction status advice is generated with the relevant status code:

         o  REJT: rejected, if the instruction fails the checks.

         o  PEND: pending, if the initial set of checks is successful.

    4. Blocking initiation: upon successful validation, the instructed quantity reservation
         is initiated and requested from T2S, using the appropriate reservation code for
       position blocking (e.g., RECA).

    5. Status advice generation: once the reservation is confirmed, a new status advice
         is generated, indicating acceptance for further processing (PACK).

    6. Entitlement calculation: following confirmation, entitlement calculations are
      performed to proceed with further processing.

    2. Earmarking initiation: Upon receiving the Client's request, the CSD participant
        instructs the earmarking of the specified quantity from T2S, using the appropriate
      earmarking code (e.g., EREF for registered securities).

    3. Entitlement calculation: Based on the position loads, Euronext Securities Milan

      Since the transmission of settlement transactions on registered securities and the related
      IRI flows are not integrated into a single process, it is essential to ensure that each flow is
      accurately transmitted and processed. This safeguards consistency between the quantity
      of securities recorded in the Register and the corresponding transaction bookings at the
    CSD level.

              •   Identify and select IRI and settlement transaction eligible to the reconciliation
                process

              •   Reconcile IRI and related settlement transaction on references basis

              •   Identify the unreconciled or partially reconciled flows (case of multiple IRI for a
                settlement transaction)

              •  Inform participants on the status of the reconciliation process for control and/or
                 regularisation purposes.

              •  Apply if necessary, penalties in case of pending reconciliation

8.3.4. Regularisation notifications and penalties

The application of regularisation notifications and penalties is performed in accordance with
French market practices.

8.3.4.1.   Principle

Regularisation notifications consist in the transmission to the custodian or registrar having
transmitted a settlement flow or an IRI that is unregularised in the required deadlines, of
a notification to regularise it.

Failure to regularise an IRI following the first notification leads to the application of a
penalty.

8.3.4.2.  Terms of application of regularisation notifications and penalties to
         IRI issued by Custodians

Regularisation notifications are applied to IRI that are not reconciled or partially reconciled
under the following conditions:

    •   If an IRI is not reconciled on the third T2S business day morning following the trade
       date, then a first regularisation notification is sent to the participant.

    •   If the IRI is still not reconciled on the morning of the fourth business day following
       the trade date then a penalty applied on a one-off basis (no other penalty is applied
       thereafter).

    •   If the IRI is not reconciled, then a regularisation notification applied every 4 days.

and applicable transaction type code (e.g., CA) are included.

Securities Milan automatically generates and process the settlement instruction with T2S.

This  approach  supports  accurate  and  efficient  processing  of  IRI  messages  and
reconciliation of IRI messages.

10.3.1. Processing flows for trades on French registered securities

Trades on French registered securities may be executed in three ways:

    1. On-exchange

    2.  Via Euronext Securities Milan PTCS for cash or deferred-settlement trades

    3. Off-exchange.

The following sections apply to French securities issued as essentially registered (VEN).
Securities issued as occasionally registered (VON) and held in registered form (pure or
administered) must be converted  prior  to  trading.  If no conversion  is performed,
settlement restrictions apply and the related settlement instructions are rejected.

10.3.1.1. IRI workflows for trades on essentially registered securities

The IRI process flow begins when the Euronext Securities Milan participant sends an IRI01
message to the IRI platform, including:

    1. The settlement instruction reference if off-exchange trades (transaction type code
    MO or BO), or
    2. The transaction notice reference if the trade has been performed on PTCS (operation
      code FO or CO).

Upon receipt of the IRI01, the IRI platform performs the standard syntax, format, and
business‑rule validations. If the message fails these validations, an IRI05 is immediately
returned to the Euronext Securities Milan participant with the relevant error code.

registrar. The registrar then accepts or rejects the request and issues an IRI03 response.
The IRI platform forwards this outcome to the Euronext Securities Milan participant through
an IRI04 message.

If the registrar rejects the request, the participant may resubmit an IRI01 using a
transaction type code ending in “J” or regularisation. The workflow then proceeds as
described above.

10.3.3. Processing flows for portfolio transfers of French registered
         securities

According to current French market practice, portfolio transfers fall into two categories:

    •  Transfer with change of ownership (for example, between spouses)

    •  Transfers without change of ownership but reflect a change of CSD participant
       or custodian holder.

Both types of operations are usually referred to as “direct transfers” or “file transfers”.

10.3.3.1. Process flow for portfolio transfer with change of ownership but no
        change of CSD participant

A portfolio transfer with change of ownership but no change of Euronext Securities Milan
participant requires a change in the issuer register. This implies the CSD participant to
generate an IRI01 to inform the registrar, including the relevant Transaction Type Code
(e.g., TT).

Securities movement

In such case, there is no securities movement. The ownership transfer applies at the
securities account level, so that there is no transfer between securities accounts.

IRI process flow

The IRI workflow for a portfolio transfer involving a change of ownership, without a change
of participant, is initiated by the Euronext Securities Milan participant. Based on the change
of ownership information, known by the delivering participant, the participant sends two
IRI01 messages to the IRI platform:

    •  one requesting the deregistration and
    •  another requesting the registration.

Both messages include the same file number, allowing them to be linked within the process.

This sequence reflects the required market practice.

Upon receipt of the IRI01 messages,  if they are technically accepted, the IRI platform
generates the corresponding IRI02 messages to the registrar. If an IRI01 message is
technically rejected, the IRI platform immediately returns an IRI05 rejection message to
the participant, including the relevant error code.

For the deregistration request, the registrar may accept or reject the message, generating
an IRI03 response. The IRI platform then returns an IRI04 message to the participant,
indicating the outcome.

If the deregistration is accepted and the registration request is received by the registrar,
the registrar sends an IRI01 message to the IRI platform, which then generates an IRI02
message to the Euronext Securities Milan participant.

If the registrar rejects the portfolio transfer request, the participant may resend both IRI01
messages  for  deregistration and  registration  with a CTO code  ending  in  “J”  for
regularisation. The process then follows the same steps as described above.

Settlement flow

No securities movement occurs, so neither the Euronext Securities Milan participant nor
the CSD generates a settlement instruction.

11.3.3. Processing flows for portfolio transfers of French registered

According to current French market practice, portfolio transfers fall into two categories:

    •  Direct transfers, which involve a change of ownership (for example, between
      spouses)

    •   File transfers, which do not involve a change of ownership but reflect a change of
     CSD participant or custodian holder.

Both types of operations are referred to as “portfolio transfers” in the following sections,
with a distinction made between transfers involving a change of ownership and those
involving a change of CSD participant.

Additionally, these transfers are not settled automatically by an IRI submission. Deliverer
participants must generate both the settlement instruction and the IRI or BRN.

11.3.3.1. Process flow for portfolio transfer with change of ownership but no
        change of CSD participant
