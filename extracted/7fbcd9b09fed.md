Connectivity
Euronext Securities Milan offers its customers the possibility to choose between the following connection channels:
SWIFT
National Interbank Network (RNI)
Milan 4U (Web based interface). the access portal that allows our customers to connect with our Membership, Pre-Settlement, Settlement, Custody and Collateral platforms
A2A
Access to Target 2 Securities services can take place in the following ways:
DCP (Directly Connected Participant)
ICP (Indirectly Connected Participant)
The difference between the connection methods is represented by the different technical infrastructure used to communicate with the Target 2 Securities platform, while still maintaining the contractual relationship with Euronext Securities Milan.
