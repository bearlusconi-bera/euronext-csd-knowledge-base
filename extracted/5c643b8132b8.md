Initial Recording of Securities for Listed Issuers
Recording of securities is available:In an individual securities account orIn an omnibus securities account orIn a Joint Investor Share, which ensures the inheritance of the securities tax-free
Benefits
Ensuring the integrity of the public record
Registering securities in a secure, fully automated electronic system operated by a licensed depository under CSDR
Efficient management and reduced operating costs for the company
Accurate maintenance of the share register at all times
Access to additional Euronext Securities Athens services, (such as corporate actions)
