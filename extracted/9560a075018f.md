1. Welcome

2. EC update on the current CSDR change legislative process

   The EC representative reported from the Council working party which took place on 2 April
   2025 where good progress was made, and the Council is expected to shortly reach an
   agreed position. Two issues were discussed: a possible exemption for securities financing
    transactions (SFTs) and a possible suspension of cash penalties around the move to T+1.


3. ESMA update on current work on regulatory changes

   The ESMA representative gave a brief update on:

     -  The timeline of the public consultation on possible amendments to the RTS on
       settlement discipline which is still open until Monday 14 April.

     -  The timeline of and the on-going work on the technical advice on cash penalties, which
         is expected to be submitted for approval to the ESMA Board of Supervisors in June.

   The following comments were made:
     -  The T+1 Industry Committee (IC) Chair indicated that it had been agreed by the IC that
             it will be up to each trade association to respond to the RTS consultation, and for the
      IC secretariat to coordinate, to ensure consistency between responses. He further
       confirmed that, based on TW work, not having major diverging positions, there should
       not be any substantial differences on key points between associations

     -  The Chair highlighted that it would be beneficial if a common message on key points
       could be agreed by the associations to ensure good progress by ESMA on the
      development of the draft RTS.

     -  The EC representative supported this message, adding that getting at least majority
       views would give a clear way forward which would also be useful for the good progress
        of the legislative process with the Council and Parliament.


4. ECB update on T2S preparations for T+1

   The ECB representative presented the status of the work that has been conducted so far,
    in particular through the dedicated T+1 Task Force set up under the T2S governance in
   January 2025 (the T2S T+1 TF):

     -   the T2S governance needs to identify and launch the assessment for any T2S change
       requests in Q3 2025;

     -   the T2S T+1 TF has prepared preliminary considerations regarding the perimeter of
       possible T2S adaptations, expected to be shared with the IC by end April 2025;

     -  work focused on possible changes to the T2S Night-Time Settlement (NTS), based on
       evidence through external data points across trading, clearing and settlement activities
      and internal data points.

   The IC Chair confirmed that this is aligned with the work done at IC level (no request for a
     full reshape of T2S, only to make certain changes; this is in particular discussed by the
   Operational Timetable TW).


•  Industry Committee Chair update [presentation]

   The IC Chair updated the Committee on the following:

     -  TW update: all co-leads feel that their workstreams are making good progress and no
        substantial roadblock to delivery of the final report by end of June has been identified
      so far.

     -  Updated 2025 work plan:
              •  15 May: TWs to finalise draft recommendations
              •  28 May: in-person IC meeting in Brussels
              •  30 June: publication of finalised Implementation Roadmap report
              •  Summer: public consultation
              •  September - December: T+1 Implementation Playbook and T+1 Q&As.

     -  Communication strategy: a letter from the Chair explaining the project and governance
        structure has been finalised and will be made public on the ESMA website. It should
      be distributed as appropriate by IC members, including to national associations and
       other market groups.
