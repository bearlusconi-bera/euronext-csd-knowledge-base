Issuing Agent Services
What we offer
Euronext Securities offer a wide variety of Issuing Agent products and services
Issuing securities, such as shares or bonds, through a central securities depository (CSD) requires documentation, decision-making, and coordination between several parties. To issue securities through the CSD, you are obliged to have an issuing agent – a key player that monitors and handles relevant practical tasks related to a successful issuance process and throughout the lifecycle of the securities
As an experienced independent issuing agent, we specialise in seamlessly facilitating the issuance of securities for corporations, financial institutions, and organisations aiming to raise capital.
