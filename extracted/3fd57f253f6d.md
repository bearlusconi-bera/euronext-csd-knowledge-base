About us
Euronext Securities Milan is the Central Securities Depository for the Italian financial market.
Authorized to operate under the European CSD Regulation (CSD-R), ES Milan provides efficient and secure settlement, custody, asset servicing and issuer services on domestic and foreign securities including bonds, government securities, debt securities, ETFs and certificates.
Supporting more than 197 intermediaries, including commercial banks, global custodians, broker / dealers, Italian and international market infrastructures and over 2,700 issuers, Euronext Securities Milan assists our clients in every phase of the customer journey.
