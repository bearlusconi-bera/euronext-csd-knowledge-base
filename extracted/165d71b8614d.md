## PDF page 23

The move to T+1


▪Go live for EU , UK and Switzerland is 11 October 2027

▪The reduction of settlement cycle is aimed at improving efficiency of EU markets making them more attractive for
  global investors.

▪The move to T+1 should be seen as a component of the broader EU strategy to make EU capital markets more
  competitive

▪Euronext Securities is widely represented in the Industry Committee and it co-leads the following technical
  working groups :

   ▪Corporate Events

   ▪Trading

   ▪Settlement Efficiency
