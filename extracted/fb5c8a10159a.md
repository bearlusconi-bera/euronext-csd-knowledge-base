          EURONEXT SECURITIES PORTO NOTICE N.º 23/1132


                            Closing days in 2024


The working calendar of EURONEXT SECURITIES PORTO is based on the calendar
defined  for the TARGET2 System (Trans-European Automated Real Time Gross
Settlement Express Transfer System).

On May 1, 2024 (Labour Day) EURONEXT SECURITIES PORTO’ systems will be open
for the settlement of free of payment instructions, according to TARGET2-Securities
(T2S) calendar.

Thus, the services managed by EURONEXT SECURITIES PORTO, during the year 2024,
 will not be available on Saturdays and Sundays and on the following days:





    Holiday       Day                   Festivity

      1st January     Monday            New Year

     29th March       Friday              Good Friday

      1th April        Monday                Easter Monday

     25th December   Wednesday            Christmas Day

     26th December   Thursday             Boxing Day




Euronext Securities Porto, 17th November, 2023
