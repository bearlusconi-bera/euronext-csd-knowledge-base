About Euronext Securities
Euronext Securities is the central securities depository (CSD) network connecting European economies to global capital markets.
With CSDs in Athens, Copenhagen, Milan, Oslo, Porto, Euronext Securities is one of the largest CSD operators in Europe.We provide secure and resilient settlement and custody solutions and a portfolio of services to support our clients along the entire value chain.
Our customers
Euronext Securities strives to provide a smooth and flawless client experience, making it easy for clients to use our services, leverage our market knowledge and take advantage of our local and global scale.
