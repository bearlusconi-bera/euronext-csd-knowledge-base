Evaluation of the Danish market migrating to TARGET2-Securities
On Monday 29 October 2018 securities settlement in Danish krone (DKK) was migrated to TARGET2-Securities (T2S). The Danish National Stakeholder Group (DK NSG) has evaluated the migration, concluded on a number of learning points and identified a number of initiatives going forward with the purpose of increasing settlement efficiency even further. The evaluation has been published in a note.
