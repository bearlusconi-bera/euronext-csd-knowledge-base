Equities and Equity Certificates
Our services provide companies with safe and efficient access to the Norwegian capital market, reduce their administrative burdens, and may be the first step in preparing a future listing on the stock exchange.
Regardless of whether your company is recently established or if you are actively pursuing listing on a stock exchange, our services provides you with several benefits. Our digitized solutions help you stay compliant with relevant laws and regulations, keep track of your shareholders, automatically tax report your shareholders register to the Norwegian Tax Authorities, help ensure correct handling of corporate actions, among many other benefits.
Additionally, investors often appreciate that companies register with Euronext Securities Oslo due to the increased investor security associated with having shares registered in a Central Securities Depository (CSD).
