Corporate Events Service
Euronext Securities is pleased to share its Common Corporate Events Service User Guide. We encourage our clients to leverage this comprehensive resource to gain a thorough understanding of our envisioned goals. You can consult the Frequently Asked Question (FAQs) below for further information.
Why a new Corporate Events Service?
Corporate Events plays a key role in capital markets. Euronext Securities is committed to streamlining and simplifying the process to meet the evolving needs of our clients.
We have prepared a comprehensive resource to help clients gain a thorough understanding of our envisioned goals.
These documents outline Euronext’s vision for a fully harmonized and secure approach to managing corporate events across all asset classes within our CSDs. This vision is aligned with the key standards established by the ECB’s AMI-SeCo working groups, the SMPG guidelines, and relevant EU regulations, including SRD II.
