If your company has electronic shares registered with us, you can benefit from our reliable platform that provides secure settlement of trades in DKK, SEK and EUR.
Most share-issuing companies have investors all over the world. To make it easier for international investors to invest in the many different shares issued via Euronext Securities, we have been part of the pan-European settlement system, TARGET2-Securities (T2S), since the beginning.
Annual dividend payments to investors
Annual general meetings and the related dividend payments are the most important event for share-issuing companies. We offer an effective dividend solution which ensures that the right tax is withheld, but most importantly, that investors receive the correct dividend in DKK, SEK or EUR.
