EURONEXT SECURITIES ATHENS
Cash Settlement Services
Euronext Securities Athens is a direct member of TARGET (the ECB Euro payment system). Through the Euronext Securities Athens Ancillary System, it provides real time cash payments to/from market Participants. For this purpose, each market Participant must be serviced by a Settlement Bank that participates in Euronext Securities Athens ASI. The Euronext Securities Athens System provides to its Participants detailed information about the daily financial obligations, and the current cash balances, as well as detailed cash transactions.
