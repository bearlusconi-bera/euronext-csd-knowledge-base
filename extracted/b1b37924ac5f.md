Legal entities
The CSDs in Athens, Copenhagen, Milan, Oslo and Porto operate under their local entities.
Euronext Securities Copenhagen
Euronext Securities Copenhagen is the commercial name of VP Securities A/S.
Euronext Securities Milan
Euronext Securities Milan is the commercial name of Monte Titoli S.p.A
Euronext Securities Oslo
Euronext Securities Oslo is the commercial name of Verdipapirsentralen ASA.
Euronext Securities Porto
Euronext Securities Porto is the commercial name of Interbolsa S.A.
Euronext Securities Athens
Euronext Securities Athens (formerly AthexCSD) is the commercial name of Hellenic Central Securities Depository S.A.
In line with local regulatory requirements each legal entity operates under a local license and is subject to local legislation.
In line with local governance requirements each CSD has it's own board, supervision and committees.

We value our customers
At Euronext Securities we do not only highly value our customers, we also appreciate their opinion and care for their interests. In line with CSDR requirements we have established User Committees for all of our CSDs to exchange information and gather input and feedback..
We consult our User Committees on key topics that impact our customers and business.
The User Committees provide independent advice to the Boards of our CSDs.
