Funds - Order Routing

Euronext Securities Porto provides a platform for processing and settlement
of subscription and redemption requests of Open-end Investment Funds (Order
Routing), which provides to the Fund Management Entities a greater facility of
commercialization as well as better management and control for all the players
in the Investment Funds market.





  The Funds - Order Routing platform offers an
  integrated and efficient solution for processing and
  settlement the subscription and redemption requests



    ORDER ROUTING

Order Routing, the placement and treatment of the requests is handled in a real time basis.
