Safekeeping and Asset Servicing
As a key financial market infrastructure, Euronext Securities Oslo has delivered extensive services to the Norwegian financial market since its founding in 1985
Euronext Securities Oslo offer comprehensive depositary services such as safekeeping and asset servicing information to intermediaries; banks, brokers, financial institutions and fund managers. Registered financial instruments are held in book-entry form and can be kept either on beneficial owner- or nominee accounts.
We offer a range of products and services to support your client’s investments so that you as an intermediary can achieve more for your clients.
