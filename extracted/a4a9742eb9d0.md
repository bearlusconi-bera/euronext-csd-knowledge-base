Beneficiaries who  already have an  Investor Share and an  individual
Securities Account or Joint investors Securities Account in the DSS, are
required to provide the Issuer with the code number of the Investor Share and the
Securities Account where the non-listed securities will be held, as well as the code
of the  relevant  Participant  in the DSS.  This  information  is  included  in the
"Investor Share & Securities Account Details" printout of the DSS, which
can be provided to the beneficiaries by the relevant Participant.

The holders of non-listed securities (shares or bonds) who do not have an
Investor Share and an individual Securities Account or Joint investors
Securities Account in the DSS, are required to create an Investor Share and an
individual Securities Account or Joint investors Securities Account in the DSS
through a Participant of their choice and provide the relevant code numbers to the
Issuer. Specifically:

a) For the creation of an Investor Share and an individual Securities
  Account (for a natural or legal person) in the DSS through an Euronext
   Securities Athens Participant, the interested holder of non-listed securities
  (Shares or Bonds):
   1. Is addressed to an Euronext Securities Athens Participant of their own
     choice
   2. Follows the Participant's instructions regarding the completion of their
     investor classification questionnaires and the signing of the relevant
     Investment Services Agreement, by submitting relevant documents, for
     example:
       ▪  in the case of Natural Persons: photocopy of the identification
          certificate (ID card or passport), recent income tax return, confirmation
         of Social Security Number (AMKA), telephone bill, email address, etc.
       ▪  in the case of Legal Entities: certificate of registration in the relevant
         Register (GEMH), certifications of legal representatives and final
         beneficiaries, income tax return, telephone bill and email of legal
         representative, etc.
   3. Requests the Participant to provide the details of the Unit and
     Individual Account code number, where the non-listed securities will be
      held, and the code of the relevant Participant in the DSS, in order to
      provide the same to the Issuer. Please note that this information is included
       in the "Share & Securities Account Details" printout of the DSS, which
    may be provided to the beneficiary by the Participant.
b) In order to create a Joint investors Securities Account in the DSS through
  an Euronext Securities Athens Participant, it is required:
1. All joint beneficiaries of non-listed securities (shares or bonds) must
  have an active Investor Share and an individual Securities Account in
  the DSS under the same Participant's management.
   2. The co-beneficiaries of non-listed securities to submit to the said
      Participant, in accordance with its procedures, the relevant application
      for the creation of a Joint investors Securities Account, in which the
      1st co- beneficiary will be designated as the person in charge of the
     Joint investors Securities Account and the order of the co-
      beneficiaries, specifying appropriately the indication of succession, so
