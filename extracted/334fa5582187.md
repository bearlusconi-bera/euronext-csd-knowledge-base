RESOLUTION No 13


"Technical terms and procedures for the Regulatory Reporting Service"



                         THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
               OF THE GREEK CENTRAL SECURITIES DEPOSITORY
                         SOCIETE ANONYME (ATHEXCSD)

                               (Meeting 22/02/2021)
Having regard to:

    1.  The provisions of Articles 5.2 of Part 5 of Section X of the Rulebook of the Greek
        Central Securities Depository (hereinafter the "Rules"), and
    2.  the need to specify the technical terms and procedures relating to the provision of
       the Regulatory Reporting Service

                       ISSUES THIS RESOLUTION AS FOLLOWS:


    1.  Scope of application

This Resolution lays down the terms and procedures for the provision of the Regulatory
Reporting Service (hereinafter the Service). The Service is provided to Participants or other
Intermediaries who are their customers for the purposes of their compliance with their
obligations to submit Regulatory Reports that ATHEXCSD may support based on  its
procedures. Compliance should be related to the transactions settled for the Participant or
the Intermediary through ATHEXCSD, in accordance with the Rulebook.


    2.  Terms and procedures for the provision of the Service

1.  ATHEXCSD shall formulate the subject matter and type of the Service on the basis of the
     individual requests of the Participants or other Intermediaries.

2.   The person concerned, in accordance with the above, shall submit an application to
    ATHEXCSD in a form issued by it, specifying the type of the Regulatory Report and the
     provisions requiring its submission.

3.   The relevant application shall also specify the period of time for which the Service is
     requested, the periodicity in terms of its provision by ATHEXCSD depending on the type
     of relevant regulatory obligation, as well as any other necessary details.

4.  ATHEXCSD may request any other data or information  it deems necessary for the
    assessment of the application. It may also refuse to accept the application, providing
    adequate reasons for its refusal.

5.  Upon acceptance of the application, ATHEXCSD set out to the person concerned the
    procedures to be followed, indicatively as regards the deadlines and the periodicity of
     submission of the data and the format of submission, for the purposes of providing the
     relevant Service.


    3.  Submission procedures

1.  ATHEXCSD submit the Regulatory Reports of the person concerned on a case-by-case
     basis, in accordance with its procedures and in compliance with the statutory procedure
     for submitting them.

2.   For the submission of par. 1, the person concerned shall be required to comply with the
    terms and procedures for the provision of the necessary information, where appropriate,
     in compliance with the terms of par. 5 of Article 2 hereto.

3.   In any case of submission, ATHEXCSD shall provide, upon completion of the submission,
     relevant information to the person concerned, in accordance with the information
     procedures, adopted in accordance with the statutory submission.

4.   For the use of the Service, the person concerned must pay the charges provided for in a
     relevant Resolution of ATHEXCSD.
