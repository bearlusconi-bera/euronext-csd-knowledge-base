European Offering Documentation
Euronext Securities Milan is committed to providing issuers, intermediaries, and investors with innovative and efficient post-trade solutions. As part of the Euronext Securities European Offering, we are introducing a range of new services tailored specifically for the French, Belgian, and Dutch markets. These services are designed to ensure seamless integration, operational efficiency, and a high standard of market connectivity, making it easier for you to expand your activities across borders.On this page, you will find relevant service descriptions, technical documentation and key materials related to these new offerings. These documents are intended to support our clients in understanding and preparing for the upcoming changes across the relevant markets.
The page is regularly updated to ensure you always have access to the latest available information.For information about our already existing services, including full service descriptions and the official CSD Rulebook, please visit our dedicated Existing services & CSD Rulebook page. There you will find information about the current range of services available to all clients, which complements the new solutions presented here.
