Issuing Agent Services
An efficient and successful issuance process.
Issuance of securities – e.g. shares or bonds – through a central securities depository (CSD) is a process that requires documentation, decision making and coordination between several parties. In order to issue securities through the CSD, you are obliged to have an issuing agent – a key player that monitors and handles relevant practical tasks related to a successful issuance process.
We have many years’ experience as an independent issuing agent, and we have extensive knowledge of the share and bond issuance process. We handle all types of Corporate Actions, e.g. dividend payments, mergers and stock splits, and we can create a tailormade set-up specific to your needs. We are known as a partner that finds solutions where others might give up. Our goal is to ensure the most efficient issuance process possible, and in our experience, the easiest way to achieve this is by initiating the dialogue at a very early stage.
Please contact us if you have any questions regarding the issuance process, how to move an issuance to VP Issuing Agent – or to start a dialogue about a future partnership.
