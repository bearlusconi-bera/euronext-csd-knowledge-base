1. Introduction


1.1 Context


As part of its strategic plan, Euronext Securities (Euronext Securities Milan as issuer CSD
and investor CSD, wants to onboard French equities, which means that there needs to be
a solution for French registered securities.

The main objective is to ensure that Euronext Securities Milan can provide a solution to
the market that is developed and adapted by the market within an accelerated timeframe.

Also, to limit the impact on the market, participants should be able to:

    •  use one solution no matter where the place of issuance or the place of safekeeping
    •  use the current format/level of information to feed the providers solution

The platform is designed to manage the Information on Registered Investors (IRI)
process flow efficiently. It gives clients a single point of contact for handling registered
securities, streamlining communication between custodians and registrars. The platform
also facilitates a reconciliation process to match IRIs with settlement instruction messages.
Additionally, it enables the transmission of required information to other CSDs, such as
Euroclear France’s BRN platform.


1.2 Purpose


This document presents in a comprehensive manner the messages exchanged between the
IRI platform and customers in the framework of the Euronext Securities Milan IRI project.
It includes message structures, fields, validation and rejection rules, examples, relevant
flows, and a glossary of technical terms.

1.5 Glossary


The following table lists all terms and abbreviations used in this document.

 Term             Definition

 ARS            Administered Registered Shares

 BIC code      Bank Identification Code.

 CCN           The CCN (“nominative current account”) contains all information
                   related to the holder of the securities (personal details, address, etc.)
                  associated with the account. Each account is identified by a CCN
              number assigned when the account is opened.

              A CCN may have one or several holders, particularly when the
                 account represents a joint ownership structure.

                        If the account has a single holder, then the information relating to the
                 account holder and the information relating to the registered owner
                 are strictly identical.

             When an account includes multiple holders, each holder is identified
                  separately.

 Code           French compagnies identification code.
 SIREN/SIRET

 CSD             Central Securities Depository.

 DSS            Deferred settlement system.

 DvP / RvP /   Types of settlement instructions, indicating whether securities and/or
 DFoP / RFoP   cash are exchanged. The acronyms used in this document refer to the
                   following:

                        •   Delivery Versus Payment (DvP)
                        •  Receive Versus Payment (RvP)
                        •   Delivery Free of Payment (DFoP)

3. IRI

The following sections detail the data models, APIs, and workflows involved.


3.1 IRI structure


The IRI is a set of organized data used to facilitate the transmission of shareholders
information in a formatted way between custodians and registrars for .

An IRI message must be transmitted for each eligible operation, from the custodian to the
registrar via the IRI platform. Once the registrar receives an IRI, the registrar can accept
or reject it and send back the answer to the custodian via the IRI platform.

The IRI includes key data such as:

    •  ISIN
    •  CCN
    •  Shareholder identity (name, address, etc..)
    •   Legal nature of the rights held
    •  Any restrictions on the securities
    •  Data end type of transaction
    •  Quantity of securities involved in the operation
    •  Any other applicable information.

An IRI is a fixed-width text structure that can include one or more lines with a fixed-width
text compound of one or several lines.


3.2 IRI 01


The IRI 01 file has 3 sections each with the following data categories:

    •  The IRI "header" section in which the general characteristics of the IRI are listed,
      such as the Value code, the IRI number, the custodian's delivery code or the number
        of records in the file.
    •  The "registration of a natural or legal person" section in which the characteristics of
       the holder (such as surnames, first names and addresses are listed).
    •   Possibly the "restriction" sections in which the legal characteristics of the holder are
        listed (such as the restriction code or surnames, first names and addresses). The
        restriction dataset can have 0 or N occurrences.

 Securities Account type                    1           AN



Definition

This mandatory field indicates the type of securities account that makes up the IRI.

Function

The value of this field depends on the type of account title. It must be consistent with
all the fields that make up the account. It defines the structure of the IRI as follows:

Account title Type = 0: Physical or legal person without restriction

The IRI is comprised of only one "registration" record. This type of account applies to:

    •   securities held by a physical or legal person, excluding joint accounts

    •   securities under joint tenancy or tenancy in common, securities held by an
       unincorporated investment fund (FCP) or by an investment club, for which the
       holder’s identifier is known and populated on the IRI. In this case, the single
        "registration"  record  indicates the  representative  of the  joint owners, the
       representative of the naked owner, the name of the FCP, or the name of the
      investment club.

Account title Type = 1: Joint securities accounts with or without restriction

The IRI is comprised of at least two "registration" records, which can be followed by
one or more "restriction" records. This type of account applies to securities held by
physical persons  that come under the agreement on  joint  securities accounts
(convention sur comptes-joints de titres).

Account title Type = 2: Physical or legal person with a restriction

The IRI is comprised of at least one "registration" record and at least one "restriction"
record. This type of account applies to securities held by physical or legal persons,
excluding joint accounts.

Account title Type = 3: Joint tenancy

The IRI is comprised of at least two "registration" records, the first being by convention
the representative of the joint tenancy. The "registration" records can be followed by
one or more "restriction" records.

Account title Type = 4: Usufruct and naked ownership

The IRI is comprised of at least two "registration" records and at least two "restriction"
records. The IRI must have the following structure:

    •  the first "registration" record indicates the naked owner or representative of the
      naked ownership; it is followed immediately by a "restriction" record (code NP),
    •  the second "registration" record indicates the usufructuary;  it  is also followed
      immediately by a "restriction" record.

Other "Registration" records can follow:

    •   in the case of joint naked ownership, each of the other naked owners gives rise to
      a "registration" record immediately followed by a "restriction" record (code NP),
    •  when there are other usufructuaries, each of the other usufructuaries gives rise to
      a "registration" record immediately followed by a "restriction" record indicating the
       type of usufruct.

Account title Type = 5: Unincorporated investment fund (Fonds Commun de Placement)

The IRI is comprised of three "registration" records. The IRI must be structured as follows:

    •  the first record indicates the name of the FCP,
