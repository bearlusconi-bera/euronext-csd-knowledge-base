Client- and account administration
Flexible solution for opening, maintain and administrate your clients and their accounts
As an account operator in Euronext Securities Oslo you have access to our client- and account administration tool. A comprehensive system allowing the account operator to open, administrate and maintain your clients account. In addition to full overview of transactions and holdings, you can assist with transfers of holdings and register rights, power of attorneys and tax information ensuring an automatic yearly reporting.
