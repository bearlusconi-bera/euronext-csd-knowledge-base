Issuance Services
What we offer
Euronext Securities offer a wide variety of issuance products and services
Let Euronext Securities become your trusted partner for comprehensive issuance services. Our goal is to provide seamless, efficient, and secure solutions tailored to meet the needs of issuers across various markets. Here’s how we support your issuance needs:
Full-Service Issuance Support
We offer end-to-end support for all your issuance activities. From the initial planning stages to the final execution, our experienced team is here to ensure a smooth process. Our services include:
Preparation and Documentation: Guidance on the preparation of necessary documentation and compliance with regulatory requirements.
Coordination and Liaison: Acting as a liaison with regulatory bodies, financial advisors, and other stakeholders to ensure a coordinated approach.
Efficient Registration and Listing
Our platform streamlines the registration and listing process, making it quicker and more efficient. We provide:
Secure Registration: Secure and accurate registration of new issuances.
Listing Services: Efficient handling of listing applications and documentation for various securities, including equities, bonds, and derivatives.
Compliance and Regulatory Adherence
Navigating the complex regulatory landscape is crucial for successful issuance. Euronext Securities ensures compliance through:
Regulatory Expertise: In-depth knowledge of local and international regulations to ensure your issuance meets all legal requirements.
Audit and Reporting: Comprehensive audit trails and reporting services to maintain transparency and accountability.
Investor Relations and Market Access
Connecting with the right investors is key to the success of any issuance. Our services include:
Market Access: Providing access to a broad network of domestic and international investors. Investment fund order routing capabilities for Subscription and redemption processes.
Communication Tools: Tools and platforms to effectively communicate with current and potential investors.
Post-Issuance Support
Our commitment to your success continues even after the issuance. We offer:
Ongoing Compliance: Assistance with ongoing regulatory compliance and reporting requirements.
Corporate Actions Management: Efficient handling of corporate actions such as dividends, stock splits, and more.
