Membership: Admission and Client Onboarding
Following the preliminary check, potential clients are required to prepare and submit the required documentation and await application approval from the Membership office. Once clients are informed that they qualify for admission, the onboarding process (phase two) can begin.
For further information on the onboarding procedures and the steps to finalizing the contract, please consult our Welcome KIT.Clients interested in becoming Issuers on Euronext Securities Milan can also refer to the Issuers Onboarding FAQs which outlines the necessary actions to fulfil for admission.
