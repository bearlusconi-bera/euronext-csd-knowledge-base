                     POWER OF ATTORNEY



                                                                     Attention: Monte Titoli S.p.A.


This Power of Attorney will become effective from the date of its signature.



(the “Company”) whose registered office is at





In  its role of Client of Monte Titoli and Accountable Party or entity acting on behalf of an
accountable party for the purpose of the French Financial Transaction Tax (herinafter “FTT”),
pursuant to article 5 of “LOI no 2012-354 du 14 mars 2012 de finances rectificative pour 2012”,
(hereinafter the “Law”).
hereby appoints

                           Monte Titoli S.p.A

(the “Attorney”), whose registered office is at Piazza degli Affari 6, 20123, Milano

in its role of Euroclear France Member, as its Attorney for the transmission on its behalf to
Euroclear France of the tax declarations as transmitted by the Company to Monte Titoli,
according to the Law.

The Company undertakes to transmitt to the “Attorney” the tax declarations according to the
terms and modalities set forth in the MT-X Service Manual.

The Company aknowledges that the “Attorney” shall not be responsible to any third party for the
accuracy and throuthfulness of information included in the tax declaration.

For the purpose of the payment of the FTT, the Company authorizes the “Attorney” to debit the
respective amounts including penalties for late declarations, that will be made available by the
Company on the RTGS Target2 account already used to have proceeds paid onCross Border
Corporate Actions.

The Company hereby agrees that it shall be fully liable to Euroclar France and the French Tax
Authorities for any and all obligations created on its behalf pursuant to the authority or purported
authority of this proxy and undertakes to ratify whatever the Attorney causes to be done under the
authority or purported authority of this proxy.
This power of attorney shall remain valid until notice of termination is received by Monte Titoli by
registered letter.

This power of attorney is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of Italy.
