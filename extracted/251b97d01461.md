User Committee

The User Committee is a forum for topics of significant relevance for Euronext Securities Oslo’
 (ES - OSL) clients and the committee acts independently of the management of ES                - OSL.

The User Committee is chaired by a chair appointed by ES               - OSL’s Board of Directors among
the clients and the User Committee meets on a regular basis. Further constitution of the User
Committee is the responsibility of the chair and the management of ES                - OSL  as described in
the mandate of the User Committee which you find on our homepage.

The responsibility of the User Committee is to advice the Board of Directors on client opinions
and expectations and allows the clients to express their views on key issues, e.g. pricing
 structure and needs for specific services, to the Board of Directors.          The User Committee has
 direct reporting rights to the Board of Directors.
