When you have an election to complete, you can use a tailored solution that meets your specific needs. Our secure online voting platform has a user-friendly interface, which makes it easy for your election participants to vote, whether this is for employee representatives on the board of directors, members of the board of representatives, or investor representatives for an investor forum, etc.
With our full-service virtual general meeting solution, including webcasting capabilities, you are able to hold your election fully or partially virtual depending on your needs. Your election participants will be able to cast votes, communicate with the meeting director and view the election unfold with high quality audio – just as though they had been there in person.
We also offer on-location meeting services, including electronic voting. As a provider of election services for many years, we can assure you the necessary expertise and relieve your internal resources.
No matter which set-up you choose, we can make sure that you have the most efficient election process possible.
