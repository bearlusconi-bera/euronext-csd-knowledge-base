  Welcome to the Onboarding Handbook. This guide has been carefully designed to help you navigate
  through the Euronext Securities process efficiently. This Handbook provides a step-by-step reference
   to ensure that every aspect  is managed seamlessly. Should you require further clarification or
   assistance   at  any   stage,   please   contact  by   email   the   Onboarding   Department:
  CSD.Onboarding@euronext.com.

  Euronext Securities Milan (hereinafter also indicated as ES-MIL) assists clients in every phase of the
   journey. Participation is open to all clients, including current ES-MIL participants, provided they sign
   the necessary contracts, fulfil the predefined requirements, and receive approval during the initial
  admission phase. These contracts are standardized and cannot be altered or revised.

 1. Membership requirements

 1.1 Procedure for admission

  1.1.1 Preliminary phase

  Any applicant seeking admission as a client must contact the ES-MIL Onboarding Department and
  submit  all required information and documentation requested to confirm compliance with the
  admission conditions and criteria.

  The following documents must be sent by email to CSD.Onboarding@euronext.com to start the
  membership procedure:

     Legal Entity Identifier (LEI)
     Legal Company Name
      Certificate of Incorporation or Chamber of Commerce Registration
     Business Case Questionnaire (mandatory for Issuers, optional for intermediaries, mandatory for
      intermediaries who wishes to open also an issuer account in ES-MIL)

   Aligning with the time frames indicated by the European Central Securities Depository Regulation
  (CSDR), the Onboarding Team will send the “Confirmation of Admissibility” to its centralisation system.
  The procedure can be expedited in cases of particular and justified urgency.

   All the contracts referenced in this document will be available as annexes.

  1.1.3 Connectivity Phase

  To ensure a smooth experience with our internal tools, we offer training sessions to help familiarize
  members with the graphical user interface. Should you require it, please feel free to schedule a test
   session or training, and the Onboarding Team will assist you. SWIFT message formats are available
  on My Standards, with access provided upon request.

  Step 1: SWIFT (RMA exchange and CUG subscription)

  To connect to ES-MIL via SWIFT, the Client must join the CUG (Closed User Group) by registering on
  SWIFT. The reference CUG is named EXP. Registration must be completed for both the production
  environment and the test environment, i.e. in EXP (Live) and in EXP (Test). Once CUG registration is
  complete and the key exchange with the ES-MIL BIC (MOTIIT22, MOTIIT22RRG, MOTIIT22CUS for
   production, MOTIIT20, MOTIIT20RRG, MOTIIT20CUS for testing) is done, messages can be selected
   in the relevant section of CLIMP, section of MOTI4U Portal.

  The RMA (Relationship Management Application) exchanges must be performed on the SWIFT platform
  between the respective BICs, to confirm their permission and connectivity.

   Please note that ES-MIL is using 15022 and 20022 SWIFT messages for settlement whereas SWIFT
  20022 for asset services will be available from February 2026.

  Step 2: MOTI4U System Admin ID

  New clients s must select at least one admin ID for MOTI4U (max 5) who will be responsible to provide
   roles and privileges to other licenses. ES-MIL portal is split in 3 domains:

     Custody: MT-X is the interactive dashboard for managing and monitoring in real-time the
      custody service and domestic and international asset servicing.
     Settlement: X-TRM is the dashboard for the relevant settlement reporting and the pre-
      settlement platform.
     Membership: CLIMP is the dashboard for the overall management of membership.

 1.2 Accounts

  1.2.1 Type of account

  ES-MIL offers three types of accounts for managing securities and financial assets. In compliance with
   the CSDR, when opening an account, clients must choose between a Property Account or a Third-
  Party Account. This requirement aims to enhance the safety and efficiency of securities settlement.

  Moreover, clients can request ES-MIL to open as many segregated accounts as per their business
  needs following the CSDR level of segregation.

   1. Third Party Account (OM): intended to record financial instruments that Participants hold on
      behalf of their clients.
   2. Property Account (OW): Intended to record the financial instruments owned by Participants.
     The proprietary accounts are opened in the name and upon request of the Participant.

   3. Segregated Account (IS): An individual account separates the securities of each client into
       distinct accounts.

  1.2.2 Account Management

  Opening an Account

     When opening an account, clients must provide specific information such as the parent BIC,
      party BIC, description of operations, and the type of segregation. Once the information is
      submitted via CLIMP, ES-MIL evaluates and validates the request, assigning an account identifier
          if necessary.

  Modifying an Account

     Clients can modify the attributes of their accounts, such as the type of segregation or the start
     and end dates for the use of auto-collateralization. These modifications entered via CLIMP also
      require validation by ES-MIL to ensure compliance and security.

  Closing an Account

    To close an account, clients must indicate a proposed closure date. ES-MIL evaluates this request
     and may adjust the date without invalidating the request. The closure of an account is subject to
      checks to ensure there are no ongoing transactions.

  Transferring an Account

    Transferring an account from one party to another involves selecting the source and destination
       parties, as well as the accounts to be transferred. The proposed transfer date is evaluated by ES-
      MIL, which may adjust it if necessary.

 2. Definition of ES-MIL services

 2.1 Services for issuers

   For more details about our services for issuers, please refer to this documentation:
   Regulations ES-MIL.

  2.1.1 Notary service

  The notary service registers financial instruments (paper or digital) in the accounting system. Issuers
   or their representatives can request this registration. ES-MIL maintains the integrity of these records
  through reconciliation measures.

  2.1.2 Corporate events support services

  Corporate Event Support Services for issuers help to manage important events like dividends,
  mergers, and shareholder meetings. These services ensure all necessary information is communicated
   to shareholders and regulatory requirements are met efficiently.

  2.1.3 FIS/CPA Services

  These services help issuers to manage and receive required notices and shareholder information. They
  ensure data quality, store and organize information, maintain confidentiality, and  facilitate fee
  payments to intermediaries.

  2.1.4 Service for the identification of financial instrument holders

  ES-MIL helps identify shareholders and holders of financial instruments. Communications are done
   electronically, and participation is open to issuers and asset management companies. Non-participants
   are regulated by specific contracts. Clients receive regular updates and reports on the status of their
   shareholder register.

  2.1.5 RCC for Issuers

   This service calculates, reports, and settles fees due to intermediaries for providing shareholder data.
   It ensures accurate invoicing and timely payments.

 2.2 Services for intermediaries

   For more details about our services for intermediaries, please refer to this documentation:
   Regulations ES-MIL.

  2.2.1 Central maintenance services

  The service provided by ES-MIL involves maintaining securities accounts for intermediaries. This
   service ensures the accurate recording and management of financial instruments within ES-MIL’s
   accounting records system.

  2.2.2 Corporate events support services

  The Service Supporting the Processing of Corporate Events provided by ES-MIL  facilitates the
  management of corporate actions for financial instruments registered in their system. This service is
   essential for issuers and intermediaries to ensure accurate and efficient handling of various corporate
   events.

  2.2.3 Service for the identification of financial instrument holders

  ES-MIL helps identify shareholders and holders of financial instruments. Communications are done
   electronically, and participation is open to issuers and asset management companies. Non-participants
   are regulated by specific contracts. Clients receive regular updates and reports on the status of their
   shareholder register.

  2.2.4 RCC for Intermediaries

   This service calculates fees owed by issuers, produces detailed reports, settles amounts on accounts,
  and prepares electronic invoices. It organizes and verifies data to facilitate payments.

  2.2.5 FIS/CPA Services

  These services assist intermediaries in sending required notices and shareholder information to
   issuers. They manage electronic transmission, ensure data quality, store and organize information,
  and maintain confidentiality and authentication.

  2.2.6 X-TRM Services

  X-TRM is a web application provided by ES-MIL, granting access to the T2S settlement system, penalty
  management, and buy-in management. This service is mandatory for ICPs (Indirectly Connected
   Participants) and  essential  for DCPs  (Directly Connected  Participants)  if they request  foreign
   settlement services to participate in external settlement.

  2.2.7 Core Settlement Services

  The Settlement Service includes the acquisition, matching, and handling of settlement instructions.
  These phases ensure the proper processing and finality of transactions.

   For detailed information on cut-off times and operating hours, please refer to the this document:
  Settlement Service Instructions.

  2.2.8 Foreign Settlement Services

  The Foreign Settlement Service facilitates the forwarding and settlement of transfer orders on
   settlement systems operated by others CSDs that do not use the T2S platform.

   Transactions are settled on accounts opened by ES-MIL in its own name and on its own behalf on
   these external settlement systems. Participants in the Settlement Service can access the Foreign
  Settlement Service.

 2.3 Auxiliary services

  2.3.1 Tax services

  Tax relief services for non-resident intermediaries on securities subject to Italian tax laws:

  Tax Withholding and Reporting

     Withholding Tax: ES-MIL calculates and withholds taxes on dividends, interest, and other income
     from financial instruments according to Italian tax laws.
     Reporting: Detailed tax reports are generated and provided to intermediaries, ensuring
      transparency and compliance with tax obligations.

  Tax Reporting

     Obligations: Intermediaries are required to report income distributions and tax withholdings to
      the Italian tax authorities.
     ES-MIL’s Role: ES-MIL facilitates this reporting by providing intermediaries with the necessary
      data and support to meet their tax obligations.

  Electronic Communication

      Efficiency: All communications related to tax services are conducted electronically, ensuring
       efficient and timely processing.
     Security: ES-MIL ensures the security and confidentiality of all tax-related data transmitted
       electronically.

  2.3.2 AGM (Annual General Meeting)

   ES-MIL's dedicated AGM and Register team provides specialized services, distinct from the standard
  AGM service available to all issuers, offering tailored solutions and exceptional support to meet clients’
  unique needs. The Annual General Meeting (AGM) and the Extraordinary General Meeting (EGM) are
   important corporate action events. The main purpose of these services is also to comply with legal
   requirements.

  Annual and Extraordinary General Meeting Management (AGM/EGM)

     Overseeing the various aspects of the General Meeting: Logistics, technical setup, management
       of documentation and operational phases.




                                                                       15ES-MIL Onboarding Handbook

## PDF page 16

  Management of the Shareholders’ Register

     Maintaining the relationship with authorised intermediaries, managing operational instructions in
       relation to corporate actions events, updates of the shareholding composition and reporting to
      issuers and authorities.

  Management of the Increased Voting List

     Managing shareholders’ requests to be included in the voting list, monitoring of communication
      channel for receiving requests, updating the lists and reporting to shareholders and issuers.

  Third-Party Account Services

     Managing third-party intermediary accounts opened by issuers: settlement instructions, register
      with details of the holders, communication of the events and reporting to issuers and holders.

  ES-MIL provides the preparation and planning of a professional AGM/EGM, and a range of cost-efficient
   services designed to fulfil the meeting requirements and enhance the shareholder’s engagement with
   the issuer.

  General Meeting voting flow

   1. Meeting setup on ES-MIL GUI by the issuer or their agent.
   2. Meeting notice disseminated by ES-MIL to their participants.
   3. Custodian banks are due to provide the issuer with their shareholders certified positions (FIS).
   4. Voting instructions are provided by the shareholders directly to the issuer or their agent
      (according to the voting instructions indicated on the meeting notice).
