Join Euronext Securities Athens
The Euronext Securities Athens provides services including initial recording, central maintenance of accounts, and settlement of securities in book-entry form as well as other ancillary services (e.g. shareholders' registers, support of the processing of corporate actions etc.) through the Dematerialized Securities System (DSS).
Joining Euronext Securities Athens, a Central Securities Depository (CSD) licensed under CSDR (Central Securities Depositories Regulation), offers significant advantages to Participants, by streamlining the settlement process, reducing risk, and ensuring the secure custody of securities in either segregated or omnibus Securities Accounts. Euronext's Securities Athens advanced technology infrastructure enhances operational efficiency by providing Participants with access to a wide range of financial products, while implementing strict security protocols to mitigate risk and ensure the integrity of the securities held, contributing to a more secure and efficient financial environment.
