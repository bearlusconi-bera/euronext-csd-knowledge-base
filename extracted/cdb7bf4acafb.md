Niels Siggaard-Rotendahl
Chief Executive Officer
Area of responsibility
VP Securities, owned by Euronext, has from the 20 September announced Niels Siggaard-Rotendahl as Chief Executive Officer.
Previous employment
As of 10 March 2021 he was appointed interim CEO after 6 years as Chief Financial Officer at VP Securities with responsibility for financial, economic affairs, data and facility management. Moreover he has experience from auditing, the retail sector, shipping and Corporate Banking.
2007 - 2015 General Manager of Finance in Dampskibsselskabet NORDEN A/S.
Education and training
Executive MBA from Copenhagen Business School, 2014. And Graduate Diploma in Business Administration (financial and management accounting, HD-R) also from Copenhagen Business School, 2004.
