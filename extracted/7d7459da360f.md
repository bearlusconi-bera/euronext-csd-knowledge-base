                                  Declaration of Beneficial Ownership





We    7 _____________________________________ (name of beneficial owner), resident of
  21  ____________________ (country of residence of the beneficial owner) within the meaning of the Double
Taxation Agreement between the country of residence of the beneficial owner and the Slovak Republic (hereinafter the
‘DTA’) hereby confirm that:

1. We are the beneficial owner of all income accruing from our property in custody of Citibank, a.s. held in a
    securities account in the name of Euroclear Nominees Limited with Citibank or with other relevant securities
    registrars or depositories (hereinafter the ‘Property’) and such income is considered to be the income of the
    beneficial owner pursuant to the tax law of the above-stated country of residence.

2. The income accruing from the Property is not attributable to any permanent establishment of the beneficial owner
    in the Slovak Republic within the meaning of the relevant Article of the DTA.




We hereby undertake to inform Citibank, a.s. in writing without delay if:

1. The income accruing from the Property becomes attributable to a permanent establishment of the beneficial owner
    in the Slovak Republic within the meaning of the relevant Article of the DTA;

2. We are no longer the beneficial owner of the income accruing from the Property;

3. We are no longer a tax resident of the above-stated country; or

4. The income from the Property is no longer considered to be the income of the beneficial owner pursuant to the tax
   law of the above-stated country of residence.
