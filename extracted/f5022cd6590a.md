What we offer
Euronext Securities offer a wide variety of register and meeting services
Our suite of services for the issuers are designed to cater to the evolving needs, ensuring seamless management of investor relations and regulatory compliance.
Register Services
At Euronext Securities, we understand the importance of maintaining an accurate and up-to-date shareholder register. Our online platform provides a secure and efficient solution, automatically updated with data from the Central Securities Depository (CSD). This eliminates the hassle of manual record-keeping, offering you a safe and uncomplicated way to manage shareholder data.
Our platform is accessible 24/7 and adheres to the highest security standards, providing you with peace of mind. Tailored specifically to meet the needs of corporate legal departments, investor relations professionals, and general management, our user-friendly interface ensures easy access to relevant shareholder information.
Meeting Services
Organising an annual general meeting (AGM) can be a complex task, requiring detailed planning and specialised expertise. Our experienced meeting services team is here to ensure that your AGM runs smoothly from start to finish.
With our comprehensive AGM management solutions, including coordination, technical set-up, and document management, you can rest assured that every aspect of your meeting is taken care of. Whether you choose a fully virtual, hybrid, or physical meeting, we have the expertise to meet your needs.
Our online voting platform makes it easy for participants to cast their votes securely, ensuring a transparent and efficient election process. Plus, with our webcasting capabilities, virtual attendees can experience the meeting firsthand, just as if they were there in person.
