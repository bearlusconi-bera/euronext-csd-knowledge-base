Notes to Forms 5000 and 5001





        Form                                    Notes

 5000–EN

 Section I                    It is necessary to fill in the field “occupation” with the relevant
                             legal status of the final beneficiary. The generic legal status
                               ‘legal entity’ will be no longer accepted as from 20/03/2013

 Section IV                      It must be left blank if the beneficial owner is a “US Resident” and
                         must be replaced by Form 6166

 Section V                        It must be left blank

 Section VIII                    It must be left blank

 5001–EN

 Section I                        It must be left blank if the beneficial owner does not require the
                               application of the tax regime of holding companies.

 Section II                       All columns must be filled in with the exception of 4 and 5. If the
                                 beneficial owner requires the application of the tax regime of holding
                           companies, columns 4 and 5 must be filled in

 Section III                      It must be left blank
