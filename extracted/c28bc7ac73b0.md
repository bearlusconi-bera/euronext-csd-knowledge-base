Each of the Company and the Attorney hereby agree that CBL shall not be held liable for any
action or omission whatsoever, whether taken or omitted to be taken, erroneously or not, by
the Company or the Attorney.

The Company and the Attorney hereby agree to hold harmless and not make any claim
against CBL for any loss, claim, liability, damage, cost or any expense whatsoever due to the
disclosure to the Attorney of all or any part of information related to the Account.

This power of attorney shall remain valid until notice of termination or amendment is
received by CBL by registered letter. Any such termination or amendment shall take effect
on the second business day in Luxembourg after receipt of the notice by CBL or such other
later date specified in the notice accordingly.



This power of attorney is governed by and shall be construed in accordance with the laws of
the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg.
