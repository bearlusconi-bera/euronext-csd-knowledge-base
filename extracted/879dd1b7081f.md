1. EC update

   The EC representative reported that the legislative process was advancing well, as both
    the European Parliament and the Council have agreed their positions (both agree on the
   date for the move to T+1; they also have similar views on the exemption of SFTs and on
    the cash penalties suspension). Intention is to start and finish trilogue negotiations in June.



2. ECB update on T2S preparations for T+1 [slides available]

   An ECB representative provided an update on the preliminary analysis of the T2S
   governance on the potential impacts of T+1 on T2S, noting that:

       After the publication of the Industry Committee (IC) draft recommendations by end
       June, the T2S governance plans to start the T2S Change and Release Management
       process, as per the T2S governance arrangements. Tentatively, and subject to a
       decision of the ECB Market Infrastructure Board, any potential changes in T2S would
      need to be deployed by June 2027. As is usually the case in such market-wide relevant
     T2S releases, testing with T2S stakeholders will be planned before and after the June
      2027 software release.
       Quality, clarity and substantiation of recommendations will bring efficiency and mitigate
       implementation risks when T2S governance starts the T2S Change and Release
      Management process [Q3 2025] - each T2S change request is subject to a detailed
         feasibility assessment  including  cost/benefit  analysis, as  per  the  existing T2S
      governance and framework.

      The ultimate objective of T2S is to be ready for 11 October 2027 across all T2S
       markets, maintaining at least the same level of settlement efficiency while striking a
       balance vis-a-vis what can realistically be achieved by that date and what can follow a
      more long-term timeline, going beyond the T+1 entry into force date.

   The EC representative reminded of the need for everyone to be ready on time. The IC
   Chair added that a key part of the work to be conducted by the IC in 2026/2027 would be
    to communicate, educate, and survey market preparedness, but involvement of authorities
     will also be key. The Coordination Committee Chair highlighted the collective responsibility
    of both the authorities and the industry to make sure everyone is aware and ready, and the
   need to continue to communicate to support this objective.



3.  Industry Committee Chair update [slides available]

   The IC Chair shared a few  highlights on the main recommendations the Industry
   Committee is finalising. These high-level recommendations:
      o  Address all critical issues identified by technical workstreams (“nice to have” to be
          addressed at a later stage)
      o   Will be non-binding (“adhere or explain” approach)
      o  Are based on current arrangements and timetable, but can be updated if necessary

   For those requiring additional work: some issues should be solved in the coming days to
   be incorporated in the roadmap, others will be further discussed to be included in a second
   document (a best practices rulebook) to be published later this year.
   The Coordination Committee Chair, as well as the representatives of ESMA, the European
   Commission, and the ECB congratulated the IC Chair for the work already accomplished
   by the IC.

   Next steps:

                                     of August.
             Post-30 June     Public statement and press release
           by mid-July       Public Event to present the road map

4. ESMA update

  ESMA staff provided an overview of the preliminary outcome from the public consultation
   on possible amendments to the RTS on settlement discipline, which closed mid-April.

   Analysis is  still on-going but at this stage, on the issues that are relevant to the T+1
    transition (e.g. allocations and confirmations, settlement instructions matching  fields,
