1. Executive summary


This document describes the Danish Tax Reporting Service provided by Euronext Securities
Copenhagen in support of Danish tax reporting obligations.

The service supports the preparation, validation, generation, submission and correction of
selected Danish monthly and yearly tax reports to the Danish Tax Agency / Skattestyrelsen
(DTA). It also supports related operational activities, including data validation, report status
management, feedback handling, and correction workflows.

The service is intended to provide a structured and controlled reporting process for Danish tax
reporting. It supports traceability from collected source data through report generation,
submission, feedback and, where required, correction and resubmission.

The service does not constitute legal advice, tax advice, regulatory advice or financial advice.
Clients and relevant service participants remain responsible for their own obligations, data
quality responsibilities and internal assessments where applicable.

The legal responsibility for compliance with Danish tax reporting requirements, including the
accuracy, completeness, and timely submission of reports, remains with the reporting party or,
where applicable under the Danish Tax Reporting Act, with Euronext Securities Copenhagen in
cases where an independent statutory reporting obligation applies. Euronext Securities
Copenhagen otherwise acts as an operational service provider facilitating the preparation and
transmission of reports on behalf of the reporting party and does not assume any additional
statutory reporting obligation or liability.

2. Introduction


Danish tax reporting requires timely and accurate reporting of selected securities, account,
investor, income, holding and transaction information to the Danish Tax Agency.

Euronext Securities Copenhagen supports these reporting obligations through a Danish Tax
Reporting Service covering both monthly and yearly reporting processes. The service includes
capabilities for collecting and validating relevant reporting data, generating tax reports in
applicable DTA formats, submitting validated reports, receiving DTA feedback and supporting
correction handling where required.

3. Service overview


The Danish Tax Reporting Service supports the end-to-end lifecycle for selected Danish
monthly and yearly tax reporting.

                                              STANDARDS AND MARKET PRACTICES


4. Standards and market practices


This service is provided in accordance with Euronext Securities Copenhagen’s authorisation
under the Central Securities Depositories Regulation (CSDR). Danish tax reporting services
constitute a non-banking-type ancillary service as defined in Section B of the CSDR Annex and
are delivered within Euronext Securities Copenhagen’s authorised scope.

The applicability of the service depends on the client custody model. For clients operating
under a segregated account model, the Danish tax reporting service is provided as part of the
standard service offering. For clients operating under an omnibus account model, the service
may, subject to agreement, either be performed by Euronext Securities Copenhagen on behalf
of the client or insourced by the client.

Where Euronext Securities Copenhagen performs the service, it acts on behalf of the relevant
reporting party within the agreed service scope. Where clients elect to insource the reporting,
they retain full operational responsibility for the reporting process.

The service is based on relevant guidelines from the Danish tax authority and Euronext
Securities Copenhagen operational procedures.

Unless otherwise agreed or specified, the service follows these principles:

    •   reporting is performed on behalf of the reporting party using applicable DTA formats;
    •   report generation is based on validated source-data snapshots;
    •   report files are subject to data and format validation before submission;
    •  DTA technical receipts are distinguished from DTA business feedback;
    •  feedback and rejected records are made available for operational follow-up and
       correction handling;
    •   corrected data and corrected reports are handled through controlled and auditable
       processes;
    •  access to data and functionality is based on role and entitlement.

The service is designed to support Danish tax reporting processes and does not replace or
assume clients’ legal, tax, compliance or operational responsibilities, including the statutory
reporting obligation where applicable.

                                                                    SERVICE LIFECYCLE



7. Service lifecycle


7.1 Data collection


The service collects relevant monthly and yearly data required for Danish tax reporting. This
may include account, investor, securities, holding, transaction, dividend, distribution,
corporate-action, price, exchange-rate, assessment-rate, market-value and beneficial-owner
data where applicable.


7.2 Data validation and snapshot creation


Collected data is validated for completeness and consistency. Once data is eligible for
reporting, the service creates a source-data snapshot. The snapshot provides the controlled
data basis for report generation and later traceability.


7.3 Price and valuation data management


The service manages price, assessment-rate, exchange-rate and market-value data required
for tax reporting, including distributing EOY prices to market participants.


7.4 Monthly report generation


Monthly reports are generated from validated monthly source-data snapshots. The service
applies relevant reporting rules, performs data and format validation, stores generated reports
and updates report status.


7.5 Yearly report generation


Yearly reports are generated from validated yearly source data snapshots. The service applies
relevant annual reporting rules, performs data and format validation, stores generated reports
and updates report status.

7.6 Submission to the Danish Tax Agency


Reports that have passed required validations are prepared for submission to DTA. Submission
is performed through the agreed technical channel (STFP/API), with submission status and
technical receipts recorded for traceability.


7.7 Receipt and feedback handling


The service receives and stores technical receipts and DTA feedback/error-list files and shares
the feedback with SACs through the agreed technical channel (STFP/API). Feedback is linked
to submitted reports where possible and made available for operational review and correction
handling.


7.8 Correction handling


Where missing, incorrect or amended data is identified, U2A correction workflows may be
initiated with the relevant party. Corrections may involve Issuer Agents, SACs or Business
Operations, depending on the data domain.


7.9 Corrected reports and resubmission preparation


Where corrected data affects generated or submitted reports, the service supports impact
assessment and corrected report generation. Corrected reports are validated and, where
eligible, made available for resubmission.
