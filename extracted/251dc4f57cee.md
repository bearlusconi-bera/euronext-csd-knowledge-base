ATHEXCSD (Euronext Securities Athens)
ATHEXCSD (Euronext Securities Athens) is a central securities depository (CSD) licensed under prior authorisation from the Central Securities Depositories Regulation (CSDR). It offers significant advantages to participants by streamlining the settlement process, reducing risk, and ensuring the secure custody of securities in either segregated or omnibus securities accounts.ATHEXCSD’s advanced technology infrastructure enhances operational efficiency by providing participants with access to a wide range of financial products. It implements strict security protocols to mitigate risk and ensure the integrity of securities held, contributing to pipeline security and efficiency in the financial environment.
