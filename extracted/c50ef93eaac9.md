Shareholder Register Services
We offer a safe and simple shareholder register service.
Every limited liability company is obliged to hold a shareholder register – a list of all shareholders registered by name. If this is done manually, it can be very time-consuming.
Euronext Securities Copenhagen - Investor Services offers you an efficient online shareholder register solution that is updated automatically on a daily basis with transactions from the Central Securities Depository (CSD).
Our secure online platform is accessible 24/7 and is protected under the same standards as the CSD. This gives your company access to relevant shareholder data via a user-friendly interface. The interface is specifically designed to meet the needs of corporate legal departments, IR professionals and general management.
The platform is also optimised to support investment funds’ investor registers, as well as shareholder registers for non-listed companies.
