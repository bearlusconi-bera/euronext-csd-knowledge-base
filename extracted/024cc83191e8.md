Euronext ISO15022 messaging general information and examples


When communicating with Euronext via ISO15022 messages the following rules apply. Examples as
seen by Participant and using artificial BICs. Please replace with corresponding BICs reported to
Euronext in the form above.

For messages from Participant to Euronext

Messages sent to Euronext need to be addressed to the relevant Euronext Securities CSD via use of
correct BIC and transport.
