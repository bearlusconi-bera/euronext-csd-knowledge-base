Supervision
VP is subject to supervision in accordance with both the European Regulation on central securities depositories and the Danish Act on Capital Markets.
VP is licensed by the Danish Financial Supervisory Authority and is subject to supervision in accordance with both the European Regulation on central securities depositories and the Danish Act on Capital Markets.
The Danish Financial Supervisory Authority regularly conducts inspections of VP.
Below you can find statements and supervisory reactions towards VP from the Danish Financial Supervisory Authority and any comments we may have published in that regard.
As a central securities depository we are considered systemically important for a well-functioning financial system. As such, we are subject to oversight by the Danish Central Bank and the Danish Financial Supervisory Authority.
You can read the overall assessment and conclusion on VP from the overseers on the Danish Central Banks website.
