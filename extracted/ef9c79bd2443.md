Safekeeping
We keep your assets safe
Financial instruments registered in Euronext VPS need to be safekept on accounts registered in the central securities register. Euronext Securities Oslo offer legal protection of your assets through a wide range of account types registered in our book-entry system. Accounts are opened through an account operator either as end investor- or as nominee account dependent on the specific need.
